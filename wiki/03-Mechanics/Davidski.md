# Davidski

Davidski is a trader who flies. He is a peaceful NPC ship that comes out once a day, at a time nobody announces, and patrols one [Danger Sector](/wiki/01-General/Danger-Sectors.md), he has the best gear the game has, and he sells every item the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md) lists, with no end to his stock, at many times the Shop's price. He belongs to no company, stands in the Hall of Fame like a pilot and does not shoot first. You buy from him in the Auction, and you can fight him in space.

![The Hall of Fame: Davidski has a row in every world, with no company, at the points of a level 20 pilot](../img/wiki-img/shots/davidski-hall.jpg)

## Where He Is

- **Once a day, unannounced**: in every [world](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#worlds-alpha-beta-and-gamma) he comes out once a UTC day, at a moment somewhere between 00:00 and 20:00 UTC that is different in every world and every day. Nobody is told: not in the chat, not in the Game Log, not by a notice. He flies until he is destroyed or the day ends.
- **One Danger Sector a day**: he flies on `DS-1`, `DS-2` and `DS-3` in turn, one a day, and in every world his own ship is on the same sector.
- **How you find him**: by looking. Only the pilots on the sector he flies can see him: his name is gold with a storefront in front of it, and the minimap draws him as a gold dot in a ring. The galaxy map does not show him. The details of one of his rows in the Auction say only whether he is out today: **Flying today**, **Not out today** or **Killed today**. They never say where or when.
- **At the day's end** he leaves when nobody has hit him for a minute. A fight is never cut by midnight: after 15 minutes he goes whatever is going on. The next day's Davidski comes out at the next day's moment, on the next sector.
- **After a wipe** his ship is made anew, but his day is remembered: if he was out and not destroyed he is back at once, and if he was destroyed he stays gone until the next day.

## His Ship

An **Ironclad TITANIC** painted gold, at the TITANIC's own size, flying the best of every kind of part, forged to Eternal:

| What | Item |
|---|---|
| Lasers | 8 Helios Beams, each with a Damage Amp IV, a Crit Amp IV and a Penetration Amp IV |
| Shields and engines | 11 Heavy Shield Cores and 4 Adaptive Cores, each with Capacity and Absorption Shield Cells IV or Impulse and Momentum Thrusters IV |
| Extras | a Repair Drone IV, an Auto-Repair CPU and a Base CPU II |
| Hull | 15 Hull Plating III |

At Alpha strength he has **858,750 hull**, **1,309,534 shield** (recharging 67,614 a second after 15 seconds without a hit), 80.5% absorbance, speed 96, 945 range and a volley of **2,052 a second**. Beta has 1.5 times those numbers and Gamma 2 times, as every alien does. That is about 2.2 million points to chew through in Alpha: one Wraith with the same lasers needs about twelve minutes of steady fire on its own, ten of them about a minute and ten seconds.

## Fighting Him

- **He is peaceful.** He flies his route and attacks nobody. A pilot in a safe ring, or one who is cloaked, is not a target for him.
- **He shoots back.** Whoever hurts him becomes his target: he flies at the first of his attackers he can reach, fires his volley, and lets go ten seconds after anybody last hit him. Left alone for a minute and a half he is whole again (his shield recharges after 15 seconds, his hull heals 2% a second from 30 seconds after the last hit).
- **Fire on him on purpose.** A double click does not open fire on him, as on a company pilot's ship: select him, then use the attack key or an ammo slot.
- **Company pilots stay out of it**: like every swarm ship he is none of their business.

### Destroyed

He pays like a boss, by the damage each pilot dealt (a share under 5% of the damage earns nothing, as at any swarm boss) and lays one [cargo](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) box for the pilot who dealt most:

| | Alpha | Beta | Gamma |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Credits | 1,060,000 | 2,120,000 | 3,180,000 |
| Thulium | 2,850 | 5,700 | 8,550 |
| Experience | 170,000 | 340,000 | 510,000 |
| Honor | 740 | 1,480 | 2,220 |

The box holds 40 to 60 Ship Fragments, 10 to 20 Power Cores and 3,000 to 6,000 Advanced Plasma, and sometimes Ancient Control Units or Dark Matter. A kill counts **37 points** for your PvE ranking, under the name Davidski in your kill statistics.

- **He is destroyed once a day at the most.** After his death he stays gone until the next UTC day, when he comes out again at the next day's moment, at full hull. Nobody is told that he fell; the pilots who fought him hear their own pay.
- **He pays a pilot for two kills a day and a world for eight** (UTC). With one Davidski a day in a world these limits rarely matter; past them you are told so, and you are paid nothing and get no points for the kill.

## Buying From Him

His stock is in the Auction's **Market** list, among the pilots' listings: a row for each of the 83 kinds the Auction lists, in Credits and in Thulium (166 rows), priced for one lot, with his name in gold, an **NPC** badge and an infinity sign for the quantity and the time left. The Market's search, categories and enchant and currency filters narrow his rows like any listing (his items are Standard), and its orders put them where their number says: by price beside the pilots' prices, after the pilots' listings under **Ending soon** and **Newest**. Pick one to see the details and **Buy**; the purchase goes to him, not to a pilot. The prices:

- **100 times the Shop's price in Credits and 40 times in Thulium**, for the items the Shop sells; a Shop price in one currency counts in the other at 200 Credits to a Thulium. A Repair Drone I (5,000 Credits in the Shop) is 500,000 Credits or 1,000 Thulium, a Quantum Laser I 800,000 Credits or 1,600 Thulium, the rockets 50,000 to 100,000 Credits each. The two prices are not the same worth: at 200 Credits to a Thulium, 1,000 Thulium would be only 200,000 Credits.
- **The gear the Shop does not sell is his last resort: 200 times the item's reference value in Credits and 80 times in Thulium**, and at least 50 times what the cheapest other way to get it costs (making it yourself, or another pilot's price). A Quantum Laser III is 84,966,000 Credits or 169,932 Thulium, a Helios Beam 682,192,400 Credits or 1,364,388 Thulium and a Hull Plating III 1,271,074,400 Credits or 2,542,152 Thulium. These rows wear a small **Last resort** badge, and so do the rare resources below.
- **The rare crafting resources cost 1,000 times more again**, in both currencies: the resources that recipes take as ingredients (Ship Fragment, Cataclysite, Power Core, Quorvium, Daraxium, Nyxite, Reinforced Hull Plate, Velkonite Reinforced Plate, Orvium Reinforced Plate and Dark Matter) and the Experimental Fusion Core. A Power Core is 20,000,000 Credits or 40,000 Thulium, a lot of ten Quorvium 16,000,000 Credits or 32,000 Thulium, an Experimental Fusion Core 44,000,000 Credits or 88,000 Thulium, a Velkonite Reinforced Plate 1,491,000,000 Credits or 2,982,000 Thulium and an Orvium Reinforced Plate 4,558,200,000 Credits or 9,118,000 Thulium. Everything else the Shop sells keeps the prices of the first line (the other ammo, the rockets, the first-tier gear). Point at the price of a rare resource and a note says so.
- **No end to his stock.** A purchase takes up to 1,000 lots (25 pieces of an item that is no stack), and a rocket is carried up to its stack as everywhere.
- **No daily limit.** The Auction's daily buying cap and the cap between two pilots are for pilots buying from pilots; he is nobody and nothing moves to anybody. There is no tax or deposit: what you pay is gone from the game.
- **What you get is a plain item**: not [Marketable](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md#marketable-items), so it cannot be listed for another pilot.
- **The Auction's rules hold**: level 5, the pause, a ban and the Auction's closing days. His rows are the same whether or not he is flying, and there is nothing to cancel or bid on.

## Rankings

He is in the **Hall of Fame** on the Rankings page like a pilot, on both boards (PvE and PvP) of every world: a row with a rank number, an avatar with his initial, his name, no company (the column says Neutral), his world and his points. The pilots below him move down one place. In the **All** view he has three rows, one for each world.

- **His points are a level 20 pilot's.** They are worked out by the same formula as a pilot's PvE points ([Ranks](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ranks.md)): 20 levels at 100 points each, plus the 5,242,880,000 experience a pilot has on reaching level 20 divided by 1,000, and no kills: **5,244,880** on the PvE board and **0** on the PvP board. A pilot with exactly as many points stands below him.
- **He is listed every day**, whether he is out or not. His row opens no profile: he has no account.
- **The row is all he has in the rankings.** He is in no company, so he is on none of the company ladders of the [ranks](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ranks.md) and cannot wear the crown. Your points, your rank and what you are paid are the same as without him; only the numbers that count the Hall of Fame (the one in front of a name and the position on a profile) count him too.
