# Achievements

Achievements are the goals of a pilot's whole career: destroy your first alien, reach level 21, hold a billion credits, live through a wipe. The game counts them for you. There are **40** of them, in **8** groups and **5** tiers. Each one pays **once per account, for good**: credits for every one, and for most of them a **title** to wear beside your name. A wipe never takes an achievement, its reward or its title back.

**In one minute**

- Open **Community › Achievements** in the station to see every goal, how far you are and what it pays.
- The tier (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic or Legendary) sets the credits: the harder the goal, the higher the tier.
- You are paid the moment you reach a goal, in flight or at the station. A card shows the achievement and its reward, and the Game Log keeps a line.
- **3** of the 40 are hidden: the page shows them as closed cards until you unlock them. They count in the 40 and are not named here.
- In the Steam build your achievements also show on your Steam profile, once you have linked your account (see below).

## Tiers and rewards

The five tiers pay the same for every achievement in them, once. The table counts all 40, the hidden ones too.

| Tier | Achievements | Credits each |

## Your progress

**Community › Achievements** lists every achievement of the game with a badge in the colour of its tier. A card shows the name, the goal, a bar for how far you are (for a goal that counts, such as 4,212 of 10,000), the reward, and, once it is yours, the day you unlocked it and what share of the pilots have it. The filters at the top show all of them, the unlocked ones, the ones you have started or the ones still locked.

A hidden achievement that is still locked is a closed card with no goal and no bar. When you unlock it, it shows like any other.

## Titles

A **title** is a short line of text beside a pilot's name. 32 of the 40 achievements give one, yours for good when you unlock the achievement. Titles also come from the end of a season (your rank, the best pilot of each company and the pilot with the most PvE points) and from the game's team, such as the **Pioneer** title for every account that existed on the day the game launched on Steam.

- You own many titles and **wear one** at a time. You choose it on your **pilot profile**, and you can take it off again.
- Your title shows on your **profile**, in the **Rankings**, in the **Group** window and when you point at your name in the **chat**. Since 0.4.27 it also shows on the map: a small, dim line in the title's rarity colour **above the name of every pilot's ship**, yours included. A long title is cut with an ellipsis. In a crowd (more than eight pilots on the screen) only your friends, your target and the ship you selected keep theirs, so the screen stays readable. It does not show in the Target window or on the minimap.
- Titles survive the wipe.

## Steam

In the **Steam build** you can link your game account to your Steam account, and then your achievements are set on Steam too. The direct downloads have no Steam, so they have no link; the game's own achievements, rewards and titles work the same in every build.

- **Link it** in **Settings › Account**, on the Steam card. You type your game password again to start the link. The game then asks Steam to vouch for who you are, and the server trusts only Steam's answer: nothing you type can name a Steam account.
- **One to one.** One game account holds at most one Steam account, and one Steam account links to one game account. If a Steam account is already linked to another pilot, the game says so and links nothing. An admin can move a link by hand when two accounts claim the same Steam account.
- **What the server keeps** is your Steam ID, the public number of your Steam account, and the time you linked it. It keeps nothing else from Steam: not your Steam name, friends, games or password. Only you and the game's administrators can see the ID; other pilots never do.
- **Unlink** on the same card, with your password; the stored Steam ID is deleted at once. After an unlink you can link a different Steam account only after 24 hours; linking the same one again works at once. For those 24 hours the server keeps a short note of the unlink (the time, and a scrambled code made from the ID, used only to tell whether you link the same account again), then deletes it.
- **Achievements reach Steam when you sign in with the Steam build** while the Steam account that is running is the one you linked. The game checks what Steam lacks and sets it, so an achievement you earned in a direct download, or while Steam was closed, arrives at your next Steam sign-in. Offline, nothing is lost: the game keeps the record, and Steam is told later.
- The credits and titles of an achievement come from the game server's own record, never from Steam.

## When counting began

Achievements came with this update. For pilots who were already playing, the game read what it already knew about them, such as their kills, level, ships and play time, and gave them the achievements and the titles they had earned, **without the credits**: the credits are for those who reach a goal from here on. Some goals had no history to read, for example the swarm, Warden and asteroid goals: they count from the day of this update. So a veteran may see such a goal start at zero, however often it was done before.
