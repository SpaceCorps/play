# Company Pilots

Every company keeps a small squad of NPC pilots on its home sectors (`M-1` to `M-4`, `T-1` to `T-4`, `G-1` to `G-4`). They fly for the company around the clock and give a hand to its pilots.

## Who They Are

- **Squad**: 3 pilots per company sector in each world (Alpha, Beta and Gamma have squads of their own), each named with the company's tag and a callsign, e.g. **[M] Vesper**. Their name tags carry a small robot mark and the company's colour.
- **Ship**: an [Ostirion](/wiki/02-Ships/Ostirion.md) with three **Quantum Laser II**, two **Light Shield Cores**, an **Engine I** and a **Repair Drone I**:
  - 48,000 hitpoints, 22,000 shield points, speed 202
  - absorbance 45%: their shields take 45% of each hit, the hull 55%
  - 195 base damage per volley (x1 ammo), no critical chance, 700 range
- **On the minimap**: a green diamond for your company's pilots, an amber one for another company's.
- **No rank**: the small symbol before a pilot's name in flight is the pilot's [rank](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ranks.md) and belongs to real pilots. Company pilots have none, and they are not on your company's ladder. The Company page lists the real pilots of your company, best PvE points first, and a pilot's rank is his place in that list.

## What They Do

- **Patrol** a loop through the sector's station, its portals and a few points in between.
- **Hunt** aliens they can take (Seekers and Phantasms; they leave Bulwarks, Goombahs and Crystalys alone) while pilots are flying in the sector. An alien another company's pilot is fighting is theirs: the squad leaves it alone.
- **Help**: when a pilot of their company within 1,500 of them is attacked by an alien, or opens fire on an alien they could take, they join that fight. They keep out of a fight a mate started with an alien they could not win (a Goombah a pilot shot at, or a Bulwark); an aggressive alien that goes after a mate is fought off whatever it is. An alien they finish pays the pilot holding its claim (the first to hit it, see [Combat](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Combat.md)); if nobody holds one, it counts for the nearest pilot of their company firing at it (locked on, within range, a volley in the last 2.5 seconds). Either way the rewards are that pilot's, and its [cargo](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) crate is reserved for them as if they had made the kill. Company pilots never claim an alien and never earn or collect anything themselves: an alien they fight alone leaves no crate. Locking on from afar, or only being shot at, earns nothing.
- **Ignore the [swarms](/wiki/05-Swarms/Swarms.md)**: a swarm ship is none of their business. They neither hunt it nor join a fight against it, and they never come to your help in one.
- **Fight back** when shot by aliens, and by other companies' pilots once PvP is open and where their [world](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#worlds-alpha-beta-and-gamma) allows PvP: none in Alpha's `x-1` to `x-3`, nor in Beta's `x-1`.
- **Repair** with their drone whenever their hull is down: 1.5% of it per second, from 10 seconds after the last hit.
- **Retreat** at 30% hull to the nearest safe zone and repair there until 90%.
- **Respawn** at the sector's station (or its first portal) 60 seconds after being destroyed.

## Rules of Engagement

- Their lasers never hit their own company's pilots or ships.
- Safe zones protect them like any pilot, and they never fire into a safe zone.
- **Your own company's pilots** can be attacked, but destroying one costs **100 Honor** (your honor can drop below 0), and so does hitting one in the 15 seconds before an alien or anyone else destroys it. Your fire never moves onto one by itself: selecting one while firing holds your fire, and only the attack key or an ammo slot fires on it. A click beside one (to fly there) doesn't select it.
- **Another company's pilots** pay nothing when destroyed (no credits, honor or ranking points), and can't be attacked during the Peace Protocol (the season's first three days).
- Attacking any company pilot follows the PvP rules of your [world](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#worlds-alpha-beta-and-gamma): none in Alpha's `x-1` to `x-3`, nor in Beta's `x-1`.
- **Wrecks**: a destroyed company pilot leaves no [cargo](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) crate and no parts, whoever or whatever destroys it: shooting company pilots down is no way to farm materials.

## Combat Notes

A ship locked on and firing turns to face its target, whichever way it is flying: company pilots circle their target with their guns on it, and aliens turn toward whoever they shoot.

You hear them as pilots: their lasers sound like any pilot's x1 fire, their ships explode large, and another company's pilot coming after you brings in the combat music like an alien would. Your own company's pilots fighting nearby leave the music calm until you join in.
