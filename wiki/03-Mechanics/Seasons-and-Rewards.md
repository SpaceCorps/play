# Seasons and Rewards

Every wipe closes your season. The server writes down the rank you reached, your place on your company's ladder and your numbers of the season, and it pays you for the rank. This page has the rewards by rank, who is paid, how ranking points start again, what the Season Report and your history show, and where the caps come in. The rest of the wipe is on [Wipe Timeline & Seasons](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md).

## What a season pays

The rank you hold at the instant of the wipe pays you:

* **Wipe Points**: 5 to 400, by rank, on top of what the sources of Wipe Points pay (up to 1,090 Wipe Points a wipe: [Earning Wipe Points](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#earning-wipe-points)).
* **Premium days**: 1 to 7, from Junior Captain up. They stack on a Premium you already have.
* **Credits and Thulium**: a restart fund that grows with the rank, from 105,000 credits and 225 Thulium for a Pilot to 6,300,000 credits and 13,500 Thulium for a Senior Admiral. It is small beside the [caps](#the-caps-and-your-rewards): a way to start the new season well, not a lead.

The top ranks are also given a **look** ([Season looks](#season-looks)).

A rank also pays **loot containers** by its tier and a **season title**.

The rewards are paid **after** the caps, so a reward is never cut. They are paid once for each wipe.

### Season looks

Two [looks](/wiki/06-Items/Cosmetics.md) come from your rank: the **Laurel** aura for Junior General or better, and the **Prism** exhaust for Junior Admiral or better. Each is given once and stays on your account for good. The pilot with the crown also gets the **Crown** aura and the **Sovereign** exhaust.

## Who is paid

You are paid for a wipe only if all of these were true:

* You **had a world** at the wipe. A pilot with no world is not wiped and not paid.
* You had at least **150 PvE points** at the wipe (the Pilot's minimum).
* You played at least **3 hours** this wipe.
* You are not an administrator or test account.

The Season Report says which of these was missing, if one was.

## Fresh points

Your **ranking points** count what you earned **since the last wipe**: the levels you gained, the experience and the alien kills of this season. At the wipe they start again from zero, your old rank goes into your history, and your rank for the new season is built from nothing, so every season is a new climb. **Honor does not set your rank.** Your all-time numbers (level, experience, honor, kills) stay as they are.

The first wipe of the new rules is the first to start the count. Until then the points you have are your whole career's, and that wipe pays you for the rank they give you. The minimum points of the ranks stay as they are.

## The Season Report

When a wipe has closed your season, a **Season Report** opens at the station, once, the first time you are at the station after it. If you were offline, it waits for you. It shows:

* your rank and place on your company's ladder (and the crown, if you wore it);
* your numbers of the wipe: PvE and PvP points, levels gained, experience, honor, kills, missions and time played;
* what was **kept**: your ships, the gear that kept its place, the contents of your Transport Cache;
* what was **lost**: the items outside the Cache and, if a cap applied, the credits and Thulium above it;
* what you were **paid**, line by line. A reward that waits for your next sign-in says so, and when you were not paid, one sentence says why.

The sheet also offers to choose your next world when you have not.

## Your season history {#season-history}

**Season page › Progression** keeps one row for every wipe you have flown, newest first: the season's number and dates, your rank and place (12 / 88), your PvE points and, in a wide window, your PvP points, levels gained and kills. A click on a row opens its detail with the credits and Thulium you kept and lost, your rewards and your numbers, and **Open report** brings that season's Season Report back. The wipes before this page was made show as one line each, under "Earlier wipes".

## The caps {#the-caps-and-your-rewards}

Credits above 5,000,000,000 and Thulium above 10,000,000 are lost at the wipe. The rule, with what comes home before the caps and what is paid after them, is on [Wipe Timeline & Seasons](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#the-caps).
