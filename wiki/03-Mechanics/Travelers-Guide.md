# Traveler's Guide {#travelers-guide}

<!-- wiki-search: traveler's guide; travelers guide; guide; bestiary; scan; scanner; organic scanner; organic scanner i; xenolab; research; dark matter plate; studied; ???; alien numbers; museum; parts; body part; damage bonus; organic scanner ii; hunting; kills; learned; destroy; interrupted; attacked -->

The **Traveler's Guide** is a book of every alien in the game, and it starts empty: every entry reads `???`. You fill it yourself, in three steps. First you **scan** aliens with an **Organic Scanner**: five scans open an entry, and the scan that opens it teaches you its hull, shield, damage and speed. Then you **research** what you scanned at the **Xenolab**, the research station at Last Light in the Deep Void. What you learn or research is yours: the guide and this wiki show an alien's numbers only to the pilots who have learned or researched them. Destroying aliens teaches you what a fight shows (see **Hunting** below); the Xenolab researches the rest. And the aliens of an opened entry can drop **parts** in a cargo box only you can take: every part you research at the Xenolab makes you deal more damage to that kind, and your **Museum** in the Skylab keeps them. The target window shows how many scans you have of the alien you aim at, and its **Guide** button opens that alien's page in the guide.

## Entries

- **Every alien, each tier its own entry.** An α Goombah and a β Goombah are two entries, each with its own scans and research. The guide groups them by where they live: the company sectors, the swarms, the Dormant Swamp, the clan Wardens and dungeons, the solo gates, Davidski and the Deep Void.
- **Three pages.** **Vitals**: hull, shield, absorbance, damage a second, speed, aggro radius and how long it takes to come back. **Behaviour**: one line for each ability and, for most aliens, what it ignores (a cloak) and where it lives. **Spoils**: its pay, its box and its special drops.
- **Line by line.** Each line is learned or researched on its own. The pages open in order: Behaviour after every Vitals line that scanning and hunting do not teach (absorbance, aggro radius, return time), Spoils after every Behaviour line. Inside a page you pick the order. When every line of an entry is learned or researched, the Guide window says "Fully researched." The Xenologist achievements count those entries.
- **Hunting.** You do not have to research everything. Destroy **10** aliens of an entry (**3** for a boss) and you learn its whole **Spoils** page (pay, box and special drops) and the Vitals lines a fight shows: **hull, shield, damage a second and speed**. No scan and no Dark Matter Plate is needed. Each tier is its own entry, and every pilot a kill pays counts it, a group mate who shares the kill included. **Absorbance, aggro radius, how long it takes to come back and the whole Behaviour page are the Xenolab's.** A line you have learned cannot be queued, and the pages still open in order for the lines that are left. The Guide window counts your kills ("Destroyed 7 / 10"), marks every line you learned with a crosshair and tells you when a kill teaches you something.

## Scanning

- **The Organic Scanner I** is an Extra for an extra slot. Assembly crafts it once you have researched it (CPUs), and Davidski sells it at the Breach in the Deep Void. **Every alien of the Deep Void needs an Organic Scanner II to be scanned**: with only a Scanner I the game answers "This alien needs an Organic Scanner II." and starts nothing (the Scanner I still scans every alien of the other maps, the Dormant Swamp's included).
- **Scan an alien.** Select it and press **Scan** in the target window: a beam holds on it for 5 seconds, and you must not be interrupted. You may move and fire, but if your ship is attacked while the beam holds, the scan is interrupted ("Scan interrupted: you were attacked."): it counts nothing and the scanner does not recharge, so you may start again at once. Radiation and the other hazards of a place are not an attack. The scan is also lost if the alien dies, goes out of sight or gets more than 1,500 units away; then, as after a scan that is done, the next scan can start 10 seconds after you pressed Scan, the 5 seconds of the beam included.
- **The SCN slot.** The Organic Scanner has a hotbar slot too, labelled **SCN**: a press does what the Scan button does, for the alien you have selected. It is on no slot at first: drag it from the hotbar's Extras picker onto a slot, and that slot's key (the scanner has none of its own) presses it. Point at the slot to read what a press does now, or why it cannot.
- **Five of a kind.** Five scans of an entry open it for research and for the Museum (a scan of the Organic Scanner II counts as two). Each scan must be a different alien: scanning the same one twice counts once. A boss counts once per life, so its five scans take five encounters.
- **What the opening scan teaches.** The scan that opens an entry also teaches you its **hull, shield, damage a second and speed**, the same Vitals lines hunting teaches. The Guide window marks them with a scanner symbol, and the Xenolab does not queue them ("That line is researched already."). The rest of the entry is for the Xenolab to research or for hunting to teach.
- **Everywhere.** You can scan on every map: the company sectors, the Danger Sectors, inside clan dungeons and solo gates, and in the Deep Void.

## The Xenolab

- **Only at Last Light.** The Xenolab is a panel at Last Light, in the middle of `DV-1`: fly within 1,600 units of its tower to open it. It is the only place where the guide is researched.
- **Fuel.** Research burns **Dark Matter Plates** from the Xenolab's tank. Put plates in from your cargo, up to 20; one plate gives 24 minutes of research (a full tank is 8 hours), and it burns only while a line is researched. Whole plates left in the tank can be taken back.
- **Times.** A Vitals line takes 6 minutes, a Behaviour or Spoils line 24 minutes. A boss's lines take twice as long.
- **A queue.** Up to 10 lines wait in the queue and are worked one at a time. The queue keeps running while you fly elsewhere or are logged off, and pauses when the tank is empty.
- **Numbers, not damage.** Research opens an alien's numbers and warnings. It gives no damage bonus: the bonus comes from the body parts you research here (see [the Museum](#the-museum)).
- **Body parts.** A part you carry is researched here too: press **Research** on it in the Xenolab's panel. The part is used up and takes a place in the queue and **1 plate** of the clock (24 minutes; a boss's part takes 2 plates, 48 minutes). A part you take out of the queue comes back to your cargo. You can research a part of an entry you have not opened yet.

## The Museum

Every entry you have opened of an alien that can be destroyed has **five parts**, named after the alien: Claw, Eye, Hide, Core and Crest (the first part of a β Goombah is its Goombah β Claw). When you destroy an alien of an opened entry, it can drop one part you do not have yet. **The part lies in a cargo box that only you can take**, where the alien blew up (where nothing lies, a part of a clan dungeon or a solo gate goes to your cargo at once). It is an item like loot: pick it up in time, fly to Last Light and **research it at the [Xenolab](#the-xenolab)**. It can be neither sold, traded nor listed on the Auction; only a **researched** part counts, and your [Museum](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md#museum), a view of the Skylab, keeps those.

Every part you have researched makes you deal more damage to **that kind in that tier**, bosses, clan Wardens, swarm leaders and the bosses of clan dungeons and solo gates included. A part you only carry gives nothing. A β Goombah's parts give the bonus against β Goombahs only.

| Parts researched | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Damage to that kind | +7.5% | +15% | +18.3% | +21.7% | +25% |

- **Who rolls.** Everyone a kill pays rolls on their own and gets a box of their own, and each pilot deals the bonus of their own Museum on their own hits.
- **Which part.** A drop is one part you do not have yet (not researched, not queued, not in your cargo and not in a box of yours), picked at random among the parts your scanner can find. Once you hold or have researched every part it can find, nothing more drops until you fit a better one.
- **The scanner.** Parts are found with an Organic Scanner fitted in an extra slot; with none fitted, nothing drops. **The Organic Scanner I finds only parts 1 and 2** (the Claw and the Eye, +15% in all). **The Organic Scanner II finds all 5**: it alone finds parts 3 to 5. In the Deep Void every part needs the Scanner II.
- **How often.** The chance is set for each kind and tier. It is small for an ordinary alien, so a set takes hours of hunting one kind, and much higher for a boss. Researching a set of five takes 5 plates of the Xenolab's clock (2 hours), and 10 plates (4 hours) for a boss.
- **Keep it safe.** A box nobody takes disappears after 10 minutes, with its part. A part in your cargo is yours like any item: a destroyed ship does not lose it, and it cannot be given to another pilot. A part in a Transport Cache cannot be researched. A wipe takes the part items like every item; the parts you have researched are kept.
- **You are told.** The Game Log says when a part drops ("A body part dropped: Goombah β Claw. It lies where it fell, in a box only you can take: research it at the Xenolab.") and a small notice shows the part when its research is done. In the target window an alien of a kind you have researched parts of shows "Museum 2/5: +15 % damage".

## The numbers in this wiki

An alien's hit points, shield, damage, speed, aggro radius, return time, pay and drops are not written in the wiki's pages: in their place the game shows what **you** have learned or researched. A line you have not learned or researched reads `???`, and so does every alien number when you read the wiki without being signed in. Names, descriptions, pictures and where an alien lives are always shown. Item numbers, mission pay, gate costs and map facts are not part of the guide and are shown to everyone.

The Goombah's four tiers, as your guide has them:

```guide
Goombah#1
Goombah#2
Goombah#3
Goombah#4
```

## Good to know

- **It is knowledge.** What you have seen, scanned, destroyed, researched and found, the queue and the tank are not taken by a [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md).
- **Where the plates come from.** Dark Matter Plates are made from [Dark Matter](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dark-Matter.md), which the aliens of the Deep Void drop often.
