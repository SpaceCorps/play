# Daily Rewards

<!-- wiki-search: daily reward; daily rewards; daily login; login bonus; login reward; calendar; advent calendar; chest; chests; season prize; souvenir; blueprint; streak -->

Every season has a **Daily Rewards calendar**: one small gift for each day of the season. At the station, press **Collect** and the day's gift is yours. The more days you collect, the more you get: four **chests** arrive at 7, 14, 21 and 28 collected days, and in the last three days of the season you can take the **Season prize**. A day you miss costs you that day's gift and nothing else. You find the calendar on the **Dashboard** (the **Daily rewards** card) and under **Season & Profile › Calendar**.

## In One Minute

- **29 tiles.** One for each UTC day of the season. A season has 30 days, but day 30 is the [Wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#30-day-season-schedule)'s 5-minute countdown, so there is nothing to collect on it.
- **You press Collect.** One press collects one tile, once per pilot. Nothing is collected for you.
- **6 hours of grace.** A tile stays open for 6 hours after its day ends.
- **No streak.** A missed day loses that day's tile and nothing else. What counts is how many days you have collected, not whether they came in a row.
- **4 chests**, at 7, 14, 21 and 28 days collected.
- **A Season prize** in the last three days of the season: a souvenir look at 10 days collected and a ship design blueprint at 20.
- **A new calendar every wipe.** You can see every gift of the season from day 1.
- **Small on purpose.** All 29 days together are worth about as much as a few hours of hunting, spread over a season.

## The Day

The calendar's day is a **UTC day**: it turns over at **00:00 UTC**, for every pilot at the same moment, and it is the same day count as the clans' daily line ("Day *n* of 29", see [Clans](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Clans.md#the-day)). It does not follow your time zone, and it is not the season day of the Season page, which turns over at the time of day the season began. The server's clock decides everything: your computer's clock changes nothing.

The card and the tab both say when the next reward opens **in your own time zone** ("New rewards open at 02:00 your time (00:00 UTC)"). To work it out yourself, convert 00:00 UTC. Some examples:

| Where | UTC midnight is |
| :--- | :--- |
| Central Europe | 01:00 (winter) or 02:00 (summer) |
| Moscow, Istanbul | 03:00 |
| Japan, Korea | 09:00 |
| US East Coast | 19:00 (winter) or 20:00 (summer) |
| US West Coast | 16:00 (winter) or 17:00 (summer) |

- **Day 1 is short.** The season begins at the Wipe, in the middle of a UTC day, so tile 1 is open from then until the end of that day, plus the grace.
- **Day 29 is long.** The last tile stays open until the Wipe's countdown starts, which can be more than 24 hours after it opened. Once the countdown has started, nothing can be collected until the new season begins.
- **Administrators can move the season day.** The calendar follows it: days that are skipped count as missed for everybody, and a tile you have collected stays collected.

## The Tiles

The Calendar tab shows a grid with one tile for each day: the day number, the gift and a state in words. Every gift of the season is visible from the first day, so you can see what is coming. It also counts your days (for example "11 of 29 days collected") and says when the next chest comes. The Dashboard card shows today's gift and one **Collect** button.

| State | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Today** | Open now. Press **Collect**. |
| **Collected** | You have it. |
| **Yesterday** | Yesterday's tile, still open during the grace. It shows how long is left and has its own **Collect** button. |
| **Missed** | The day and its grace are over. The gift is gone, and nothing else has changed. |
| **Opens in …** | A day to come. You can see its gift but not collect it yet. |
| **Before you joined** | A day before your first sign-in. It can't be collected. |

While a reward waits, a small **(!)** shows on **Season & Profile** in the sidebar and on **Return to Base** in flight, and one message at sign-in tells you (once for each day on each computer).

## Grace and Missed Days

A tile does not close at midnight: it stays open for **6 hours** after its UTC day ends. During those 6 hours two tiles are open, yesterday's and today's, and you collect each with its own press. After the grace, yesterday's tile is **Missed** for good, and you can't go back any further than that.

A missed day costs you **that day's gift and nothing else**. There is no streak to break: your count of collected days, your chests and the Season prize all go on. A tile you collect during the grace counts exactly like one you collected on time.

## Chests

A chest comes with the **Collect** that makes your count of collected days reach a number, and not before: there is nothing to press and no key to find. It is the count that matters, not the date: the 7-day chest comes with your 7th collected day, whichever day of the season that is.

| Chest | Comes at |
| :---: | :--- |
| **1st** | 7 days collected |
| **2nd** | 14 days collected |
| **3rd** | 21 days collected |
| **4th** | 28 days collected |

Every chest holds a **Specimen Vault**, the container that holds a little of everything. The later chests can add a small extra that the wipe does not take. The Calendar tab shows what each chest holds. A container is yours alone and you open it on the Containers page. A container you have not opened stays on your account through the [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md).

If the game can't hand out the container, for example because containers are not switched on, the chest pays a **bundle** of Credits, Thulium and a booster of about the same worth instead. A chest is never lost, and you never get both.

## Season Prize

The Season prize has two steps. You collect them in the **last three days** of the season (days 27 to 29), until the Wipe's countdown starts. Before day 27 the tab says **Opens in the last three days**, even if you already have the days. Each step is collected once, in either order.

| Step | Needs | Prize |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | 10 days collected | A **season souvenir**: a look |
| **2** | 20 days collected | A **design blueprint**: the technology of a ship design |

- **The souvenir** is a skin, a colour, an exhaust effect or an aura ([Cosmetics](/wiki/06-Items/Cosmetics.md)), picked for the season from a short list of looks set aside for it and never above Epic. Like every look it is only for show, belongs to your account and stays through the wipe. If you already own it, you get the next souvenir you don't have. If you own them all, you get 250 Thulium.
- **The blueprint** is the research of one ship design, done for you: the design's technology and the technologies it needs. Converting a ship into the design is still done in Assembly and still costs what [Ship Designs](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ship-Designs.md#getting-a-design) says. The design of a season is one of the 13, taken in a fixed order, so a design does not come round again for 13 seasons. If you already know it, you get the next design you lack. If you know all 13, you get 4 Dark Matter Plates, which are items: put them in the Transport Cache before the wipe.

## The Gifts

A day's gift is small. It is one of these:

- **Ammo and rockets**, in modest stacks.
- **Materials, plates and consumables**, among them Forge kits (the materials of one Forge step).
- **Boosters** of a few hours. A booster gift adds its hours to your [boosters](/wiki/06-Items/Boosters.md#active-boosters).
- **Credits and Thulium**, in small amounts.
- **A few special items** (Workforce Contract, Forger's Seal): never more than 4 tiles of a season.

This page names no gift of any season, because the calendar is different every wipe. The Calendar tab shows this season's gifts. A gift is a gift: items from the calendar carry no **Marketable** tag ([Marketable items](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Inventory.md#marketable-items)) and can't be listed on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md).

## The Wipe

A new season is a new calendar. At the [Wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) the old one closes and the new one opens with its own 29 gifts, and your count starts again at 0. The wipe takes nothing you collected except what it takes from every item:

- **Deleted:** gifts that are items (ammo, rockets, materials, plates, consumables) are deleted by the wipe unless they are in your [Transport Cache](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#transport-cache-travel-capsule-). The Calendar marks such a gift with a note.
- **Kept:** boosters, Credits, Thulium, special items, looks, technologies (a blueprint) and containers.
- **The last four days (26 to 29) give only gifts that are kept**, so a gift of the last week is never lost to the wipe.

## Late Joiners and Late Calendars

**If you join in the middle of a season,** the tiles before your first sign-in show **Before you joined** and can't be collected. The chests and the Season prize ask the same number of days from everybody, so a pilot who joins late can reach fewer of them. A pilot who first signs in on day 16 has 14 tiles left, so 14 days is the most they can collect: both of the first two chests and step 1 of the Season prize are in reach, step 2 (20 days) is not.

**A calendar can start late too.** Usually it opens with the season. It can open later, for example when the game gains Daily Rewards in the middle of a season. Such a calendar has only the days that are left, and everything scales with it: one that opens on day 14 has 16 tiles ("of 16"), chests at 4, 8, 12 and 15 days collected, and Season prize steps at 6 and 11. The Dashboard card says on which day it opened. If fewer than 10 days are left, the running season gets no calendar at all (**No calendar this season**) and the next one starts with the wipe.

## Questions

- **Why can't I collect day N?** There are four reasons. The day has not come yet (**Opens in …**). The day and its grace are over (**Missed**). The day was before your first sign-in (**Before you joined**). Or the Wipe's countdown has started. A season without a calendar, or a calendar that is switched off, has nothing to collect.
- **Does it follow my time zone?** No. The day is a UTC day. Only the clock times shown are converted to your time zone ([The Day](#the-day)).
- **What if the server was down?** The 6 hours of grace cover a short outage around midnight. For a longer one, an administrator can keep a day open for everybody for longer. Its tile then shows as **Yesterday** with its own countdown.
- **Can I collect two days at once?** Only while two tiles are open, in the 6 hours after a UTC midnight: yesterday's and today's, one press each. A tile can't be collected twice, or in advance.
- **Do I have to sign in every day?** No. There is no streak: a day you skip costs you that day's gift, and the chests and the Season prize only count how many days you collected.
- **Does Premium change it?** No. Premium gives no extra tiles and no better gifts, and the calendar gives no Premium days.
- **Do I need a clan?** No. The calendar uses the same day as the clans' daily line, but it belongs to you alone.
- **Is there a level limit?** No. Every pilot can collect, from level 1.
- **What happens at the wipe?** You get a new calendar and start again at 0 days. Gifts that are items are deleted unless they are in the Transport Cache; the rest is kept ([The Wipe](#the-wipe)).

## Good to Know

- The gifts, the numbers and the grace are set on the game server and may change in an update; the Calendar tab always shows the ones in force.
- A game server from before Daily Rewards has no calendar, and the card and the tab stay hidden. An administrator can also switch the calendar off; what you collected stays collected.
- Each new calendar is made ahead of the wipe, so the game's administrators can look at it before it starts and change a gift that is wrong.
