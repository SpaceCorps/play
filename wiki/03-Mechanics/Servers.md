# Servers

SpaceCorps runs on three game servers: **Helsinki** (Europe), **Singapore** (Asia) and **US East** (North America). Helsinki is the **home server**: your account, your station, your ship, your items and your rewards live there. Singapore and US East are **regional servers**: you can fly from them to be nearer to the game and see a shorter delay, and the flight is sent home to Helsinki when you dock. Every server has its own sky and its own pilots; the rules, the aliens, the swarms and the season clock are the same on all of them.

## At a glance

| | Helsinki (home server) | Singapore and US East (regional servers) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Your account, station, ship, items and rewards | here | at Helsinki |
| Flying, fighting, mining, looting, missions | yes | yes |
| The pilots you meet | those flying from Helsinki | those flying from that server |
| The Hangar, Skylab, Assembly, Auction, shop, Clan page and Galaxy Gates | as usual | the station's: the window says so and offers **Return to Base** |

## How a flight from a regional server goes {#how-a-flight-goes}

1. **Launch.** Choose the server in the list ([below](#choosing-a-server)) and press Launch. The home server sends you to the regional server, and your ship is there as it stands in your station at that moment.
2. **Fly.** You fight, mine and loot as anywhere. What you earn stays on the regional server for now: credits, Thulium, experience, honor, your statistics, and the experience of your drones, pets and pilot skills. About every **30 seconds** it is sent home to Helsinki, so little is lost if something goes wrong.
3. **Dock.** When you dock (**Return to Base**, or a dock at a station), the flight ends, the rest of what you earned is sent home and you are back at your station. Your ranking points, ranks and the ladders are worked out at Helsinki, so they move when you dock, not during the flight.

If the game closes or the connection is lost, the regional server waits for you a short while and then ends the flight itself and sends it home, as a dock would. The home server stays the only place that knows what you own: the regional server reports what you did, and nothing else of yours changes there.

## Choosing a server

The **Servers** list is on the sign-in screen and on the launch sheet. Each row has the server's name and region (Europe, Asia, North America), its **Ping** (how long a connection to it takes: the lower the nearer), how many are **Flying** there and its **Status**. Click a row to choose it: the choice is remembered on this computer, and Launch flies from it. If the server you chose is not open when you launch, you fly from Helsinki instead and the game tells you so. A regional server takes 60 pilots at a time.

### The status words {#status-words}

| Status | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| Open | you can launch there |
| Full | all its places are taken: try again in a moment, or choose another server |
| Updating, Being updated | the server is on another version of the game while an update is on its way: wait a little |
| Update the game | the server runs a newer version than your game: update the game |
| Down | the server does not answer Helsinki just now: try again later |
| Maintenance | no one can launch from it right now: a wipe is counting down, or the server is switched off for work |
| Staff only | not open to players yet |
| Unreachable | your computer cannot reach it: see below |

## When a server cannot be reached {#unreachable}

**Unreachable** means your computer could not connect to that server, and the words in brackets (in the row's tip, and in the message when a launch fails) say how: its name could not be found, it did not answer in time, it refused the connection, or the secure connection failed. Some networks block a server's address, and a server can be reachable for one pilot and not for another. **Fly from Helsinki meanwhile**: nothing is lost, and your station is there. If it keeps happening, tell us which server, the words in brackets and the country you play from; it tells us what to fix.

A server can also have a **backup route**, a second address of the same server. When your computer cannot connect to the server’s own address but can reach its backup route, the row is open, its Ping is the one of the backup route, its tip says “Reached through its backup route.” and the launch goes that way. You do not have to choose it, and nothing else changes.

## Bring my ship home

If you close the game while you fly from a regional server, your ship may still be on its flight when you come back to the station. The station then says **Your ship is still flying on another server**, and the **Bring my ship home** button ends that flight and brings the ship home, so that the station works again.

## Updates and the wipe

The servers are updated together: before an update the regional servers are asked to send their flights home, and while one is on another version its row says Updating. All servers follow the home server's season clock. In the last five minutes before a [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) nobody can launch from a regional server (Maintenance), half a minute before it the regional servers send everyone home, and they open again once their sky is rebuilt for the new season.

## Your data

While you fly from a regional server, a copy of your ship, items and wallet is held there for the flight, without your e-mail address or password, and it is deleted when the flight has been sent home.
