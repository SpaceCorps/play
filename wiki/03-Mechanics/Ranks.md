# Ranks

Every pilot has a **rank**, from Junior Pilot to Senior Admiral. It is a military ladder of 8 tiers with 3 grades each, 24 ranks in all. Your rank comes from your **PvE points**: the points you earn from your level, your experience and the aliens you destroy. Every rank asks for a **minimum** of PvE points, and the six best ranks also have a fixed number of **places** in each company. In flight the game shows only a small **symbol** before a pilot's name, so you can tell the best pilots of a company from a fresh one at a glance, and one pilot of the whole server wears a gold **crown** over his symbol. The words are on the Company page and in the hover tip of a symbol.

**In one minute**

- Your company keeps one list of its pilots, the pilot with the most PvE points first. Every company has its own, and the pilots of the other companies never count against you.
- You hold the **highest rank whose minimum your points reach and that still has a place free in your company**.
- The six best ranks have a number of places per company: **Senior Admiral 1** (from 50,000 PvE points), **Admiral 2** (from 45,000), **Junior Admiral 4** (from 40,000), **Senior General 8** (from 30,000), **General 10** (from 27,500) and **Junior General 15** (from 25,000). The ranks below have no limit on pilots, and each asks 2,500 points less than the rank above it, down to the 2,500 of a Junior Captain.
- **Equal points, equal rank.** Two pilots with the same points never hold different ranks. When the last place of a rank falls inside a tie, the tied pilots all take the better rank, even if that goes over its places.
- Only pilots with PvE points who flew in the last 30 days are on a list. Everybody else is a Junior Pilot until they fly again, and holds no place.
- A **gold crown** over the symbol marks the pilot with the most PvE points of the whole server: every company, all three worlds.
- Your rank moves. Up to Senior Colonel it depends on your points alone, so it only rises. The six best ranks also move with the other pilots of your company: they can fall when others take the places above you. A fall is never announced; a new best rank is.
- The wipe does not change anybody's points, so the lists do not start over.
- The symbol before a name is the rank: a shape for the tier, one to three marks for the grade.

## The company ladder

The pilots of your company, **all three worlds together**, stand in one line, the pilot with the most PvE points first. This line is the **ladder**. When two pilots have the same points, the one with the higher level is listed first, then the older pilot, but that only sets the order of the list: it never gives them different ranks. Your rank comes from two things, your points and the places of your company:

1. **Every rank has a minimum** of PvE points ([the table below](#the-24-ranks)). A pilot never holds a rank whose minimum his points have not reached.
2. **The six best ranks have places.** They are filled from the top, the best rank first: the best pilots who reach 50,000 points are the Senior Admirals, up to the one place; of the pilots left, the best who reach 45,000 are the Admirals, up to their two places; and so on down to the Junior Generals.
3. **Everybody else holds the highest rank his points reach below those six**, which is a Senior Colonel at best. A pilot who reaches the minimum of a rank but finds its places full falls to the best rank that is open to him.
4. **A tie is never split.** Pilots with the same points hold the same rank. If the last place of a rank falls inside a tie, the whole tie takes the better rank, so a rank can hold more pilots than it has places, and never fewer than it should.

**An example.** The twelve best pilots of a company, with the rank each one holds:

| Place | PvE points | Rank |
| :---: | ---: | :--- |
| 1 | 60,000 | Senior Admiral |
| 2 | 52,000 | Admiral |
| 3 | 51,000 | Admiral |
| 4 | 50,500 | Junior Admiral |
| 5 | 44,000 | Junior Admiral |
| 6 | 30,000 | Senior General |
| 7 | 29,000 | General |
| 8 | 28,000 | General |
| 9 | 26,000 | Junior General |
| 10 | 24,999 | Senior Colonel |
| 11 | 21,000 | Colonel |
| 12 | 1,000 | Senior Sergeant |

Place 4 has 50,500 points, which reaches the Senior Admiral's minimum, but the Senior Admiral's one place and the Admirals' two are taken, so he is a Junior Admiral. Places 7 and 8 have 29,000 and 28,000: both reach the General's 27,500 and not the Senior General's 30,000, and the Generals have places left, so both are Generals. The pilot with 24,999 is a point short of a Junior General and is a Senior Colonel, the best rank open to him. If two pilots in the first places both had 60,000, both would be Senior Admirals, and the next two, with 50,000 and 45,000, the two Admirals.

> [!NOTE]
> The places bind only when more pilots of a company reach a minimum than the rank has places. In a small company a rank is simply the points': a pilot alone in his company is a Colonel with 21,000 PvE points and a Senior Admiral with 50,000.

## Who is on the ladder

A pilot is on the ladder of his company when **all three** are true:

- he is in a company;
- he has PvE points: the first kill puts a new pilot on the ladder;
- he has flown in the last **30 days**.

A pilot who is not on the ladder is a **Junior Pilot** wherever a rank shows, has no place and is not in the company's list. The rule is there so that a pilot who tried the game once and left does not sit at the bottom for ever, and so that a pilot who is away cannot keep one of the few best places, or the crown, for months.

> [!TIP]
> Nothing is lost while you are away. Your points are kept, and when you launch again, or just open the Company page, you are placed again with the points you left with, in the rank those points and the places of your company give you then.

If you change company, your points go with you and you stand on the ladder of the new company, with the rank its places give you there.

## Your rank moves

- **Up to Senior Colonel it is your points' alone.** It rises when your points reach the next minimum, as soon as your kill is counted, and no other pilot can lower it.
- **The six best ranks move with the other pilots of your company.** A pilot who reaches a minimum before you takes one of the places, and when the places of your rank are full you drop to the next rank that is open to you. When a pilot leaves the ladder or the company, a place opens and the pilots behind him can rise into it.
- A pilot with the same points as you or fewer never takes a place from you.
- Your own rank is told to you when it **rises**, and only then ([Promotions](#promotions)). A fall is never announced: your symbol just changes.

## How you earn PvE points

Your PvE points are worked out from three things:

**level × 100 + experience ÷ 1,000 (rounded down) + the points of every alien you destroyed**

A tougher alien is worth more: a kill adds points by how much damage it takes to destroy the alien. The swarm ships and the Clan Wardens count by the same rule, so a Pirate Scout is worth as much as a Bulwark. Every kill is counted under the alien's own name in your kill statistics.

| :--- | ---: |
| Seeker | 1 |
| Phantasm | 2 |
| Bulwark | 4 |
| Goombah | 7 |
| Crystalys | 16 |
| Boss Seeker | 5 |
| Seeker Slave | 1 |
| Pirate Boss | 15 |
| Pirate Scout | 4 |
| Dormant Force | 25 |
| Dormant Pulse | 11 |
| Brood Warden I, II, III | 14, 18, 35 |
| Siege Warden I, II, III | 13, 17, 32 |
| Wrath Warden I, II, III | 14, 18, 34 |
| Brood Drone I, II, III | 1, 1, 2 |
| Siege Escort I, II, III | 2, 3, 6 |
| Wrath Guard I, II, III | 2, 3, 6 |

The first five rows are the regular aliens and the next six the ships of the three swarms. Last come the Clan Wardens, the leaders of a Clan Warden fight, and their crews, the Brood Drones, Siege Escorts and Wrath Guards: I, II and III are the Warden's strength, and the points of each are listed in that order.

For example, a pilot at level 4 with 12,500 experience who has destroyed 300 Seekers, 80 Phantasms and 10 Bulwarks has 400 + 12 + 300 + 160 + 40 = **912** points. That makes him a Sergeant: the minimum is 800, and the next rank, Senior Sergeant, asks 1,000. Up to Senior Colonel nothing but the points count.

The **Rankings** page (Community › Rankings) shows how your own points are made up, and the **Hall of Fame** on it lists the best pilots. The **(i)** next to the PvE points there lists the five aliens, what a kill in each of the swarms ([Swarms](/wiki/05-Swarms/Swarms.md)) is worth, from its smallest ship to its boss, and the [Clan Wardens](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Clans.md#clan-wardens).

## The symbols

The game draws a rank as a small symbol: a **shape** for the tier, in a colour of its own, and **one, two or three marks** for the grade: one for a Junior, two for the plain rank, three for a Senior. The colours run from cool and plain to warm and bright as the tiers rise.

| Tier | Shape | Colour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pilot | dots | steel blue |
| Sergeant | chevrons, one above the other | copper |
| Lieutenant | flat bars, one above the other | silver |
| Captain | diamonds | green |
| Major | upward triangles (pennants) | cyan |
| Colonel | hollow rings | gold |
| General | five-pointed stars | orange |
| Admiral | a laurel wreath open at the top, round one to three stars | rose |

## The crown

The pilot with the **most PvE points of the whole server** wears a small **gold crown** over the symbol of his rank. "The whole server" means every company and all three worlds, not only your company: there is one crown on the server, and the best pilot of each company still holds the rank of his own. If several pilots have exactly the same points at the top, all of them wear it.

Only a pilot on a ladder can wear it. A pilot who has been away for 30 days does not, however many points he has: the crown goes to the best pilot who is on a ladder, and he gets it back when he flies again and is ahead. Nobody is told when the crown moves, you see it. It is not a rank and gives nothing: rest the pointer on a crowned symbol and its tip says "Top PvE pilot of the server".

## Where you see them

In flight you see **only the symbol**, never the words. It stands before the pilot's name, and the crown over it, in all of these places:

- over a ship, in the name tag, and in the Target window;
- in the chat, before the name of the pilot who wrote the line;
- in your group's list, and in the hover tip of a group mate on the minimap;
- in the kill feed, before each pilot who is named (not before aliens);
- in the Hall of Fame, in the Ranking window and, large, on a pilot's profile;
- on the Company page: your own rank, the list of the best pilots and the table of all ranks.

The **words** (Junior Sergeant, Captain …) are on the Company page and in a hover tip: rest the pointer on a symbol. A symbol shows where a pilot stands among **his own company's** pilots, so the Major of another company is a Major among them, not among yours. [Company pilots](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Company-Pilots.md) are the game's own squads and have no rank.

## The company ranking

Open **Economy › Company**. Its **Company ranking** section shows, from the top:

- **Your rank**: its symbol (with the crown, if it is yours) and name, your place ("Place 16 of 31") and a small tag with your percentile ("Top 52%").
- **A bar and a line under it** that say what the next rank takes. A line like "2,500 PvE points to Junior General" says how many more PvE points the minimum of the next rank asks. When the next rank has no place free, the line names the pilot who holds the last one: "3,100 PvE points to pass Sable and reach Junior General". Matching his points is enough, because equal points hold the same rank. The Senior Admiral reads "The highest rank."
- **The best pilot** of your company, with his points.
- **All ranks**, shut until you open it: the 24 ranks, the best first, with their **Places** per company (a dash: no limit), the **Pilots** who hold each right now, with a bar as wide as the rank with the most of them, and the **Min. points**. Your own rank is marked, and a tip on a row says "Senior Admiral: 1 place per company, from 50,000 PvE points." A line under the table says who is on the ladder.
- **The list**: the best **50 pilots of your company across all three worlds**, with place, symbol, name and PvE points. Your own row is marked.

- A pilot with no PvE points yet is not on the ladder and not in the list: destroy an alien to get on it. A pilot who has been away for 30 days is not on it either, until he launches or opens the page again.
- Only names and public numbers are shown.

## Promotions

- When you reach a new best rank you get a toast ("Promoted: Senior Sergeant") and a short, quiet sound. "Best" means the best rank you have been told since you last launched: if you fall a grade and win it back, there is no second message. A rank you rose to while you were away is told once, at your next login. A fall is never told.
- From **Junior Major** up, a promotion also shows in the chat's **System** tab for the pilots of your company who are flying in your world: "Nova was promoted to Junior Major." The company hears it only when your own PvE points lifted you; if you rose because other pilots left the ladder, you get your toast and nobody else is told.

## Ranks and the wipe

A wipe changes nobody's points. It keeps your level, experience and kill statistics ([Wipe Timeline](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#cross-season-progression-permanent-buffs-)), and your PvE points are worked out from them, so the ladder of your company is in the same order after the wipe, and so are the ranks and the crown. Your rank is not for life, though: the six best ranks move with the other pilots of your company, and a pilot who stays away for 30 days leaves the ladder until he flies again.

## How long it takes

These are **estimates** from a model of how fast a pilot hunts, not measurements. A pilot who hunts about two hours a day, 58 hours in a season, has about 21,200 PvE points after one season: a Colonel. In hours of hunting, a Pilot's minimum comes inside the first hour, a Sergeant's in 4, a Captain's in 18, a Major's in 38, a Colonel's in 55, a Junior General's in 67, a General's in 73, a Senior General's in 78, a Junior Admiral's in 101, an Admiral's in 113 and the Senior Admiral's in 124.

So a typical active pilot reaches the ranks with places in his second season. Whether he gets a place is up to the other pilots of his company, who hunt too: the places of a rank matter only once more pilots reach its minimum than it has places. Until then the rank is simply your points'.
