# Ranks

Every pilot has a **rank**, from Junior Pilot to Senior Admiral. It is a military ladder of 8 tiers with 3 grades each, 24 ranks in all, and you climb it with your **PvE points**: the points you earn from your level, your experience and the aliens you destroy. In flight the game shows only a small **symbol** before a pilot's name, so you can tell a fresh pilot from an old hand at a glance. The words are on the Company page and in the hover tip of a symbol.

**In one minute**

- Your rank follows your PvE points: hunt, level up, and the ranks come.
- There are 24: Pilot, Sergeant, Lieutenant, Captain, Major, Colonel, General and Admiral, each as Junior, plain and Senior.
- The symbol before a name is the rank: a shape for the tier, one to three marks for the grade.
- A rank is **for life**: the wipe keeps your level, experience and kill statistics, so it keeps your points and your rank.
- The Company page lists the top 50 pilots of your company in your world and shows how far you are from your next rank.

## How you earn PvE points

Your PvE points are worked out from three things:

**level × 100 + experience ÷ 1,000 (rounded down) + the points of every alien you destroyed**

A tougher alien is worth more: a kill adds points by how much damage it takes to destroy the alien. The swarm ships and the Clan Wardens have points of their own. Every kill is counted under the alien's own name in your kill statistics.

| Alien or ship | PvE points per kill |
| :--- | ---: |
| Seeker | 1 |
| Phantasm | 2 |
| Bulwark | 4 |
| Goombah | 7 |
| Crystalys | 16 |
| Boss Seeker | 5 |
| Seeker Slave | 1 |
| Pirate Boss | 10 |
| Pirate Scout | 1 |
| Dormant Force | 25 |
| Dormant Pulse | 10 |
| Leader of a Warden I, II, III | 10, 15, 25 |
| Brood Drone, Siege Escort, Wrath Guard (every strength) | 1 |

For example, a pilot at level 4 with 12,500 experience who has destroyed 300 Seekers, 80 Phantasms and 10 Bulwarks has 400 + 12 + 300 + 160 + 40 = **912** points, which is a Junior Sergeant.

The **Rankings** page (Community › Rankings) shows how your own points are made up, and the **Hall of Fame** on it lists the best pilots. The swarm ships ([Swarms](/wiki/05-Swarms/Swarms.md)) and the [Clan Wardens](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Clans.md#clan-wardens) are in the table.

## The symbols

The game draws a rank as a small symbol: a **shape** for the tier, in a colour of its own, and **one, two or three marks** for the grade: one for a Junior, two for the plain rank, three for a Senior. The colours run from cool and plain to warm and bright as the tiers rise.

| Tier | Shape | Colour |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pilot | dots | steel blue |
| Sergeant | chevrons, one above the other | copper |
| Lieutenant | flat bars, one above the other | silver |
| Captain | diamonds | green |
| Major | upward triangles (pennants) | cyan |
| Colonel | hollow rings | gold |
| General | five-pointed stars | orange |
| Admiral | a laurel wreath open at the top, round one to three stars | rose |

## Where you see them

In flight you see **only the symbol**, never the words. It stands before the pilot's name:

- over a ship, in the name tag, and in the Target window;
- in the chat, before the name of the pilot who wrote the line;
- in your group's list, and in the hover tip of a group mate on the minimap;
- in the kill feed, before each pilot who is named (not before aliens);
- in the Hall of Fame, in the Ranking window and, large, on a pilot's profile.

The **words** (Junior Sergeant, Captain …) are on the Company page and in a hover tip: rest the pointer on a symbol. [Company pilots](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Company-Pilots.md) are the game's own squads and have no rank.

## The company ranking

Open **Economy › Company**. Its **Company ranking** section lists the best **50 pilots of your company in your world**, by PvE points: place, symbol, name and points. Above the list are your own rank, your place ("Place 3 of 41") and a bar that shows how many PvE points you still need for the next rank. **All ranks** unfolds a table of all 24 ranks, like the one above.

- A pilot with no PvE points yet is not listed: destroy an alien to get on the list.
- The list is kept for **your world**: a pilot of your company who plays in another world is on the list of that world.
- Only names and public numbers are shown.

## Promotions

- When you reach a new rank you get a toast ("Promoted: Senior Sergeant") and a short, quiet sound. A rank you earned while you were away is told once, at your next login.
- From **Junior Major** up, a promotion also shows in the chat's **System** tab for the pilots of your company who are flying in your world: "Nova was promoted to Junior Major."

## Ranks and the wipe

A wipe changes nothing here. It keeps your level, experience and kill statistics ([Wipe Timeline](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#cross-season-progression-permanent-buffs-)), and your PvE points are worked out from them, so your points and your rank go through every wipe. A rank is for life.

## How long it takes

These are **estimates** from a model of how fast a pilot hunts, not measurements. The second rank comes inside the first hour of hunting and a Sergeant's within five. A pilot who hunts about two hours a day ends the first season as a Captain or Senior Captain. The Majors take a season or two, the Colonels two to five, the Generals six to eleven. The last three ranks need more than 880 hours of hunting, fifteen seasons at two hours a day, and the very last more than 1,700: they are for the very best.
