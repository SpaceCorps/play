# P.E.T.s {#pets}

A **P.E.T.** (Personal Escort Technology) is a small companion that flies beside your ship and adds boosts to it: more Credits, more Thulium, more hull or shield, more damage or more luck. Two of the ten types also work for you while you fly: the **Retriever** takes the cargo boxes around your ship and the **Mole** breaks asteroids. You find **Cluster Chunks** in broken asteroids, put one with an Orvium Reinforced Plate in the **Cradle** of your Hangar, and some time later a P.E.T. hatches. You **dock** it in your **P.E.T. Station**, an [Extra](/wiki/06-Items/Extras.md) you fit in an Extras slot of your ship; while the Station is **ON** the docked P.E.T. adds its boosts, earns experience and flies beside your ship as a small drone, and while it is **OFF** it does none of it.

## In one minute

- **Get the Station.** Research a **P.E.T. Station** in the Research Centre and make it in Assembly. It has three levels, with room for **2, 3 and 5** P.E.T.s. Fit it in an Extras slot of the ship you fly. The Cradle works without it: you can hatch P.E.T.s before you own a Station.
- **Find chunks.** Break asteroids from the second tier up: a **Regular**, a **Mythical** or a **Legendary Cluster Chunk** can be left whole in the ore chunk of the party that dealt the most damage.
- **Hatch.** In the Hangar's **P.E.T.** view the Cradle takes one chunk and one Orvium Reinforced Plate for each P.E.T. and has four places in its queue. The chunk's grade decides how many boosts the P.E.T. has: **1, 2 or 3**.
- **Dock and fly.** Dock the P.E.T.s you want while your ship is landed. With a Station fitted and ON they add their boosts and gain experience as you hunt, mine and fly, up to level 20.
- **ON and OFF.** The **PET** slot of the hotbar switches the Station, in flight, and the change counts at once: OFF, your P.E.T.s give no boosts, earn no experience and do not work; ON, they are back. A Station you have not switched is ON.
- **Put two to work.** A docked **Retriever** takes the boxes lying around your ship and every docked **Mole** breaks the nearest asteroid for you, each its own, whenever you fly outside a safe zone, idle or not, with no limit for a day of it: [the working types](#the-working-types). An idle ship is a target.
- **They are yours.** P.E.T.s are bound to you: no trade, no Auction. They stay through the wipe.

## The P.E.T. Station {#the-station}

The Station is an **Extra**. Three items, **P.E.T. Station I, II and III**, are researched in the [Research Centre](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Research.md) (3 hours, 10 hours and 1 day, no Dark Matter) and made in Assembly one after the other: Station I from parts, **Station II by upgrading a Station I** and **Station III by upgrading a Station II**. An upgrade uses up the Station below, which has to be loose in your inventory (not fitted on a ship and not in the [Transport Cache](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#transport-cache-travel-capsule-)), and asks the rest of its price besides, so the three crafts together cost what a Station III is worth, once. You **fit** one in an Extras slot, as you fit a [Cloaking CPU](/wiki/06-Items/Extras.md), and it gives room for **2, 3 and 5** docked P.E.T.s. What each one costs is in the [item tree](/wiki/06-Items/Extras.md#item-tree) of the Extras. The Station and its research stay through the wipe.

- **Only the ship that flies with it.** Your P.E.T.s count on the ship you fly with a Station fitted in the configuration you fly. A Station in your inventory, in the other configuration or on another ship does nothing; swap configurations or ships and the P.E.T.s follow at once.
- **ON and OFF.** Switch the Station with the **PET** slot of the hotbar (drag it there from the Extras picker). **ON**: the first 2, 3 or 5 of your docked P.E.T.s add their boosts, earn experience and work. **OFF**: none of it, and no drone is drawn. The slot says **ON** or **OFF**. The switch is yours and stays through launches and logins; a Station you have never switched is ON. There is a second between two presses.
- **What you see.** A Station that is ON is drawn on your ship: a docking ring floating over the hull with a berth for each place, 2, 3 or 5, each lit in the colour of the P.E.T. docked in it. Switch it ON and the P.E.T.s fly out one after the other to hover beside the ship; switch it OFF and they fly back in and the ring folds small and dark on the hull, where only you see it.
- **More docked than room.** If you dock five P.E.T.s and fit a Station II, the first three (by their places) count and the Hangar marks the others "Not counted". The Hangar docks into the best Station you own, fitted or not, so you can arrange your P.E.T.s before you fit it.

The Hangar's P.E.T. view says which Station you own, where it is fitted and whether it is ON, how many places are taken and which technology and which item come next, with a button to the Research view and to Assembly. When your P.E.T.s give nothing, it and the **Boosters** window say why.

## Cluster Chunks

Cluster Chunks are crystal clusters that rocks hold. They come in three grades, **Regular**, **Mythical** and **Legendary**, and a break of a rock leaves at most one of each. From tier 2 of the [rocks](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Asteroid-Mining.md) up a break can leave a Regular chunk, from tier 3 a Mythical one and from tier 4 a Legendary one; the Motherlode gives them most often. The chances are in [Resources](/wiki/06-Items/Resources.md#regular-cluster-chunk). The chunk is not part of the break's pay: it goes whole, in the ore chunk, to the party that dealt the most damage, and no Luck changes its chance, a P.E.T.'s included.

A chunk is a resource: it cannot be sold, and it is deleted by the wipe unless you carry it over in the [Transport Cache](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#transport-cache-travel-capsule-).

## The Cradle

The Cradle is the machine of the Hangar's P.E.T. view. A **job** takes one Cluster Chunk and one Orvium Reinforced Plate, both at once when you start it, and runs on the server's clock, so it goes on while you are away and through a restart. A Regular chunk takes 30 minutes, a Mythical one 3 hours and a Legendary one 12 hours. Four jobs fit in the queue; each starts when the one before it ends, and a finished one waits in its place until you collect it (**Collect all** from two finished).

- **The chunk's grade decides the P.E.T.'s grade**, and with it how many boosts it holds (1, 2 or 3) and which types it can be: 4 of the 10 types for a Regular chunk, 7 for a Mythical one and all 10 for a Legendary one. The Retriever can come from any chunk; the Mole needs a Mythical or a Legendary one.
- **You choose the type** among the ones you have discovered, or press **Discover**: the Cradle then hatches a type of that grade you have not discovered yet, drawn when the job starts and shown only when you collect the P.E.T. A type another running job has already drawn is left out, so two queued discoveries never hatch the same type.
- **You can cancel a job** that runs or waits: press the small cross on its slot, and press again within 3 seconds when it asks **Cancel?**. The Cluster Chunk and the Orvium Reinforced Plate come back, the jobs behind it move up, and only the time it had run is lost. A finished job is collected, not cancelled. A discovery you cancel frees its type to be drawn again.
- **The Codex** at the foot of the page shows the ten types as silhouettes until you have hatched them. It belongs to your account and nothing is paid for filling it.
- **The P.E.T. Hangar** holds at most **24** P.E.T.s, docked or stored, and a running job counts as one. You can **let one go**: nothing comes back.

## The ten types

A type is an ordered list of three boosts. A **Regular** P.E.T. of the type has the first of them, a **Mythical** one the first two and a **Legendary** one all three, so a Legendary Magpie exists.

| Type | First made by | Boosts in order | What it looks like |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Magpie** | Regular chunk | Credits, Luck, Thulium | A small bird-like drone with a bright coin-like crest |
| **Tardigrade** | Regular chunk | HP, Shield, Damage | A round, eight-legged body of soft overlapping plates |
| **Hornet** | Regular chunk | Damage, Thulium, Shield | A slim segmented body with a stinger and two wing blades |
| **Pangolin** | Mythical chunk | Shield, Credits, HP | A curled body of overlapping scale plates |
| **Packrat** | Mythical chunk | Thulium, Luck, Shield | A round body with a backpack of small crates and a long antenna tail |
| **Fox** | Legendary chunk | Luck, HP, Credits | A slim body with two tails of light fibres |
| **Owl** | Legendary chunk | Damage, Luck, HP | A wide head with two lens eyes and feather-like panel wings |
| **Drake** | Legendary chunk | Thulium, Damage, Credits | A long body with wing blades, horns and a glowing coil along its back |
| **Retriever** | Regular chunk | Luck, Shield, Credits | A sleek body with a wide hoop of soft light for a collar and two long reaching arms |
| **Mole** | Mythical chunk | HP, Damage, Thulium | A stout, wedge-nosed body with two broad digging claws and a small lamp at the nose |

Eight of the ten only boost. The Retriever and the Mole work as well: [the working types](#the-working-types).

## Boosts and levels

A P.E.T. has up to level **20**. Every level adds **0.2 points** to each of its boosts (a 2% HP boost is 2.2% at level 1 and 6% at level 20); Luck rises 0.06 points a level, so that five Luck P.E.T.s stay under the Season Store's top. A boost does not depend on the grade: a Legendary P.E.T. has three boosts, not three bigger ones.

| Boost | At level 0 | At level 20 | Five P.E.T.s of level 20 |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Credits | 1.5% | 5.5% | 27.5% |
| Thulium | 1% | 5% | 25% |
| HP | 2% | 6% | 30% |
| Shield | 2% | 6% | 30% |
| Damage | 1% | 5% | 25% |
| Luck | 0.5 points | 1.7 points | 8.5 points |

- **Damage, HP and Shield** add to your boosters and your clan's boosts, they do not multiply them: the numbers in the Hangar, the Ship window and your pilot page count them, and the **Boosters** window counts them in the totals of the categories they raise (a line **P.E.T. Damage Boost** under Damage, and so on) and has a **P.E.T.** card for the pets docked and the Station's state. Shield is shield capacity (the maximum shield points).
- **Credits and Thulium** add to what a kill pays you, your share of a boss and of a group's kill, and what a mission pays when you claim it, as your [clan's boosts](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Clans.md) do. They never add to a rock's cash, the Skylab's farms, a bonus code or the Auction.
- **Luck** raises the chance of every loot line of an alien or a swarm ship, and of a Warden's private boxes, as the [Loot Luck Booster](/wiki/06-Items/Boosters.md) does. Nothing else.
- **No Station ON, no boost.** Only the docked P.E.T.s that count give a boost: those within the room of the Station fitted on your ship, and only while it is ON. A P.E.T. you took off or never docked adds nothing and earns nothing, and neither does any with the Station OFF or not fitted.

## Experience

Every docked P.E.T. that counts earns the same experience, in full: it is not split between them. A stored P.E.T. earns nothing, and neither does a docked one while the Station is OFF or not fitted.

- **A kill.** What a [drone](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Drones.md#levels) earns for the same kill, times the world's pay (1, 2 or 3).
- **A rock you broke.** 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 for a rock of tier 1 to 5 and 64 for the Motherlode, times the world's pay, to every pilot the break pays; a rock your Mole broke counts.
- **A minute of flight** outside a safe zone with your ship alive: 6, up to 360 minutes a day (UTC). Waiting in a safe zone earns nothing.

Level 1 asks 810 experience, level 20 asks 40,000 in all. At an hour and a half of flying a day a P.E.T. gets there in 23 to 51 days, at six hours a day in 6 to 13. A level gained in flight counts from your next launch, and the Game Log says so.

## The working types

Eight of the ten types only boost. The **Retriever** and the **Mole** boost too, and each also works for you while you fly: the Retriever takes the cargo boxes lying around your ship, each Mole breaks the asteroid nearest to it. They work while your ship flies outside a safe zone, **whether or not you touch the keys**, and **a day of work has no limit**: what bounds a Mole is what bounds you, the asteroids within reach and how fast they grow back ([The Mole P.E.T.](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Asteroid-Mining.md#the-mole-pet)). An idle ship is a target: any pilot who may attack it where it stands can, and a working pet does not protect it.

### When they work

- **Docked, with the Station ON.** Only a docked P.E.T. that counts works: switch the Station OFF and the Mole's beam and the Retriever's pickup stop at once, ON and they go on. Its strength is read when you launch: a level gained in flight counts from your next launch.
- **Every Mole works, one Retriever.** Since 0.4.27 every docked Mole works, each on an asteroid of its own (two Moles of yours never work the same one), each with its own drone and beam, so a Station with three Moles breaks about three times as many asteroids as one Mole, as long as there are enough asteroids in reach. One Retriever works, the one with the longest reach; your other Retrievers only boost.
- **Flying, outside a safe zone.** They stop while a [safe zone](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Combat.md#2-safe-zone-immunity) protects your ship, and while it is destroyed, under [spawn protection](/wiki/01-General/Getting-Started.md#dying-and-coming-back), cloaked or jumping, and go on when none of these holds. A pilot who is logged off has no ship in space, so nothing works for him.
- **Idle or not.** There is no rule about being at the keys: a Mole works the same while you hunt, while you look at a window and while you are away from the screen.
- **What they never do.** A Retriever takes no box you could not take yourself, so never another pilot's protected boxes. A Mole never works an asteroid in a safe zone: asteroids stand at least 1,500 units from a safe ring.

### The Retriever

A docked Retriever takes the nearest cargo box within its **reach**, one box at a time and each in half a second, the time your own pickup takes: it is never faster than a hand. Its drone flies to the box, a ring of light closes round it, and where the box lay a small blip sounds.

- **Reach.** From the middle of your ship to the middle of the box. Your own pickup reaches 200 units; a Retriever starts above that and grows with its grade and level, to 880 units (8 ship lengths) for a Legendary of level 20. A ship is about 110 units long.
- **Which boxes.** Only boxes you could take yourself: yours and your clan's, and everybody's once its reservation is over. Never a box that is still reserved to another pilot or clan, and never another pilot's [private box](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md#private-boxes); your own private boxes it takes. The loot of a kill, the chunks of an asteroid, Dark Matter crates and the boxes of a giant excavator are all boxes. A box you are collecting by hand is left to you, and when you and your Retriever reach one box together, exactly one of you gets it.
- **What you get.** What your own pickup would give: the items go to your Hangar inventory, the [Resource Magnet Booster](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md#boosters) adds its part, and a chunk's Credits and Thulium are paid as when you collect it yourself.

### The Mole

A docked Mole works the nearest asteroid it may work within **600 units** of your ship (about five and a half ship lengths) and gives it **one pulse every 3 seconds**, the time your rockets recharge in. It keeps the asteroid until it breaks, then looks for the next. Its drone flies out to the asteroid and a thin beam joins them. **Each docked Mole does this on its own** (0.4.27): with three Moles you see three drones at three asteroids, and they never pick the same one.

- **What a pulse is.** A share of what the best rocket, [Rivet III](/wiki/06-Items/Rockets.md#the-twelve-rockets), would take off that very asteroid, so armour slows a Mole as it slows a rocket (the Ironhide, the Plateback, the Anvil and the Ancient Husk). Nothing you fit changes it: not your lasers, rockets, amps, boosters, formation or the Damage of your P.E.T.s. A Mole uses no ammo and no rockets.
- **How strong.** By grade and level ([the tables below](#how-strong)). A **Legendary Mole of level 20** breaks **at least as many asteroids an hour as a pilot who fires the best rocket at them**, while it has asteroids to work: from 100% to 118% of his on each of the sixteen sectors that have asteroids, in all three worlds. The weakest Mole you can hatch, a Mythical of level 0, breaks about a tenth. In Alpha a top Mole breaks a Pebble in 12 seconds, a Star Crystal in under 2 minutes and the Motherlode, the biggest asteroid, in 5.8 minutes; the weakest takes 2.1 minutes, 19.8 minutes and about an hour.
- **Which asteroid.** The nearest in reach that is not covered (an asteroid or an [Energetic Wall](/wiki/06-Items/Extras.md#energetic-wall) in the way makes it take the next). Every asteroid a rocket could hurt is one: the **Motherlode** too, and the four wrecks (Cache Pod, Scrap Hulk, Derelict Hulk and Derelict Cruiser), also an asteroid that another pilot has hurt already or that another pilot's Mole is working, and you share the break by damage as you would with rockets. The wrecks you see drifting in the background are only scenery, not asteroids: nobody can hit them. It keeps its asteroid to the break, also when someone else starts to hit it, and works one at a time.
- **What a break pays.** The same as if you had broken it: the Mole's damage stands under your name, so [who gets the chunks](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Asteroid-Mining.md#who-gets-the-chunks) is settled as ever, by damage. The chunks, the finds, the chance of a Cluster Chunk, your Asteroids mission and the experience of your docked P.E.T.s come with the break. The Game Log tells you of the break, not of every pulse. The chunks lie where the asteroid broke, and you collect them like any cargo, by hand or with a Retriever.
- **Asteroids in reach.** An asteroid never grows back within 1,200 units of a pilot, so a ship that stops has the asteroids that stood within 600 units of it when it stopped, and when those are broken it has none until it flies on.

### How strong

Both go by the pet's grade and level. The Retriever's reach, in units, at some levels:

| Grade | Level 0 | Level 1 | Level 5 | Level 10 | Level 15 | Level 20 |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Regular** | 255 | 264 | 304 | 353 | 402 | 451 |
| **Mythical** | 284 | 303 | 376 | 467 | 558 | 649 |
| **Legendary** | 319 | 347 | 459 | 599 | 740 | 880 |

And what a Mole breaks, as a share of the asteroids an hour of a pilot who fires the best rocket at them (the average of the sixteen sectors of Alpha; in Beta and Gamma the figures differ by under 3 points). A Mole is a Mythical or Legendary pet:

| Grade | Level 0 | Level 1 | Level 5 | Level 10 | Level 15 | Level 20 |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Mythical** | 10.5% | 13.5% | 25.3% | 39.6% | 53.8% | 67.4% |
| **Legendary** | 16.1% | 20.6% | 38.4% | 60.4% | 80.5% | 105.0% |

A Mole pays no rockets: what it breaks is yours without the price of the rockets a miner pays. The cards in the Hangar show the numbers of each pet, now and at level 20: the Mole's card says what one of its pulses takes off an asteroid, as the least and the most over asteroids of tier 1 to 5 in your world, and its hover lists the five tiers. A Legendary Mole of level 20 hits **2,125 to 4,800** every 3 seconds in Alpha, 3,000 to 5,143 in Beta and 3,400 to 5,334 in Gamma; armour takes some off.

### A day without a limit

Neither pet has a limit of its own for a day: they work every hour you fly. Nothing bounds what **any** pilot is paid in Credits and Thulium for chunks over a day, a Mole's pilot included: the chunks a Mole breaks pay in full. What stays is the supply: a sector holds only so many asteroids and each one grows back only after a while, a Mole works only the ones within its reach, and in a crowded sector every pilot gets fewer. How chunks are paid is in [The rules](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Asteroid-Mining.md#the-rules) of Asteroid Mining.

## Crossbreeding

Two P.E.T.s of yours, neither docked and none in a job, can be **crossbred** into one. Open **Crossbreed** on a P.E.T. card, pick the second one and the sheet shows the result before you confirm.

- **Its grade** is the higher of the two.
- **Its boosts** are as many as that grade holds, chosen by you among the boosts of both parents together, never more: two Regular parents give one boost, two Mythical parents two, and a pair with a Legendary three.
- **Its type**, the look, the name and the work, is the one of either parent you pick: a child of the Mole's or the Retriever's type works, a child of any other type only boosts, and two parents that both worked give one kind of work, not two. Its strength is that of its own grade and level, never more than a Legendary's of level 20.
- **Its level** is the more experienced parent's; the progress towards its next level is lost.
- **The fee** goes by the grade of the result: **20,000 Credits** for a Regular one, **100,000 Credits and 25 Thulium** for a Mythical one and **400,000 Credits and 150 Thulium** for a Legendary one. Both parents are gone.
- **It never makes a stronger P.E.T. than the better parent has**, and a result that would equal one parent is refused as "Nothing to gain". What it is for is moving a boost, or a level, from one P.E.T. to another: a Legendary Drake and a Legendary Fox can become a P.E.T. that keeps Damage from one and HP and Credits from the other.

## Docking and flying

- **Dock and undock only while your ship is landed.** In flight the Cradle works with the Hangar open over the game (**New assembly**, **Collect**, **Collect all**, **Cancel**, **Crossbreed** and **Let go**), but **Dock** and **Undock** are greyed out and say why on hover ("Park your ship to dock or undock your P.E.T.s."). A change of docking counts from your next launch, so nothing swaps in the middle of a fight. The Station is the exception: its PET slot works in flight.
- **They fly beside you.** Every docked P.E.T. that counts is drawn as a **small armoured drone with moving parts** (wings that beat, legs that walk, a hoop that turns, claws that dig) beside its ship while the Station is ON, up to five a ship, and everybody sees them and the ring. They cannot be targeted, clicked or hit. Each has a light in its type's colour and a crystal over it in its grade's colour, so they are not taken for a formation's drones. **Show P.E.T.s** in Settings › Interface (**All**, **Mine only** or **Off**) hides them and the Station without touching your drones, and your **Detail** setting (Settings › Graphics) decides how many ships' P.E.T.s are drawn at once, the nearest 4, 8, 12 or 16.
- **They go to work.** While a Mole works, its drone leaves its place for the asteroid and a thin beam joins them; a Retriever's drone flies to its box and back. One drone of a ship is away at a time, and with **Reduce Motion** on they stay at their places. Hiding the P.E.T.s hides the drones, not the work. **Reduce Motion** also makes the Station and the P.E.T.s appear and go at once instead of flying.
- **Bound to you.** A P.E.T. cannot be sold, given or traded, and it is not an item, so there is nothing to put on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md).

## The wipe

The [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) keeps your P.E.T.s, docked and stored, their levels, the jobs running in the Cradle, the Codex and your P.E.T. Stations with their research. What it deletes is what is loose in your inventory: Cluster Chunks and Orvium Reinforced Plates you did not put in the Transport Cache.

## Sounds

Starting an assembly, collecting a P.E.T., collecting one of a type you did not have yet, docking, undocking, crossbreeding, letting one go, a level gained and the Station going ON or OFF each have a quiet sound of their own, on the Sound Effects volume. So does the work: a Mole's beam hums softly where it works and a Retriever's pickup ends with a small blip where the box lay (in place of the cargo pickup sound). You hear other pilots' pets nearby too, more quietly.
