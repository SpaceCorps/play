# Drone Formations

A **drone formation** is a pattern your drones take around the ship, and it changes how the ship fights. Every formation gives some bonuses and charges some prices: a bigger shield for weaker guns, harder rockets for a thinner hull, faster alien kills for a slower ship. There are **16**. You research one in your [Skylab](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md), make it in Assembly and wear **one** at a time, any of the ones you own. You may change it every **2 seconds**, in a fight and in a safe zone alike.

## In one minute

- **Get one.** Research its technology in the Research Centre (two trees, *Defence* and *Strike & Mobility*), then craft it in Assembly for Thulium. The cheapest costs 7,000 Thulium and 5 Dark Matter, the dearest 46,000 Thulium and 20 Dark Matter. The Shop sells none.
- **Own it.** A formation is not fitted anywhere: a copy in your inventory is enough. You need at least one drone, a Slave Drone or a Master Drone, or no formation works.
- **Wear it.** Drag it from the hotbar's Formations list onto any slot. Click the slot or press its key to wear the formation. The list also has **Standard**, which is no formation: drag it onto a slot and press that to wear none again. You may change once every 2 seconds, in a safe zone as well, and there is no combat lock.
- **See it.** Each formation has its own shape, and your drones fly it round your ship: a roof, a heart, wings, a sword, a drill. Other pilots see it too. See [How they fly](#how-they-fly).
- **Pick for the next minute.** Every formation pays for its bonuses, so none is best everywhere. Wear the one that suits what you are about to do: hunt, duel, fire rockets or run.

> [!NOTE]
> A drone formation is two things: a set of bonuses and prices, and the **shape** your drones fly in ([How they fly](#how-they-fly)). With no formation worn they fly the plain "Wingman" layout ([Drone Mechanics](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Drones.md#formation-movement)).

## Getting a formation

1. **Research it.** The Research Centre of your Skylab has two trees for formations. **Defence** (6 formations) holds the ones that keep the ship alive: shield, hull and a mending shield. **Strike & Mobility** (10) holds the ones that win the fight or leave it: rockets, lasers, piercing, alien hunting and speed. A research takes **10 hours, 1 day or 2 days** and asks **5, 13 or 20 Dark Matter**, plugged in before you start: the stronger the formation, the longer and dearer. Some need another formation researched first (the table below says which). All sixteen together are 16 d 2 h of research, one after the other, and 189 Dark Matter. The fuel, the boost and the Dark Matter are on the [Research](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Research.md) page.
2. **Craft it.** Assembly makes the formation, in the Drones category, for Thulium and materials and no Credits. It takes 5, 10 or 15 minutes. A formation cannot be forged or enchanted.
3. **Keep it.** Nothing is fitted: a copy in your inventory is all it takes. The Drones view of the Hangar, at the station or in flight from a safe zone ([The Hangar in Flight](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Hangar.md)), lists the formations you own under your drones. You may own several copies; one is as good as all of them.

## Wearing a formation

- **On the hotbar.** The **Formations** button over the hotbar opens the list of the formations you own. Drag one onto any slot, in either row, as you do with ammo or a rocket. The slot shows the formation's picture and its three letters, and it keeps the key of that slot.
- **Wearing it.** Click the slot or press its key. Pressing the lit slot again does nothing: to wear none, put **Standard** from the list on a slot and press that. The slot of the formation you wear is lit. The title bar of the Ship window names what you wear.
- **The keys.** A formation has the key of the slot you put it on (1 to 9 and Shift+1 to Shift+9 at first). You can rebind the slots in Settings › Controls.
- **Every 2 seconds, anywhere.** There is no combat lock and no settle time: the new numbers are in force at once. After a change you wait 2 seconds for the next one, in a fight, in open space and in a safe zone alike. The first change after you launch or respawn goes at once and starts the 2 seconds.
- **You see the wait.** For 2 seconds after a change, every other formation slot is dimmed by a dark wedge that sweeps away with the seconds left on it (1.8, 1.5, 0.4 …), and so is every entry of the Formations list. Press a slot too early and nothing is sent: the slot flashes red, a soft refusal sound plays and a note says "Formation ready in 1.2 s". Keep the key down and the seconds change in the same note. With **Reduce Motion** the slots just stay dim, with whole seconds and no sweep. If a request still gets through too early, on a laggy connection, the chat says "The formation can change again in 1.4 s."
- **It follows you.** The game remembers the formation you wore last and asks for it again when you launch or respawn. That counts as your first change: it goes at once and starts the 2 seconds, so a change by hand right after launching has to wait for them. A swap of configuration (key `C`) leaves it on, with no wait.
- **It needs a drone.** With no Slave Drone or Master Drone in your fleet, the formation does nothing. One is enough: the number of drones, their levels and what they carry do not matter.
- **Others can see it.** Every pilot who sees your ship sees your drones fly the shape of your formation. Select a pilot, and the Target window says "Formation:" and its name. Nothing is shared with your group.

## How they fly

Every formation gives your drones **a shape of its own**. Wear one and the drones leave the plain Wingman layout and glide into its shape round your ship, in **0.8 seconds**, swinging round the hull and never through it. It is a shape in space: the roof of Testudo floats over the wings, the Auger is a cone, and the whole shape turns with your ship, so its front is always where your nose points. Six of them are in the pictures at the top of this page, each a Paragon with eight level 8 drones seen from above with the camera tilted a little: Auger, Culler, Gyre, Sanctum, Stiletto and Testudo.

- **No formation, no shape.** With none worn (**Standard** in the Formations list) the drones fly the plain "Wingman" layout of [Drone Mechanics](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Drones.md#formation-movement).
- **Made for the drones you have.** A shape is built for exactly the drones you own, 1 to 8: with one or two you see only the start of it, with all **8** the whole shape, as in the pictures. Their levels and what they carry change nothing.
- **Fitted to your ship.** A long ship flies a longer shape than a small one, and the drones keep clear of the hull.
- **Seen by everyone.** Other pilots see your shape and you see theirs. A ship more than **3,500 units** from your camera shows no drones, and in a crowd only the nearest **64** ships do.
- **Switch it off.** Settings › Interface has **Show My Drones** and **Show Enemy Drones** (the drones of other pilots). They only change what you see: your formation's bonuses work as before. **Reduce Motion** (Settings › Graphics) holds the shapes that move, Cordon, Asterism, Culler, Gyre and Auger, still and cuts the glide to **0.2 seconds**.
- **See the height.** The roof, the bowl and the drill have height, which a view from straight above shows little of. Turn the camera with a right drag ([Getting Started](/wiki/01-General/Getting-Started.md#keyboard-controls-keybindings)) to see them from the side.

## The rules in detail

- **Your hull keeps its share.** A formation that changes your maximum hull changes your hull by the same share: at full hull you are full of the new maximum, at 80% you are at 80%, in both directions, and changing back returns the same number. A change never heals you and never hurts you, and it is not damage. Your shield is different: a formation that lowers your maximum shield cuts the current value down to it, and one that raises it adds nothing, so a shield you charged under Cordon is cut when you leave it.
- **Kill bonuses wait 10 seconds.** The XP and Honor that Shrike, Culler and Centurion add to a kill count only once the formation has been worn for 10 seconds. They count the kills of aliens (swarm ships too) and not quests or pilots. Everything else in a formation works at once.
- **A drain runs only while the formation is worn.** Stiletto and Gyre burn a share of your *current* shield every second, but only in a fight (a hit taken, a laser shot or a rocket fired in the last 10 seconds) and never inside a safe zone. Change the formation and the drain stops that second. It is not damage, so it does not restart the 15 seconds before your shield recharges.
- **Regeneration works in a fight.** Adamant and Redoubt give a share of your maximum shield back every second, on top of the ship's own recharge, up to the cap in the table. It stops when the shield is full and does not count as damage taken.
- **Evasion is a chance to dodge a direct hit.** Asterism's 7% means that each direct hit on you (a laser volley, a direct rocket, an alien's shot) has a 7% chance to do no damage at all, and a floating "Miss" shows over your ship. The other hits land whole, so over many hits you take about 7% less, but a single rocket is all or nothing. An area blast has no aim and is never dodged.
- **Penetration adds to the ammo's.** Gemini and Stiletto add their points to the penetration of your ammo and of a direct rocket. The sum stops at 40% for a rocket and at 50% for a laser (the laser amps add to it too), and a blast has none.
- **Rockets, all 14.** A formation's rocket bonus multiplies the damage of every rocket, the N.U.K.E. and the N.I.K.E. included, whether it hits a ship or an [asteroid](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Asteroid-Mining.md#breaking-one): an asteroid's armour comes off after the bonus. Testudo's all-damage price counts on rockets too, and Culler's damage to aliens counts on rockets that hit an alien. All the factors on one rocket together stop at ×1.59. The best rocket formation, Ballista (+55%), makes a N.I.K.E. hit for up to 116,250 and a N.U.K.E. for up to 77,500: a fresh Paragon (128,000) still survives the one, and a Goombah (80,000) the other.
- **A rocket's reload is set when you fire it.** The 3 seconds become 4.05 under Asterism, 3.33 under Cordon and 2.19 under Redoubt, but never less than the rocket's flight plus a moment (a rocket with a long flight, a Lancet III for one, waits for it even under Redoubt): 4.1 seconds after a N.U.K.E. and 4.6 after a N.I.K.E. Changing formation afterwards does not shorten the wait you already have.
- **Abilities do not follow the formation.** Shield Surge and Emergency Repair work out their total from your maximum without the formation, so a swap cannot make them stronger. The Afterburner multiplies your speed as it is now, and a Repair Drone mends a share of your maximum hull as it is now.
- **A slower ship has a farther point of no return** at the [black hole](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Black-Hole.md). Rampart (−17%) and Redoubt (−11%) slow you down most; Gyre (+10% speed) is the formation for leaving.

## Which one when

- **Hunting aliens:** Auger (+21% laser) for the hard ones, Culler (+12% damage to aliens and +12% XP) and Shrike (+7.5% laser damage to aliens, +6% XP) for the XP. Put on a kill-bonus formation *before* the pull: its bonus counts only after 10 seconds.
- **Duels:** Gemini (+9 points of penetration) cuts through a shield, Rampart (+17% absorption) makes yours hold, Stiletto (+16 points of penetration and +13% hull) bites hardest but burns your shield (3.5% a second) in a fight, and Sanctum (+15% hull and +10% shield) takes the hits.
- **Rockets:** Ballista (+55%) is the heavy hitter, Bodkin (+29%) the mild one with a smaller price, and Asterism (+24%, with 7% evasion) pays for it with a slower reload.
- **Taking hits from a boss or a swarm:** Cordon (+95% shield), Redoubt (+38% shield that mends) and Sanctum.
- **Running away:** Gyre (+10% speed).
- **Changing in the fight is the point.** Open with Gemini to strip the other shield, switch to Rampart or Sanctum when you are the one being hit (a change keeps your hull's share, so it never heals you), and take Gyre when you want out. Three or four on your hotbar are enough for all of that.

## Keeping them

Drone formations stay with you through the season wipe, like your drones: every formation you own stays, wherever it is (in your inventory, on your hotbar, in the [Transport Cache](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#transport-cache-travel-capsule-) or on a ship), so there is no need to spend room in the Cache on one, and your hotbar keeps its slots. The research stays too.
