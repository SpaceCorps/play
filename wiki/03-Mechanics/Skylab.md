# Skylab

The Skylab is your personal orbital facility for resource production and technological advancement. It allows you to construct and upgrade modules that generate Credits and Thulium, providing a steady stream of income to support your operations.

## Overview

Your Skylab operates independently of your ship, continuing to produce resources even while you are offline. However, proper management of power and upgrade schedules is essential to maximize efficiency.

## Modules

### Core Module

The heart of your Skylab. The level of the Core Module determines the maximum level cap for all other modules. You cannot upgrade any facility higher than your Core Module.

### Solar Module

Power is the lifeblood of the Skylab. The Solar Module generates the energy required to run your production facilities.

- **Production**: Generates Power.
- **Importance**: If your power consumption exceeds production, your farming modules will shut down.

### Credit Module

An automated financial exchange center.

- **Production**: Generates [Credits](/wiki/01-General/Getting-Started.md) over time.
- **Consumption**: Requires Power to operate.

### Thulium Module

A specialized refinery for processing raw materials into Thulium.

- **Production**: Generates [Thulium](/wiki/01-General/Getting-Started.md) over time.
- **Consumption**: Requires Power to operate.

## Mechanics

### Building and Upgrading

- **Construction**: Each module must be built individually. Upgrading a module increases its production rate (or power output) and storage capacity, but also increases its power consumption (for farms).
- **Time & Cost**: Upgrades cost Credits and Thulium and take time to complete.
- **Production Pause**: While a module is being upgraded, it **stops producing resources** and **consumes no power**. Plan your upgrades accordingly!

### Power Management

Your Skylab has a limited power budget.

- **Balance**: Ensure your Solar Module output is always higher than the combined consumption of your farms.
- **Active State**: You can manually turn modules ON or OFF to manage power. If you are low on energy, you might need to disable one farm to keep another running.
- **Blackout**: If total consumption exceeds production, all farming modules will automatically cease production until the power balance is restored.

### Resource Collection

Modules have a limited internal storage capacity (approx. 72 hours of production). You must manually collect the resources to transfer them to your ship's account.

- **Capacity**: Once a module's storage is full, it will stop producing until you collect.
- **Transfer**: Collected resources are immediately added to your global account balance.
