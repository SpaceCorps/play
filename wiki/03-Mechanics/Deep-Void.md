# Deep Void

<!-- wiki-search: deep void; dv-1; the hollow reach; void gate; threshold; seed; unwakened; crossing; breach; last light; lantern; lanterns; snuffer; fear; monster; flare; flares; davidski; leviathan; lost expedition; darkness; running dark; void bloom; static storm; dark matter; graveyard; maw; lattice; throat; drowned flagship; listener; revenant; void mite; shade; blackout; gravity sink; crystal; wall shadows; quartermaster; contract; whisper; dormant swarm -->

The **Deep Void** is the dark beyond the Dormant Swamp. Its first map, **`DV-1` "The Hollow Reach"**, is the biggest map in the game, and it is dark: you see only what a light shows. Each world opens its way in by killing the Unwakened and building a **Void Gate** in `DS-4`; a keycard holder then opens the gate and everyone near it crosses. Inside, light keeps you alive: the Lanterns and Last Light in the middle. The flares you carry light your way and pull guided rockets off you, but they keep nothing out. The longer you stay in the dark, the more **Fear** you gather, and full Fear brings something that cannot be killed.

**In one minute**

- The [Unwakened](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dormant-Swamp.md) can die now. Each world's **first kill of the season** opens that world's **Threshold** in the top-right corner of `DS-4`.
- The pilots of the world fill its **Void Gate** with **20 Dark Matter Plates** and **200,000 Thulium**; it then builds for **24 hours**, faster with **speed-up Thulium**.
- A pilot with the **Dormant Keycard** (the last [Signal](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Signals.md)'s reward) opens the gate for **5,000 Thulium**: after **120 seconds** everyone inside its ring crosses to the **Breach**, together.
- In `DV-1` you see a cone in front of your ship. **True light** (lit Lanterns, Last Light, the Breach) keeps the Void's aliens out and drains your Fear. A flare is not true light.
- Dying in the Deep Void sends you to your base. PvP is on everywhere outside the safe zones.

## The Threshold and the Void Gate

- **The Unwakened is mortal.** It comes back 24 hours after its death, and the swamp's three guns (a rocket turret and two laser turrets) now have hit points: a destroyed gun stays down until the Unwakened returns. See [Dormant Swamp](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dormant-Swamp.md).
- **The Threshold.** The first time a world's Unwakened dies in a season, a place opens in the top-right corner of that world's `DS-4`, around **27,400 / 4,800**: the **Seed**, a tiny black hole (scenery: no pull, no radiation), inside the empty frame of the **Void Gate**, with twenty dark sockets on its base. Slumbering Voids and Dormant Sentinels patrol a ring 2,500 to 4,100 units round it. It stays until the wipe. `DS-4` is PvP, and so is the Threshold.
- **Each world builds its own gate.** Alpha, Beta and Gamma each have their own Threshold, gate and pool; all three gates lead to the server's one `DV-1`. The server is told when a world's gate begins to build and when it opens.
- **The panel.** Within **800 units** of the frame, its panel takes **Dark Matter Plates** from your cargo and **Thulium** from your wallet (in steps of 1,000), any amount, any time, by any pilot of the world. What you add stays in the frame: nothing is given back. Cloaked ships cannot use the panel. The panel lists the top contributors and how the other worlds' gates stand.

| Pool | Holds | What it does |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Dark Matter Plates | 20 | half of what starts the build |
| Thulium | 200,000 | the other half |
| Speed-up Thulium | up to 1,000,000 | makes the build faster |

### The build

When the 20th plate **and** the 200,000th Thulium are in, the frame builds for **24 hours** on the server's clock, in **five stages**, one every 4 hours 48 minutes: Anchors, Lower arc, Struts, Crown and Ignition. Then the gate is **open** for the rest of the season.

**Speed-up Thulium** makes the build faster. It can be added from the moment the Threshold opens until the gate is open, up to **1,000,000** Thulium a gate. Each doubling of the speed costs more than the one before; Thulium added during the build speeds up the work that is left.

| Speed-up Thulium | Build from scratch | Speed |
| ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 24 h | ×1 |
| 100,000 | 11 h 13 min | ×2.1 |
| 200,000 | 6 h 41 min | ×3.6 |
| 400,000 | 2 h 48 min | ×8.6 |
| 600,000 | 1 h 18 min | ×18.5 |
| 800,000 | 39 min | ×37 |
| 1,000,000 | 20 min | ×72 |

**Waves at the frame.** While the frame builds, a wave of **9 Slumbering Voids and 3 Dormant Sentinels** comes for it every 2 hours of build work: 11 waves in a build, at any speed. The wave flies to a ring round the frame and **fires at the gate**; the world is told "The frame is under attack." While any of it is alive and in range the build **pauses** and the frame **loses work**, at most **30 minutes** a wave and never back past the start of its stage. Kill the wave to stop it. Speed-up Thulium makes the build faster, not safer: the waves come at the same points of the work.

### The crossing

The open gate is a mechanism that takes everyone near it, not a portal each pilot jumps alone.

- **Activate.** A pilot who carries the **Dormant Keycard**, within 800 units of the Seed, activates the gate and pays **5,000 Thulium**.
- **The countdown: 120 seconds.** The server is told who opened it, and again 30 seconds before the end. A ring of **1,500 units** round the Seed marks the crossing area.
- **The waves.** Four waves of **12, 15, 24 and 27** aliens (78 in all) come from the Threshold's edge at 0, 30, 60 and 90 seconds: Slumbering Voids and Lurkers first, Dormant Sentinels from the second wave and Inert Masses in the last. They hunt the pilots in and near the ring, the activator first. Rivals can come too: `DS-4` is PvP.
- **The crossing.** At 0, every living pilot inside the ring crosses to `DV-1` together and lands at the **Breach**. Passengers need no keycard and pay nothing; cloaked ships cross too. A pilot who carries a mission item cannot cross.
- **Rest.** The gate rests **3 minutes** before it can be activated again.
- **The way back is free.** The portals of Last Light and the Breach take you alone to your own world's Void Gate, as a normal portal jump; a Base CPU works in the Deep Void too.
- **The wipe.** At the wipe every Void Gate collapses into its Seed and the next season builds it again. Your keycard, your explored map, your Lost Expedition and your achievements stay.

## DV-1, The Hollow Reach

- **One map for the whole server.** The pilots of Alpha, Beta and Gamma fly `DV-1` together; every server (Home, US, Singapore) has its own. Your world chat and clan stay your world's.
- **Size.** 128,000 × 72,000 units, 16 times the area of `DS-4`: about six minutes across at cruising speed.
- **Strength and pay.** One set of numbers for everyone, Gamma's: aliens are twice as strong as Alpha's and pay three times as much.
- **PvP everywhere outside the safe zones.** Last Light's safe zone, the Breach's camp and the portals' rings are safe; everywhere else any pilot may attack you, whatever his world.
- **Death.** A death in the Deep Void sends you to your base; you need a crossing to come back.
- **Walls.** Crystal ridges, wrecks and broken megastructures block ships, aliens and shots, and they stop your light too (see [Light and walls](#light-and-walls)). A course you click is planned round them.

| Region | What it is |
| :--- | :--- |
| Last Light | The base in the middle: floodlit, guarded, safe (see below). |
| The Breach | Where every crossing lands, in the west, with Davidski's camp. |
| The Hollows | A dense maze of crystal ridges in the north-west; Lurkers wait in the side passages and Shades walk among them. |
| The Glow | Radiation fields in the north: Void Blooms of every size, and clouds of Void Mites. |
| The Silent Expanse | Open and pitch black in the south: the Leviathan's ground, and where the Dormant Swarm calls once a day. |
| The Graveyard | The wrecks of the lost expedition in the north-east: Revenants, Shades and, in the middle, the Drowned Flagship. |
| The Maw | Broken rings in the east round three small black holes that glow from afar; Deep Masses keep the posts. |
| The Lattice | Crystal pylons in the south-west, joined by pale crystal walls you can shoot apart; Lurker nests wait behind them. |
| The Throat | Sealed in the far south-east by living crystal until your Lost Expedition opens it for you; the Listener lairs inside. |

### Darkness and sight

- **Your light** is a **cone** in front of your ship, to the edge of the screen, and a small **halo** round it. You see what any light shows: yours, your group's, other pilots' cones, the Lanterns. The Skylab's [Radar Module](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md#radar-module) at level 3 widens your cone from 80 to 110 degrees (read when you arrive on the map).
- **Glowing things** are seen from afar: Void Blooms, Lanterns, Last Light, the Seed, the Angler's lure, the Maw's gravity sinks, the Leviathan's eyes. What no light shows is not drawn: no ship, no name, no health bar.
- **The server decides.** What no light shows is not sent to your game at all, so the dark is fair in PvP.
- **Running dark.** Press **0** to turn your light off: other pilots see you only when another light falls on you, and you see only what glows. Firing turns your light back on for 5 seconds. In the dark without your light your Fear grows half as fast again.
- **The map is fog.** `DV-1` is 2,304 squares of 2,000 units, black until your light finds them; what you find is yours for good. The galaxy map shows how much you have explored (**Explored 37 %**).

### Light and walls

- **Walls stop your light.** Your cone and halo do not pass crystal, wrecks or rock. An alien, or a ship running dark, behind a wall is not lit for you: it is not drawn, has no name or health bar, and you cannot lock it. This is what makes the Hollows a real maze. Within 150 units of your ship nothing is hidden.
- **What shines is never stopped.** A ship with its light on shines for itself (your light gives you away), and Lanterns, Last Light and the Breach shine over walls: the Void is kept out whatever stands between. Flares, Void Blooms and the Angler's lure shine over walls too, but they only show what stands in them.
- **The server decides.** What a wall hides from you is not sent to your game at all, so a changed game has nothing to reveal.
- **Computers draw the shadows, phones do not.** **Wall shadows** (Settings, Graphics) is on by default on a computer and only changes the picture. Phones and the browser draw no shadows; the server hides the same things for them.
- **A wall that is gone stops nothing.** When crystal is shot apart (see below), your light, your shots and your course pass through the gap at once.

### Breakable crystal

The Lattice's pale crystal walls can be shot apart; every other wall holds.

- **Lasers only.** Press on the crystal from within 1,400 units to target it, then fire as at a ship (double press, the Target window's Fire, or the attack key). The Target window shows **Crystal**, its hit points, and **Too far** or **Line blocked** when your lasers cannot reach it. Rockets, blasts and the Siphon Battery do nothing to it.
- **Everyone's damage adds.** A lone pilot needs a minute or more, a crew of five about 15 seconds. A hurt wall never mends; a broken one grows back whole **30 minutes** later, pushing out whatever stands in it.
- **It pays nothing and it is a door for everyone.** The whole map is told that a wall broke (not who broke it), a rival can use the gap as well as you, and a Lurker nest behind it is open.

### Hazards

- **Void Blooms**: radiation patches of 4,000 to 7,500 units, more of them in the Glow than anywhere else, that burn 1 % of your ship's maximum hull a second at the rim and 4 % at the heart, straight out of the hull (your shield does not soak it), cloak or not. [Radiation tech](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Pilot-Skills.md) lowers the dose. They glow, so you see them from far away. A click that would fly you across one is planned round it, as the black hole bends your course; a click that ends inside one is flown as given. A gauge shows how long you have left, a warning sounds before you cross the rim, and a Geiger counter clicks inside.
- **The Static Storm**: a storm of 3,000 units that drifts through the outer regions for 10 minutes. Inside it you lose 1 % of your ship's maximum hull a second, straight out of the hull, and your light reaches half as far. The map is warned 30 seconds before it rises.
- **Gravity sinks**: the three small black holes of the Maw, each the [black hole](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Black-Hole.md) of `DS-4` at half its size. A sink pulls from 1,500 units, burns with radiation from 2,000 (Radiation tech lowers the dose) and swallows ships and aliens alike at 150. It glows from 2,400 units, so you see it before it kills; the gauge, the warning and the Geiger counter work as at the black hole. A click that would fly you across one is bent round it; a click that ends inside is flown as given. Aliens keep out of the circle on their own, but the pull takes one that is pushed in.
- **Blackouts**: every 20 to 30 minutes the whole map goes dark. A heartbeat sounds 5 seconds before; then, for **20 seconds**, every light reaches only 40 % as far: your cone and halo, Lanterns and flares. Last Light's floodlight and the Breach's light never shrink, so run for them. Fear drains only inside what is left of a light. It is when the dead rise: Revenants move half as fast again, and two more are raised against each pilot the dark leaves outside true light (at most 24 at a time; they are gone 80 seconds after the dark falls).

## Light against dark

**True light** is light that keeps you alive: a lit **Lantern** (2,500 units), **Last Light** (5,000) and the **Breach** (1,500). Inside it:

- **No Void alien enters it**, and their lasers stop at its edge. An alien that was chasing you breaks off and goes back into the dark.
- **Unless you shoot it from there.** A creature you hit while you stand in true light takes you as its target for a short while after your last hit: it comes into that light and attacks you, and a Revenant is not frozen by it. A hit from the dark changes nothing, and a pilot beside you who did not shoot it is left alone. **Last Light's and the Breach's light never give way**: nothing you shoot from them is provoked. Deep Masses, the Dormant Swarm's ships and the bosses are not provoked.
- **Your Fear drains** (see below), and only here.
- **Other pilots can still attack you.** The light keeps the Void off, not your rivals, and it shows you to them.

A ship's cone, a flare, a Void Bloom and the Angler's lure are not true light.

### Lanterns

The lost expedition planted **78 Lanterns** across `DV-1`; most went out long ago.

- **Kindle one** within 400 units with **1 Dark Matter** from your cargo: **10 minutes** of light. Anyone can add more, up to **an hour** in its tank. It burns on the server's clock.
- **Guttering.** In its last minute a Lantern flickers, and the pilots near it are told.
- **It can break.** A Lantern has 300,000 hit points. Snuffers eat it, and **pilots can shoot it out** too (that pays nothing, and the map is told who did it). A broken Lantern goes dark at once, loses its fuel and can be kindled again 3 minutes later.
- **Shared.** A Lantern is everybody's, all three worlds'. Your minimap and the full map show each Lantern you have found: lit, guttering, dark or broken.
- **First light.** The first kindle of each Lantern in your life pays **1,000 XP**, and counts for the **Lamplighter** [achievements](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Achievements.md).

### Snuffers

Pale, moth-like and weak, the **Snuffers** ignore pilots completely: they never fire. They come out of the dark in waves to every lit Lantern, the one alien that enters true light, and each one at a Lantern eats its hull and burns its fuel. A Lantern nobody guards is eaten in minutes. Shoot them off; they sometimes drop Dark Matter.

### Fear and the Monster

**Fear** is a bar of 0 to 100 % next to your hull and shield, on Deep Void maps only.

| What you do | Fear |
| :--- | :--- |
| Being in the dark | +0.8 a second (empty to full in about 2 minutes) |
| Running dark (light off) | the dark's rate × 1.5 |
| A groupmate within 1,000 units | the dark's rate −20 % each, at most −40 % |
| Flying: the faster, the more | nothing up to 350 units a second, +1 a second at 1,000, and on in the same straight line above it (+2 at 1,650); an Afterburner is just a faster ship |
| A laser volley | +0.15 |
| A rocket | +4; a special rocket +8; a N.U.K.E. or N.I.K.E. +15 |
| **Being in true light** | **−10 a second**, and nothing adds to it |

Four [Pilot Skills](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Pilot-Skills.md) each cut one source, by up to 20 % at level 100: **Grit** the dark's, **Thruster theory** flight's, **Laser tech** the volleys' and **Rocket tech** the rockets'.

| Fear | What happens |
| ---: | :--- |
| 50 % | Aliens notice you from 1.5 times as far. Whispers begin at the edge of your screen. |
| 75 % | The strong aliens within 8,000 units come hunting you. Dark shapes crawl at the edge of your light. |
| 100 % | **The Monster comes.** |

**Whispers and shapes** are drawn on your own screen only, outside true light: a short line behind your ship every 45 to 90 seconds, and a few worms at the rim of your halo. They are atmosphere: they cannot be shot and cannot hurt you (real creatures lurk at the edge of the light too). **Whispers and shapes in the dark** (Settings, Interface) turns them off.

**The Monster** is a swarm of tentacles and worms that comes out of the dark for the one pilot whose Fear is full. It moves at **750**, faster than any ship, passes through walls and **cannot be killed**. If it reaches you, it **consumes your ship at once**, shield and cloak no help. It never enters light: the moment your Fear starts to drain (you reached true light), it turns and flees. Fear goes back to 0 at a death, at a crossing and at login.

### Flares

A **flare** (the special rocket of the Shop, Davidski's and the Quartermaster's) is fired toward your cursor, up to 1,000 units, and **hangs there burning for 10 seconds**. While it burns, every guided rocket locked within 800 units of it turns to it and bursts harmlessly, on every map. In the Deep Void its **700 units of light are a light to see by, not true light**: they show you what stands in them and **stop nothing**. The Void's aliens and bosses attack you under a burning flare exactly as if it were not there, Void Mites are not drawn to it, your Fear does not drain in it and the Monster does not turn away from it. A flare is a 10-second light and a decoy for guided rockets, not a refuge.

## Last Light

The outpost of the expedition that never came back, in the middle of `DV-1`. Its defences still run.

| Ring | Radius | What it does |
| :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Floodlight | 5,000 | True light, lit like day. |
| Defence | 4,500 | Four laser turrets and a rocket battery fire at aliens, never at pilots. |
| Safe zone | 3,000 | Nothing can hurt you; no PvP. |
| Mission Control, the Xenolab and the Quartermaster | 1,600 | **Void Control** gives the Lost Expedition and the daily contracts; the **Xenolab** researches the [Traveler's Guide](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Travelers-Guide.md); the **Quartermaster** sells laser ammo, rockets and Repair Drones. |
| Heal ring | 1,200 | Restores 2 % of your total HP a second while the safe zone protects you. |

The turrets' damage pays nobody: a pilot is paid for the share he dealt himself. Last Light's portal, in the middle, takes you home to your own world's Void Gate for free. There is no hangar: you cannot land, so the **Quartermaster** sells to a pilot in flight, at the Shop's price for you. It also sells **Flares**: 25,000 Credits each (Davidski's cost 30,000), and you can carry 20.

## Davidski's camp {#davidskis-camp}

Every crossing lands at the **Breach**, where [Davidski](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Davidski.md) has parked his gold Ironclad inside the Breach's light. He got here first, as usual.

- **The camp is safe**: no PvP within 1,500 units of it, and its light is true light.
- **He cannot be killed.** Shoot him and he answers with two or three shots of 300,000 each, safe zone or not.
- **Talk to him** within 1,500 units: his window opens by itself on your first visit. He explains the dark, gives the Lost Expedition's first two steps and replays the recording of the *Calloway*'s last flight on request.
- **His shop**: **Flares** for 30,000 Credits each and the **Organic Scanner I** for 250,000 Credits (it scans the aliens of every other map; the aliens of the Deep Void need an **Organic Scanner II**, which only Assembly makes). Assembly also makes the scanner once you have researched it (CPUs).

## The aliens

Every alien of the Deep Void is a threat to a lone pilot in the dark, and none of them can reach you in true light, unless you shot it from there (see [Light against dark](#light-against-dark)). Most of them drop **Dark Matter**, the map's common drop: what you do not burn in Lanterns you take home. Their numbers are in your [Traveler's Guide](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Travelers-Guide.md): this wiki shows them only for what you have learned or researched.

- **Slumbering Voids** from the Dormant Swamp roam everywhere outside the base. **Deep Masses** keep to their posts: they are the Dormant Swamp's Inert Mass with a far thicker shield, a kind of their own.
- **Lurkers** wait unlit in side passages and in the nests of the Lattice. One wakes when a ship comes close, lunges and bites hard, then fights on.
- **Anglers** hang a light that looks like a lit Lantern from afar. It is no Lantern: up close the light goes out and Lurkers come out of the dark.
- **Revenants**, wrecks of the lost expedition, fill the Graveyard and move only in the dark (a Blackout raises more of them). **True light**, any pilot's cone and any pilot's halo freeze one, **but not the halo of the pilot it hunts**: your own halo does not hold it, so on your own you hold it by keeping your cone on it. A Void Bloom's glow and an Angler's lure never freeze it. In the dark it closes fast and **drains your shield and your hull together**: the shield does not guard the hull, and **Grit and a Diminisher do not cut the drain**. A frozen Revenant drains nothing. Keep it lit, and kill it.
- **Void Mites** fly in clouds of ten to thirty, mostly in the Glow, and are drawn to light: to the brightest pilot they may hunt (a flare draws them nowhere and holds them off nothing). Running dark loses them (a shot lights you again for 5 seconds), and so does true light, which no Void alien enters. Each Mite that touches your ship drains your shield, and your hull once the shield is gone; a whole cloud kills an unprotected ship, and a ship the Mites eat dies with no killer and no credit. Weak alone: a big blast or penetrating fire clears a cloud.
- **Shades** look like pilots: a real ship's look, a dot on the minimap and a name of noise (three to six of `%`, `#`, `@`, `&` and `?`), and they shine as a ship with its light on does. A pilot's name is letters, digits and underscores, never noise: that is how you tell them. A Shade shows what it is when you come close or when it hunts you, and a hit shows it to everyone for 20 seconds; it never enters true light. Invitations and private messages to it are answered as if no such pilot were there. Shades haunt the Graveyard and the Hollows.
- **Snuffers** eat the Lanterns (see above). **The Monster** comes for full Fear (see above).
- **Six drops** belong to the new kinds: Revenant Ash, Mite Swarm Core, Shade Mask, Flagship Log, Listener Shard and Void Coordinates. They are collectibles for now: they cannot be sold at the Auction and have no recipe yet. See [Resources](/wiki/06-Items/Resources.md).

### The Leviathan

A segmented worm about **10,000 units long** roams the **Silent Expanse**. You see it only where a light falls on it, and the eyes along its body.

- **Its body hurts** every ship it passes through, the head most of all, and pushes you away.
- **Its eyes fire**: each segment's eye opens and glows violet a second before it fires at the nearest pilot in reach who is not in true light; running dark does not hide you from it. An open eye takes full damage; a closed segment only half. The head takes more.
- **It charges**: every eye closes at once and the head rushes in a straight line.
- **It dives** into the dark and comes up through the pilots; a violet ripple shows where, 2 seconds before.
- **Every part has its own hull and shield.** The head, each segment and the tail are destroyed one by one, a hit hurts only the part it hit, and nothing heals.
- **It breaks.** Shoot a part down and the worm is cut there: the pieces fly on by themselves. A short piece cannot charge, dive or coil.
- **It is one boss.** It is dead only when every part of every piece is destroyed; the last part to fall is the kill. The 60 % and 30 % below count what is left of the whole fight.
- **It keeps to its own ground** in the Silent Expanse.
- **Below 60 %** it coils round a group: break the glowing segment to open the ring. **Below 30 %** the Lanterns near it burn their fuel five times as fast.
- Near it your Fear grows by one more a second, so a long fight is also a race against the Monster.
- It comes back {{guide:vitals.respawn@The Leviathan}} after its death. Its box is rolled once and shared among the pilots its kill pays.

### The Drowned Flagship

The expedition's flagship, taken by the Static, lies in the heart of the **Graveyard**: a huge, slow ship that never enters true light. It is one boss in three phases, and the phase follows whichever is lower, **its hull's share or its hull-and-shield share**, so no amount of piercing fire skips one.

- **Broadsides, from the start.** Each side of the ship that has a ship in sight fires a salvo of shells, a flash showing it a moment before. With pilots on both sides, both batteries fire.
- **From two thirds down to one third: Lights Out.** Every {{guide:behaviour.ability:lightsout.everySeconds@The Drowned Flagship}} seconds every pilot within {{guide:behaviour.ability:lightsout.reach@The Drowned Flagship}} units loses their light for {{guide:behaviour.ability:lightsout.seconds@The Drowned Flagship}} seconds (firing does not bring it back), and so do the Lanterns and flares in that reach (a Lantern keeps its fuel and comes back). Pilots who see the ship are warned a few seconds before. Revenants rise to hunt the pilots it darkened. Last Light and the Breach never go out. A pilot whose light is out is not lit: a rival who saw you only by your own light cannot see or lock you either, and you see only what shines.
- **Below one third: the breaches leak.** Its torn hull lets Void Mites out until it dies, and Lights Out is over.
- **What it raises and leaks counts for nothing.** Its Revenants and Mites behave and hurt like any others, but they pay nothing, drop nothing and count for no quest, contract or ranking.
- It comes back {{guide:vitals.respawn@The Drowned Flagship}} after its death, and its box goes to the pilot who dealt the most damage. Taking part in its kill is step 8 of the Lost Expedition.

### The Listener

The source of the Static: a huge crystal ear in the **Throat**, behind the living crystal. The seal opens for **you** when the exploring task of Lost Expedition step 9 is done, and for good when the step is complete. It opens for each pilot alone: a crewmate who has not got there cannot follow.

- **It never hunts and has no laser of its own.** It hears. Every laser volley and rocket you fire within its hearing, which carries far beyond your lasers' reach, adds one **Noise** to you; the bar under your Fear bar shows your stacks.
- **At {{guide:behaviour.ability:noise.stacks@The Listener}} Noise it may pick you.** One pilot at a time: a short tell, then a heavy beam. The beam breaks when you reach true light, a safe place or a cloak, put a wall between you and it, fly out of its reach or go dark; a pilot already dark is not picked. After a pick it waits a few seconds before the next, and the pilot it picked starts again at zero.
- **Quiet clears it.** Running dark for {{guide:behaviour.ability:quiet.clearDarkSeconds@The Listener}} seconds without a shot clears your Noise. A crew takes turns to be loud.
- Its body is wide: lasers reach it from inside it, because the range is counted to its middle.
- It comes back {{guide:vitals.respawn@The Listener}} after its death. Its box is rolled once and shared among the pilots its kill pays; one **Void Coordinates** is in it. Taking part in its kill is step 10 of the Lost Expedition.

### The Dormant Swarm's visit {#the-dormant-swarms-visit}

Once a day the [Dormant Swarm](/wiki/05-Swarms/Dormant-Swarm.md), a Dormant Force with its two Dormant Pulses, comes to the **Silent Expanse** and stays 90 minutes, or until it is destroyed if anyone has fought it. Nobody announces it and no map marks it: you find it in the dark. It is the swarm of the Dormant Swamp: passive until it is hit, and then everything close to the one you hit joins in. Its ships pay and drop what they do at `DS-4`, at the Deep Void's strength. The first visit comes one to six hours after the map is built, and the next one a day after the last has ended.

## The Lost Expedition

The Deep Void's quest chain, one step at a time and each once in your life (a wipe does not take it). Davidski gives the first two steps at the Breach, *The Recording* and *Light One*; **Void Control** at Last Light gives the rest. Every step after Davidski's pays a Dark Matter Plate. The steps and their pay are in the [Quests](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Quests.md#lost-expedition-table) table.

**Void contracts.** Once you have claimed *First Light*, Void Control also posts three contracts a day, the same for every pilot: an easy one, a middle one and a hard one, out of ten (hunt a number of Voids, Lurkers, Snuffers, Revenants, Mites, Shades or Anglers, kindle Lanterns, keep ten minutes in a Lantern's light, or take part in a Leviathan kill). Take, do and claim them like quests; what you have not finished is gone when the day rolls at 00:00 UTC. There are no streaks. Each pays a quarter of a step, and a claim for work done in the Deep Void is paid by **your own world's** multiplier (Alpha ×1, Beta ×2, Gamma ×3): the day's three pay, before boosts, 1,192,500 Credits and 8,925 Thulium in Alpha, twice that in Beta and three times that in Gamma, plus 25 Dark Matter. The hard contract alone adds one Dark Matter Plate.

## Good to know

- **Bring fuel.** Dark Matter for the Lanterns and an empty Fear bar for a long crossing. A flare lights a few seconds and pulls guided rockets off you, but it keeps nothing away.
- **Fly together.** A groupmate near you slows your Fear in the dark, and your cones light the same ground.
- **Rockets are loud.** Every rocket adds a lot of Fear; a fight in the dark is a fight against the bar too.
- **The light shows you.** In PvP a lit Lantern shows you to every rival who can see it.
- **Shoot crystal with care.** A wall you break is a door for your rivals too, and it grows back only 30 minutes later.
