# Ship Designs

<!-- wiki-search: design; designs; refit; convert; conversion; thunder; bucky; lich; notsum; bratan; recon; duma; titanic; raptor; billy; menati; ataraxis; matt -->

A **design** is one of the four ships you craft in Assembly with a few numbers changed on purpose: less hull for more speed, one laser slot fewer for tougher shields. Eight of the thirteen designs also come with one of seven [abilities](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Abilities.md#ship-abilities) that the plain ship does not have. You research a design in the Skylab, then convert a ship you own into it in Assembly. **The conversion cannot be undone.**

## Getting a design

1. **Research it.** Each design is a technology in the Ships tree of the Research view of your Skylab ([Research](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Research.md#ship-technologies)). It takes 10 hours and 10 Dark Matter, and asks for the technology of its ship and the one of the Dark Matter Plate. Researching it makes nothing yet.
2. **Convert the ship.** The Designs section of the Assembly lists the thirteen conversions. Pick one and confirm: the job joins the Production Queue and takes 10 minutes. It finishes by itself, and you do not need to collect it.
3. **Pay for it.** A conversion costs Thulium, about a quarter of the materials of the ship's own recipe and Dark Matter Plates, as the table says.

## The technologies

- **Hull plate slots.** One technology for each [hull plate slot](/wiki/06-Items/Hull-Plating.md#hull-plate-slots) of the four ships you craft. Each takes 1 h and 10 Dark Matter and comes after the one before it, the first after the ship's own technology.
- **Ship designs.** One technology for each design. Each takes 10 h and 10 Dark Matter and needs the technology of its ship and the one of the Dark Matter Plate.

| Ship | Hull plate slots | Time for the slots | Dark Matter for the slots | Designs | Time for the designs | Dark Matter for the designs |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Paragon** | 5 | 5 h | 50 | 3 | 30 h | 30 |
| **Storm** | 7 | 7 h | 70 | 3 | 30 h | 30 |
| **Ironclad** | 15 | 15 h | 150 | 2 | 20 h | 20 |
| **Wraith** | 9 | 9 h | 90 | 5 | 50 h | 50 |
| **All four** | 36 | 36 h | 360 | 13 | 130 h | 130 |

Together they ask 490 Dark Matter on top of the 414 that every other technology asks: 904 for every technology of the game.

## What happens to your ship

- **It is the same ship.** The conversion changes it in place: its fittings, its hull plates, your drones and the formation you wear stay as they are.
- **Lasers can lose their slot.** A design with fewer laser slots (BUCKY, RECON, DUMA, ATARAXIS) sends the lasers of the slots that go back to your inventory, with their amps and their Forge rolls. A slot that is new arrives empty.
- **Full hull and shields** when it is done. Until then the ship is locked: it cannot be made active, fitted or converted again.
- **There is no way back.** To get another design, or the plain ship again, craft the ship again in Assembly. You own one ship of each kind, so a Paragon and a THUNDER can be yours at once, and two THUNDERs cannot.
- **The wipe** takes a design like any other ship that is not your active one.

## Looks and size

Each design has a model of its own, its ship's with a few changes that show what it does. They are plain grey like the ships, so that colour can come later as skins. BUCKY and TITANIC are 20% larger, and a larger ship is easier to hit with a rocket. The Hangar marks a design with a DESIGN badge, and other pilots see its name in the Target window and the kill feed.

## Shop, Auction and points

A design is never sold in the Shop and never listed on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md): you make it from your own ship. The Shop's Ships category does show the designs of each ship under its card (a picture, what the design changes and the conversion price in Thulium), for information; the conversion itself is made in Assembly. A kill of a design pays the same PvP points as a kill of its ship.
