# Statistics

The **Stats** page (**Community › Stats**) is your record. It shows what you did in the wipe you are flying now, what you did in all your wipes together, what you did in each wipe that has ended, and, in its **Logbook**, a diary of what happened to your pilot. The sections sit in a bar along the top: **Overview**, **Logbook**, **Ranking**, **Hall of Fame** and **Sector Intel**. The button at the right end of the bar fetches the section again.

## This wipe, Total and Past wipes

Overview, Ranking and Sector Intel have a switch with three choices:

- **This wipe** is what you did since the current [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) began. The page opens on it.
- **Total** is what you did in all your wipes together, since you started to play.
- **Past wipes** is a list of the wipes that have ended, newest first. Pick one and the page shows what you did in that wipe.

A line beside the switch says which wipe is running, its day and when it began. Every tile also shows the other number on its second line (**Total 61,200** under a wipe number, **This wipe +5,100** under a total), so nothing hides behind a click. Your choice is remembered. Where the three buttons do not fit (a narrow window, a long word in your language) the switch becomes a menu with the same three choices.

The game does not keep a second set of counters for the wipe. At the start of every wipe it writes down your figures as they stand; **This wipe** is your figures now less those. Nothing extra is written when you shoot, fly or destroy something, and your totals are never reset, so the two numbers always agree.

| | This wipe | Total |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level** | the levels you gained (**+2**, with **Level 14 now** under it) | your level |
| **Experience** | the experience you gained | your experience |
| **Honor** | the honor you earned (below) | your honor now |
| **PvE and PvP rank points** | the points you hold now | the points you hold now; the second line shows your best result in an earlier wipe, when you have one (**Best 21,300 (wipe 2)**) |
| **Distance flown**, **Shots fired** | what you flew and fired in this wipe | all of it |
| **Missions** | the missions you finished in this wipe | all you have finished |
| **Kills**, **Losses**, **Ammo usage** | two numbers on every line, **This wipe** and **Total** | |

The two points tiles always show the points you hold now. How rank points restart at a wipe is told in [Ranks](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ranks.md) and the [Wipe Timeline](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md).

## Honor

Honor can go down: friendly fire takes some, changing company halves it, and an administrator can set it. **This wipe** counts only the honor you **earned**. What was taken away from you is added back before the number is shown, so a loss never hides what you earned, and honor that someone set to a lower number does not count as earned. After 150 honor lost to friendly fire you have still earned everything you earned. The **Total** number is your honor as it stands.

## Past wipes

Pick a wipe from the list (**Wipe 2 (2026-10-01 – 2026-10-24)**) and the tiles and lists show what you did in it. Above them a line gives the result: your place in your company, your rank and your PvE and PvP points. **Open the logbook for this wipe** opens your Logbook on that wipe, and **Sector intel for this wipe** shows its sector table. Before your first wipe has ended the page says so and keeps your results for when it does.

- **Counted from a date.** Statistics for each wipe began with game version 0.4.26. For a wipe that was already running then, the game could not know your figures at its start, so it counts from the day the server was updated (or from the record the wipe itself left, where there is one) and the page says **counted from** that day. Sometimes only the **Missions** tile carries the mark: the game knew every figure of the wipe's start but not how many missions you had finished, so it counts those from that day and the rest is exact.
- **Test wipe.** A wipe that an administrator ran by hand on a test server is marked **(test wipe)**.
- **Your results are kept** for as long as you have your account.

## Sector Intel

Sector Intel lists, sector by sector, the distance you flew, the shots you fired and the aliens you destroyed and the times you were destroyed there. It follows the same switch as the Overview. The sector table of **Total** counts every kill you ever made in a sector, so it holds more kills than the **Kills** list of **This wipe**; put the two on the same choice to compare them. The wipe's per-alien split inside a sector is not kept: a past wipe lists the kills of a sector as one number.

## The Hall of Fame by wipe

The Hall of Fame (here and on the Rankings page) lists the best fifty pilots of each board (PvE and PvP) of every world, and of all worlds together. Beside the board and world switches a **Wipe** menu picks the list: **Now**, or a wipe that has ended (**Wipe 2**, **Wipe 1** ...), as it stood at its end. A server that keeps an all-time PvE list also offers **All time**. The lists of past wipes are kept for good. If a pilot has deleted his account, his place stays and the row reads **Deleted pilot**; every other row opens the pilot's profile.

## Your profile

A pilot's profile, which every signed-in pilot can open, shows under the kills tiles how many aliens and pilots he has destroyed **in this wipe**, and a strip with his result in each wipe that has ended (**W2 #41 PvE · Captain**: his place on the PvE list of all pilots, and his rank). That is all the profile says of past wipes. Your deaths, shots, distance flown, sectors, time played per wipe and honor lost are private: only you see them.

## The Logbook

The Logbook is your pilot's private diary: a list of what happened, newest first, in your own language. **It is not a chat log: chat is never recorded.**

### What it records

Each entry belongs to one of six groups, and the chips above the list filter by them (several at once; **All** clears the choice; every chip shows how many entries it holds):

- **Combat**: aliens destroyed, being destroyed (by what or by whom), pilots you destroyed, friendly fire, a first kill of a boss or a Warden.
- **Economy**: what you bought in the Shop, the subscriptions you took, crafting, the Forge, boosters, the Auction (sold, bought, won), bonus codes and Invite Friends.
- **Mining**: the asteroids you broke and the chunks you collected.
- **Progress**: a new level, a new rank, a finished mission, a Skylab module built, a P.E.T. hatched, a ship or a drone formation bought.
- **Rewards**: containers opened, the daily reward, special items used, achievements, titles, a linked Steam account, season prizes and gifts from support.
- **System**: that you joined the game, that the Logbook began, when a wipe began and ended, and what happened in your clan (joined, left, a new role).

The kills and the mining are **folded into one entry for each hour of play**: **Destroyed 143 aliens: Seeker ×90, Phantasm ×40, Bulwark ×13**. A click on the entry unfolds the credits, Thulium, experience and honor of that hour (for mining, the rocks by kind). Everything else is one entry for one thing. Another pilot in a line (the one who destroyed you) is stored as a number and shown by his name as it is now; when his account is gone the line says **a pilot**.

### How long entries are kept

- **Routine entries** (kills, mining, purchases, deaths and so on) are kept for **21 days**, and at most the newest **400**.
- **Milestones** (a new level, a rank, a finished mission, a wipe, a prize and the like) are kept for **a year (365 days)**, and at most the newest **500**.

Your journal begins on the day the Logbook is switched on for the server. Nothing from before is added, and the first entry says that the Logbook began.

### Who can read it

Only you. The game server reads your journal for you when you open the page, and no other pilot can ask for it. Support can read one pilot's journal only when that pilot asks for help, and every such reading is recorded like the other things an administrator does. When you delete your account your journal is deleted with it.

### Reading it

- **Wipe** picks the wipe whose entries you see: the current one (the page opens on it), an earlier one, or **All wipes**. A line across the list marks where a wipe began and where it ended (**Wipe 3 began**, **Wipe 2 ended · you placed 41st in your company**), also for a wipe in which you did nothing.
- The search box and **Find** look in the entries' kind and details.
- **Copy page** puts the lines on the page into your clipboard, one to a line, as text you can paste into a message or a table (time, group, sentence).
- The list shows fifteen entries a page with the page buttons under it. In a narrow window each entry becomes a small card.

### When the Logbook is not available

The Logbook is a small service of its own next to the game, and the game never waits for it. Two things can happen, and neither stops the game:

- **The Logbook is not switched on for this server.** The page says so. Nothing is recorded on such a server.
- **The Logbook cannot be reached right now.** The page says to try again in a minute. Your entries are not lost: the game keeps what happens meanwhile and sends it when the service is back. Only a very long outage can cost the oldest routine entries (the milestones are kept first).

## Questions

**Where did my kills go after the wipe?** They did not go. The Stats page opens on **This wipe**, which starts at nothing in every new wipe, and **Total** is next to it on every tile and in every list. Your totals are never reset.

**Why does This wipe show more honor than my honor went up by?** Because **Total** is your balance, and honor that was taken from you (friendly fire, the halving when you change company) lowered it. **This wipe** counts what you earned (see [Honor](#honor)), so it is the bigger number.

**Why can a sector show more kills than my Kills list?** Because the sector table on **Total** adds up every wipe and the Kills list shows **This wipe**. Set both to the same choice.

**Why does a wipe say counted from a date?** See [Past wipes](#past-wipes): the statistics per wipe began with version 0.4.26.

**Can other pilots read my Logbook?** No. See [Who can read it](#who-can-read-it).

**Why does my Logbook not go back further than three weeks?** Routine entries are kept for 21 days. The milestones of the year are there: pick **All wipes** and the **Progress** or **System** chips.

## Good to know

- The figures of **Past wipes** and the Hall of Fame of a wipe are written when that wipe ends and do not change afterwards.
- The Stats page of a server from before version 0.4.26 shows no switch and no past wipes; the Logbook there is the plain table of earlier versions.
- [Ranks](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ranks.md) says how the rank points and the company ladder work; the [Wipe Timeline](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) says what a wipe takes and keeps.
