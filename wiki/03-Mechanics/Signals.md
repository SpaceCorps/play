# The Signals

<!-- wiki-search: signal; signals; the static; static; dormant keycard; keycard; purple keycard; signal quests; signal 20; the last signal; code; 20-letter code -->

Mission Control picks up a strange noise, **the Static**, and it sends twenty missions: the **Signals**. Each one is a small mystery, and the point of all of them is one thing: the **Dormant Keycard**. Claim the last Signal and the card is yours.

## The Dormant Keycard

- **Where it comes from.** Claiming the **20th and last Signal** gives it.
- **A purple card.** It is an Epic item, so it shows in purple. It sits in your Hangar with your resources.
- **Infinite uses.** Nothing uses it up: it is not spent, it does not wear out, and no recipe asks for it.
- **Only one.** You can carry a single Dormant Keycard. If a claim would give you a second, the claim still works and pays everything else; you just do not get another card.
- **Yours for good.** It cannot be sold on the Auction, it cannot be thrown away in the Hangar, and a [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) does not take it.
- **Not used yet.** It opens nothing today. A later update will use it to give access to a new map, which is not in the game yet. Keep your card until then.
- **Already claimed the last Signal?** A pilot who had claimed it before the card existed (the 0.5.10 update) got the card once, by itself, when the server updated.

## How the Signals Work

- **Start at Mission Control.** From level 5, Signal 1 waits there. Claim it and the next one opens. They come one at a time.
- **One letter each.** Every Signal you claim shows you **one letter** of a personal 20-letter code.
- **Your code is yours alone.** It is different for every pilot, so a friend's letters or answers will not help you.
- **Out of order.** The letters do not arrive in the order of the code. Keep track of what you find and piece it together.
- **The code box.** Claim all twenty, then type your code into the Bonus code card on the Thulium page. Something is waiting for the pilots who get there.
- **The pay.** Every Signal pays experience, Credits, Thulium and honor like a mission, and **10 Orvium Reinforced Plates** on top: 200 in all.

## Good to Know

- **Never repeated.** A Signal pays once, for ever. A claimed Signal stays claimed after a wipe, and so do your letters and your card.
- **Not every Signal is a kill count.** Expect to look, count, wait and think.
- **Some open later.** A few open only later in the season, and at least one needs a clan.
- **Keep the answers to yourself.** Give friendly hints and let other pilots find it for themselves.
