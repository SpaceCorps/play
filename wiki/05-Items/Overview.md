# Items Overview

Items are the core components that upgrade your ship's capabilities. They can be purchased from the Shop, found as drops, crafted in Assembly, or merged through Fusion.

## Rarity Levels

Items come in different rarities, which determine their quality and base stats:

- **Shoddy**: Basic starter equipment.
- **Common**: Standard military grade.
- **Rare**: High-performance gear.
- **Epic**: Advanced technology.
- **Mythical**: Legendary experimental prototypes.
- **Legendary**: Rare and powerful relic modules.
- **Eternal**: Cosmic tier modules with unrivaled power.

## Item Enchants

When you craft an item in the Assembly, it has a chance to receive an **Enchant**. Enchants modify the base parameters of an item dynamically with random multiplier bonuses. There are 5 Enchant tiers:

1. **Standard** (Default, 90% chance): No extra stat modifications.
2. **Tainted** (10% craft chance): Up to 1 parameter modified (up to +2.0% bonus).
3. **Godly** (1% craft chance): Up to 2 parameters modified (up to +3.5% bonus).
4. **Rupturing** (0.1% craft chance): Up to 3 parameters modified (up to +5.0% bonus).
5. **Eternal** (0.01% craft chance): Up to 4 parameters modified (up to +7.5% bonus).

Enchanted stats are displayed on the item icon grid card (e.g. `[T]` for Tainted, `[G]` for Godly) and outlined in the item's statistics tooltip.

## Item Fusion (Merging)

Players can merge items to improve their enchants through the **Fusion** page in the main menu:

- **Requirements**: You must select exactly **5 items** of the same item definition (e.g. 5x Light Shield Core) and the **same enchant tier** (e.g. 5x Tainted).
- **Free Fusion**: Merging is completely free (consumes the 4 extra items, but has no credit or thulium cost).
- **Upgrade Chance**: Fusion has a **25% chance to upgrade** the resulting item's enchant to the next tier (e.g. Tainted -> Godly), and a **75% chance** to keep it at the same tier.
- **Stat Inheritance**: The resulting item is guaranteed to keep the **best stats** from all 5 merged items. If multiple items have the same stat bonus, the highest multiplier is selected.
- **Parameter Capping**: If the resulting tier has a lower parameter limit than the number of unique inherited stats, a random subset of those enchants is selected.
- **Upgrade Rolls**: If the item upgrades to a higher tier, new stat bonuses are rolled. For each stat, the maximum of the inherited merged stat and the new roll is kept.

## Currencies

- **Credits**: Standard currency used for basic items. Earned by killing aliens and completing [quests](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Quests.md); each level's Special mission also pays items (lasers, shield parts, Power Cores, Ship Fragments).
- **Thulium**: Premium resource used for high-end equipment. Rare drop from bosses or purchased.

## Categories

- **Lasers**: Your primary weapon systems.
- **Shields**: Generators and cells for defense.
- **Propulsion**: Engines and thrusters for speed.
