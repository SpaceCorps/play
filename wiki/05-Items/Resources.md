# Resources

A **resource** here is anything the game hands you, collects for you and asks back: the **materials** (items of the Resource kind, which you build and forge with) and the two **currencies**, Credits and Thulium. This page says, for each one, what it is, how to get it, what it is for and how to farm it best.

- **Materials** are items in your Hangar inventory: they drop from aliens as [cargo crates](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md), come as mission rewards, or are made in the [Skylab](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md). They are spent in Assembly recipes, in the [Forge](/wiki/05-Items/Forge.md) and on Skylab builds.
- **Currencies** live in your account. Every alien pays them, and so do missions and the Skylab's farms.
- **Not on this page**: ammunition ([Lasers](/wiki/05-Items/Lasers.md), [Rockets](/wiki/05-Items/Rockets.md)), equipment and boosters keep their own pages. Experience and Honor are scores, not money: they are in the alien table below.

Where a number says "sector", it is the sector of your company's map (1 is next to your base, 4 is the border). The figures are the game's own data, taken straight from it, so they follow when a drop, a recipe or a rate changes.

Every yield in this page is **per kill, on average**: the chance of each drop times the middle of its amount, added up. It counts kills, not time: a Crystalys takes far longer to kill than a Seeker. It assumes the crate is yours (no Resource Magnet) and no luck (no Loot Luck booster, no Luck Boost from the Season Store: both raise the chance of every drop, the Luck Boost by up to 10 points), and every drop is rolled on its own. The Credits and Thulium figures are those of the Alpha world; the other worlds pay more (see [Currencies](/wiki/05-Items/Resources.md#currencies)).

### Velkonite

*Uncommon resource.* Ore mined by a Velkonite collector in your Skylab and kept in the Resource storage. The Forgery makes it into Velkonite Reinforced Plates.

**How to get it**

- **Skylab**: only the Velkonite Collector mines it, into a hopper of 72 hours, then Collect moves it to the Resource Storage. Aliens do not drop it.

| Module level | 1 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
| :--- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| Velkonite an hour | 12 | 29 | 89 | 273 | 833 |

These are the rates of a powered station: a power deficit stops every farm and collector, and a module cannot be upgraded above the Core, which stops at level 20 (see [Skylab](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md)).

Building the collector takes Core level 5, 10 Ship Fragments, 10,000 credits and 500 Thulium.

- **Otherwise**: not sold in the Shop.

**What it is for**

- [Skylab](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md) Forgery: **40** for each Velkonite Reinforced Plate at Forgery level 1, 1.5% less with every level above 1 (71.5% of that at level 20)

**How to farm it**: raise the collector's level (each level adds 25% to its hourly rate) and empty the hopper before it fills: it stops at its cap.

- Dark Matter Plate: **1** (with 5 Dark Matter, 1 Velkonite Reinforced Plate, 250 Thulium)

**Module upgrades**: Assembly upgrades a Starfire-3 into a Helios Beam. Each upgrade uses up the piece it starts from and asks for these plates as well as its other materials. The new piece keeps the Forge tier of the piece you put in, and its buffs are rolled again, so they can come out better or worse than the old ones. See [Module upgrades in the Assembly](/wiki/05-Items/Forge.md#module-upgrades-in-the-assembly).

**How to farm it**: feed the Forgery the ore, start a full batch and collect the plates while your ship is landed.

### Dark Matter

*Epic resource.* What a black hole gives back for a N.I.K.E. it swallows, in small crates on the rim of its zone. Assembly presses five of it, with a Velkonite and an Orvium Reinforced Plate, into a Dark Matter Plate.

**How to get it**

- **The black hole**: a [N.I.K.E.](/wiki/05-Items/Rockets.md) rocket that crosses the event horizon of the black hole in the middle of Danger Sector 4 is swallowed, and the hole gives back **1, 2 or 3** Dark Matter (2 on average) in crates of at most 2 on the rim of its zone, 3,050 to 3,950 units from its centre. The crates are yours and your clan's for 60 seconds and last 240 seconds. A N.I.K.E. that meets a ship on its way hits it instead and is used up. See [The Black Hole](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Black-Hole.md#dark-matter).
- **Otherwise**: not sold in the Shop.

**What it is for**

- Dark Matter Plate: **5** (with 1 Orvium Reinforced Plate, 1 Velkonite Reinforced Plate, 250 Thulium)

**How to farm it**: fire [N.I.K.E.](/wiki/05-Items/Rockets.md) rockets into the black hole (Assembly makes them) and take the crates from the rim of its zone before anyone else does.

### Dark Matter Plate

*Mythical resource.* Pressed in Assembly from Dark Matter and two reinforced plates. The Forge asks for two of them for each of its top two tiers.

**How to get it**

- **Assembly**: pressed from 5 Dark Matter, 1 Orvium Reinforced Plate and 1 Velkonite Reinforced Plate, for 250 Thulium and 120 seconds. Aliens do not drop it.
- **Otherwise**: not sold in the Shop.

**What it is for**

- [Forge](/wiki/05-Items/Forge.md), Godly to Rupturing: **2** (with 20 Reinforced Hull Plate, 120 Cataclysite, 200,000 credits; 75% success)
- [Forge](/wiki/05-Items/Forge.md), Rupturing to Eternal: **2** (with 8 Power Core, 240 Quorvium, 500,000 credits, 2,000 Thulium; 60% success)

A Forge step that fails gives 50% of its materials back, rounded down.

**How to farm it**: it is pressed, not found: collect Dark Matter (above) and the two plates the Skylab Forgery makes, then start the craft in Assembly.

## Good to know

- **Crates**: an alien's drops fall in one crate where it blows up, reserved for the pilot who killed it and their clan for 30 seconds. The [Resource Magnet](/wiki/05-Items/Boosters.md) booster adds 25% to what a crate holds. See [Cargo](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md).
- **Wrecks**: a destroyed [company pilot](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Company-Pilots.md) leaves no crate and no parts, whoever or whatever destroys it, so the pilots' ships are no source of materials. You can shoot down the pilots of another company once the Peace Protocol is over, where your [world](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#worlds-alpha-beta-and-gamma) allows PvP; destroying one of your own company's pilots costs 100 Honor.
- **Module upgrades**: Assembly makes the Starfire-3, Helios Beam, Nova Amp, Apex Amp, Sovereign Shield Cell, Plasma Thruster, Heavy Shield Core, Engine III and Thruster III by upgrading the piece one step below, which it uses up. An upgrade asks for Velkonite Reinforced Plates and Orvium Reinforced Plates, as the Quantum Laser 3 and Dark Matter Plate do. The new piece keeps the Forge tier of the piece you put in, and its buffs are rolled again, so they can come out better or worse than the old ones. See [Module upgrades in the Assembly](/wiki/05-Items/Forge.md#module-upgrades-in-the-assembly).
- **Where they are kept**: loose stacks in the inventory are what Assembly, the Forge and Skylab builds use. Stacks on a ship or in the Transport Cache are not.
- **The wipe**: materials in your inventory follow the [wipe rules](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md); ore banked in the Skylab's Resource Storage stays.
