# Propulsion & Speed

Propulsion systems determine your ship's movement velocity and maneuverability.

## Engines

Engines are the primary source of thrust for your ship. An engine in an **ability slot** instead gives you the **Afterburner** in the last column, a burst of speed for ten seconds (longer with more engines), and adds no thrust of its own (see [Abilities](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Abilities.md)).

| Name | Rarity | Base Speed | Speed Bonus % | Shield Bonus % | Slots | Special Effect | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Engine I** | Shoddy | +2 | +2% | -2% | 1 | Afterburner I | 20,000 Credits |
| **Engine II** | Common | +4 | +4% | -8% | 2 | Afterburner II | 2,000 Thulium |
| **Engine III** | Rare | +6 | +5% | -15% | 3 | Afterburner III | Craftable Only |

The engines' Shield Bonus is in the item data, but the game has never applied it: engines don't weaken your shields, and the item cards leave it out.

## Thrusters

Thrusters are nested inside Engines or Adaptive Cores to boost their speed output:

| Name | Rarity | Flat Speed Boost | Speed Multiplier | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Thruster I** | Shoddy | +5 | 1.00x | 20,000 Credits |
| **Vector Thruster** | Uncommon | +7 | 1.02x | 80,000 Credits |
| **Thruster II** | Common | +10 | 1.05x | 2,000 Thulium |
| **Ion Thruster** | Rare | +13 | 1.08x | 3,000 Thulium |
| **Thruster III** | Rare | +15 | 1.10x | Craftable Only |
| **Plasma Thruster** | Epic | +18 | 1.12x | Craftable Only |

The Plasma Thruster is made in [Assembly](/wiki/05-Items/Overview.md#upgrading-modules) from an Ion Thruster, with Thulium, drops and 6 Velkonite Reinforced Plates from your Skylab. It keeps the enchant tier of the thruster it uses up, and its buffs are rolled again ([Module upgrades](/wiki/05-Items/Forge.md#module-upgrades-in-the-assembly)). Thrusters don't fit an [ability slot](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Abilities.md); they belong inside engines and Adaptive Cores.

### Outrunning aliens

The aliens fly at 120 (Seeker), 160 (Phantasm), 175 (Bulwark), 180 (Gorvane) and 230 (Crystalys). An Ostirion with one Engine II and two thrusters flies at 222.6 with Thruster I and 226.9 with Vector Thrusters: still under the Crystalys, so only Thulium thrusters (233.4 with Thruster II, 239.9 with Ion Thrusters, 250.7 with Plasma Thrusters) outrun it.
