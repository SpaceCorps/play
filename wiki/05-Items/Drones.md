# Drones

Drones are purchasable or craftable support units. You can have up to **8 Drones** active at once, Slave Drones and Master Drones together.

## Available Drones

| Name             | Rarity | Slots | Description                                                                                  | Cost                             |
| :--------------- | :----- | :---- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------- |
| **Slave Drone**  | Common | 1     | A basic drone with a single equipment slot. It grows through 8 levels as you destroy aliens. | From 100,000 Credits (see below) |
| **Master Drone** | Rare   | 2     | A Slave Drone upgraded in the Assembly. It flies in gold, keeps its number and what it carries, gets a second equipment slot, and starts again at level 1. | Upgrade a Slave Drone: 40,000 Thulium and 100 Ship Fragments |

A Slave Drone starts as a small sphere and grows into an armoured gunship at level 8. Every drone you own earns the same experience whenever you destroy an alien, and a higher level adds a little damage to the laser in its slot (up to +7% at level 8). The Drone Mechanics page (under Mechanics) has the levels and what each asks for. Crafting a Master Drone does not use up a drone: it upgrades the Slave Drone you pick, in place, and **its level and experience are reset to 0** when the upgrade finishes (the Assembly says so and asks you to confirm). In return the drone has **two equipment slots** instead of one: what it carried stays in its first slot and the second is empty. Your drones, with their levels and experience, are kept through the season wipe.

## Slave Drone prices

Every Slave Drone you buy costs more than the last. The price depends on how many drones you hold when you buy (a Master Drone counts as one), and the Shop always shows the price of your next one. The first three cost Credits only; from the fourth on, Thulium is added.

| Drone | Credits    | Thulium |
| :---- | :--------- | :------ |
| 1st   | 100,000    | –       |
| 2nd   | 200,000    | –       |
| 3rd   | 400,000    | –       |
| 4th   | 800,000    | 10,000  |
| 5th   | 1,600,000  | 20,000  |
| 6th   | 3,200,000  | 30,000  |
| 7th   | 6,400,000  | 40,000  |
| 8th   | 12,800,000 | 50,000  |

All eight together cost 25,500,000 Credits and 150,000 Thulium. Your drones stay through the season wipe, so the price goes on from the number you hold: with three drones your next one is always the 4th, and with eight there is none left to buy.

## Usage

1. **Buy** Slave Drones from the Shop. **Upgrade** one into a Master Drone in the Assembly when you want the gold one (its level and XP start again).
2. **Equip** them in the Hangar under the "Drones" tab.
3. **Load** them with Lasers or Shields to boost your power: a Slave Drone has one slot, a Master Drone two.
4. **Level** them up by destroying aliens: the Hangar shows each drone's level and how much experience the next one asks for.
