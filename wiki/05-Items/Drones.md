# Drones

Drones are purchasable or craftable support units. You can equip up to **8 Drones** active at once.

## Available Drones

| Name             | Rarity | Slots | Description                                 | Cost            |
| :--------------- | :----- | :---- | :------------------------------------------ | :-------------- |
| **Slave Drone**  | Common | 1     | A basic drone with a single equipment slot. | 100,000 Credits |
| **Master Drone** | Rare   | 2     | An advanced drone with two equipment slots. | _Loot / Event_  |

## Usage

1. **Buy/Obtain** drones from the Shop or via loot.
2. **Equip** them in the Hangar under the "Drones" tab.
3. **Load** them with Lasers or Shields to boost your power.
