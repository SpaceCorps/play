# Crystalys

Crystalys is a massive battleship-class alien entity. Encountering one is highly dangerous, requiring exceptional shield capacity and firepower to survive.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 256,000
- **Shield**: 160,000
- **Damage**: 10,000
- **Speed**: 230
- **Attack Range**: 900
- **Behavior**: Aggressive

## Rewards

- **Credits**: 60,000
- **Thulium**: 200
- **Experience (XP)**: 12,000
- **Honor**: 52
- **Shield Recharge**: 500 per second (5s delay)

## Loot Drops

Dropped as a [cargo crate](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) where it blows up, the killer's for 30 seconds.

- **Ship Fragment**:
  - 100% chance (Min: 5, Max: 5)
  - 50% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)
  - 25% chance (Min: 2, Max: 2)
- **Cataclysite**: 100% chance (Min: 8, Max: 8)
- **Power Core**: 50% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)
- **Ancient Control Unit**: 20% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)

## Lore

The Crystalys is a legendary, battleship-class alien dreadnought. Formed from super-dense crystalline lattices, its energy absorption is unparalleled, allowing it to field shields that recover rapidly from damage. A Crystalys behaves with extreme aggression, obliterating target ships in seconds. Approaching one without a fully-kitted battleship is considered suicide.
