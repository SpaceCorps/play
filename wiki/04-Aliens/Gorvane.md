# Gorvane

Gorvanes are formidable cruiser-class alien vessels. They possess high shield capacity and deal massive damage.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 64,000
- **Shield**: 48,000
- **Damage**: 4,000
- **Speed**: 200
- **Attack Range**: 800
- **Behavior**: Aggressive

## Rewards

- **Credits**: 12,000
- **Thulium**: 75
- **Experience (XP)**: 3,000
- **Honor**: 24
- **Shield Recharge**: 100 per second (5s delay)

## Loot Drops

Dropped as a [cargo crate](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) where it blows up, the killer's for 30 seconds.

- **Ship Fragment**:
  - 100% chance (Min: 3, Max: 3)
  - 25% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)
- **Cataclysite**: 100% chance (Min: 4, Max: 4)
- **Reinforced Hull Plate**: 60% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)
- **Power Core**: 25% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)

## Lore

A formidable cruiser-class vessel within the alien hierarchy, the Gorvane (a designation given by early SpaceCorps cartographers, who knew it by its distinct domed crest) is a frontline warship. Equipped with heavy shield generators and a massive plasma discharge beam, it poses a severe threat to standard sector patrols.
