# Seeker

Seekers are basic scouting and reconnaissance units. They are passive, meaning they will not attack unless provoked first.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 800
- **Shield**: 800
- **Damage**: 200
- **Speed**: 120
- **Attack Range**: 600
- **Behavior**: Passive

## Rewards

- **Credits**: 800
- **Thulium**: 4
- **Experience (XP)**: 100
- **Honor**: 2
- **Shield Recharge**: 10 per second (5s delay)

## Loot Drops

Dropped as a [cargo crate](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) where it blows up, the killer's for 30 seconds.

- **Ship Fragment**: 20% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)

## Lore

Seekers are light scouting probes deployed by the alien Swarm to map sector jumpgates and trace electromagnetic signatures of human fleets. Possessing minimal armaments and fragile structures, they are highly passive, retreating or ignoring vessels unless fired upon. However, they coordinate with larger combat units, signaling their positions if engaged.
