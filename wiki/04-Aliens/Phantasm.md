# Phantasm

Phantasms are aggressive spectral-class drones. They will attack any player who enters their aggro radius (600 units).

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 1,600
- **Shield**: 3,200
- **Damage**: 400
- **Speed**: 140
- **Attack Range**: 700
- **Behavior**: Aggressive

## Rewards

- **Credits**: 2,400
- **Thulium**: 12
- **Experience (XP)**: 300
- **Honor**: 6
- **Shield Recharge**: 20 per second (5s delay)

## Loot Drops

Dropped as a [cargo crate](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) where it blows up, the killer's for 30 seconds.

- **Ship Fragment**: 100% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)

## Lore

A bizarre manifestation of dark-matter energy, the Phantasm is a spectral combat drone that hovers silently in asteroid fields. It operates with a simple, highly aggressive defense routine, attacking any ship that wanders into its proximity. Sensors struggle to lock onto its shifting, non-euclidean geometry, making it a frustrating target to hit.
