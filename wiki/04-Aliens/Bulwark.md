# Bulwark

Bulwarks are heavily armored defensive platforms. They are highly aggressive and pack a punch.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 16,000
- **Shield**: 16,000
- **Damage**: 1,200
- **Speed**: 170
- **Attack Range**: 700
- **Behavior**: Aggressive

## Rewards

- **Credits**: 4,000
- **Thulium**: 25
- **Experience (XP)**: 800
- **Honor**: 10
- **Shield Recharge**: 50 per second (5s delay)

## Loot Drops

Dropped as a [cargo crate](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md) where it blows up, the killer's for 30 seconds.

- **Ship Fragment**: 100% chance (Min: 2, Max: 2)
- **Cataclysite**: 100% chance (Min: 2, Max: 2)
- **Reinforced Hull Plate**: 30% chance (Min: 1, Max: 1)

## Lore

Serving as heavy guard platforms for Swarm nests, Bulwarks are armored monstrosities designed to absorb immense punishment. Their hulls are reinforced with dense crystal composites that react dynamically to energy weapons. Bulwarks are highly aggressive and will relentlessly hunt down anything invading their defensive perimeter.
