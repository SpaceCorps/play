## Lore

The Protos is the legendary starter chassis issued to all newly commissioned SpaceCorps academy graduates. While basic in its stock configuration, its highly modular design has made it a favorite among tinkerers and salvage operators. Many legendary captains began their journeys in the humble, trusty cockpit of a Protos.
