# Protos

The Protos is the basic starter ship given to all new pilots in SpaceCorps. It is lightweight but highly upgradeable for beginner operations.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 8,000
- **Base Speed**: 150
- **Laser Slots**: 2
- **Extra Slots**: 3

### Generator & Support Slots

- **Core Slots (100% efficiency)**: 2
- **Support Slots (75% efficiency)**: 2
- **Auxiliary Slots (50% efficiency)**: 0

---

## Price

- **Credits**: 0 (Starter Ship)
- **Thulium**: 0

## Lore

The Protos is the legendary starter chassis issued to all newly commissioned SpaceCorps academy graduates. While basic in its stock configuration, its highly modular design has made it a favorite among tinkerers and salvage operators. Many legendary captains began their journeys in the humble, trusty cockpit of a Protos.
