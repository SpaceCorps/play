## Lore

A product of classified black-ops research, the Wraith battleship represents the pinnacle of dreadnought engineering. Its dark-matter drive and dense energy grids allow it to field multiple high-power laser banks simultaneously. Extremely rare and expensive, the sight of a Wraith on the sensors is enough to make even the most seasoned pirate captains turn back.
