## Lore

The Paragon combat cruiser is the workhorse of the sector patrol fleets. Engineered for endurance, it features a heavy titanium-shielded hull and advanced weapon mounting arrays. Its multi-nozzle engine configuration allows it to maintain exceptional stability under fire, making it a reliable bulwark against alien incursions.
