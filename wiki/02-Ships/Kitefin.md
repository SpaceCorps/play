## Lore

The Kitefin began as a courier airframe for the supply runs between company stations: a flat kite of a hull, cheap to fuel and easy to patch. When the Swarm turned every courier lane into a front line, the yards bolted a cannon under each wing and a third under the nose, tripled the plating and sold the result to pilots who had outgrown their Protos but could not yet afford an Ostirion. Its forked tail, coral-tipped prongs and warm canopy light are a common sight on escort duty, where its three guns keep pace with far bigger ships and its thin hull teaches pilots to mind their shields.
