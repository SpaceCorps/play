# Kitefin

The Kitefin is a light gunship and the first ship most pilots buy: a third laser, twice the hull of the Protos and a quicker drive, for under a third of an Ostirion's price.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 16,000
- **Base Speed**: 175
- **Laser Slots**: 3
- **Extra Slots**: 3

### Generator & Support Slots

- **Core Slots (100% efficiency)**: 2
- **Support Slots (75% efficiency)**: 3
- **Auxiliary Slots (50% efficiency)**: 0

## Price

- **Credits**: 200,000
- **Thulium**: 0

## Lore

The Kitefin began as a courier airframe for the supply runs between company stations: a flat kite of a hull, cheap to fuel and easy to patch. When the Swarm turned every courier lane into a front line, the yards bolted a cannon under each wing and a third under the nose, doubled the plating and sold the result to pilots who had outgrown their Protos but could not yet afford an Ostirion. Its forked tail, coral-tipped prongs and warm canopy light are a common sight on escort duty, where its three guns keep pace with far bigger ships and its thin hull teaches pilots to mind their shields.
