# Ironclad

The Ironclad is a heavy armored tank: the thickest hull of any ship and the most room for shields, bought with fewer guns and a slower drive. It is made in Assembly, between the Paragon and the Wraith.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 600,000
- **Base Speed**: 92
- **Laser Slots**: 6
- **Extra Slots**: 3

### Generator & Support Slots

## Lore

The Ironclad began as a siege hull: a plated wedge built to sit in front of the station guns and take whatever the Swarm threw at it. The yards kept the armor and made it thicker, gave it seven core generator slots and 85% more hull than the Wraith, hung a pair of oversized engine nacelles on the back to move the weight, and cut the guns to six to pay for it. It is not built to outrun anything and it does not need to. Pilots park it between the Swarm and whatever they want to protect and let the teal plating do the work. The cream armor slabs are replaced after every sortie; the orange strips along the wings are the only part of it that was ever meant to be seen.
