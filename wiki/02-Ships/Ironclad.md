# Ironclad

The Ironclad is a heavy armored tank: the thickest hull of any ship and the most room for shields, bought with fewer guns and a slower drive. It is made in Assembly, between the Paragon and the Wraith.

## Stats

- **Hitpoints (HP)**: 600,000
- **Base Speed**: 92
- **Laser Slots**: 6
- **Extra Slots**: 3
- **Hull Plate Slots**: 15

### Generator & Support Slots

- **Core Slots (100% efficiency)**: 7
- **Support Slots (75% efficiency)**: 4
- **Auxiliary Slots (50% efficiency)**: 3

## Research

- **Hull plate slots.** The Ironclad has 15 [hull plate slots](/wiki/06-Items/Hull-Plating.md#hull-plate-slots) for [Hull Plating](/wiki/06-Items/Hull-Plating.md), all locked when you make it. Each one is a technology of its own in the Skylab, 1 h and 10 Dark Matter, researched in order from the first; it opens that slot on the Ironclad and on every one of its designs. All 15 filled with Hull Plating III add 225,000 hull.
- **Designs.** The Ironclad has 2 [designs](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ship-Designs.md). Research one (10 h and 10 Dark Matter), then convert your Ironclad into it in Assembly: the change cannot be undone.

## Lore

The Ironclad began as a siege hull: a plated wedge built to sit in front of the station guns and take whatever the Swarm threw at it. The yards kept the armor and made it thicker, gave it seven core generator slots and 85% more hull than the Wraith, hung a pair of oversized engine nacelles on the back to move the weight, and cut the guns to six to pay for it. It is not built to outrun anything and it does not need to. Pilots park it between the Swarm and whatever they want to protect and let the dark grey plating do the work. The pale armor slabs are replaced after every sortie; the light strips along the wings are the only part of it that was ever meant to be seen.
