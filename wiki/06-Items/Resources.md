# Resources

A **resource** here is anything the game hands you, collects for you and asks back: the **materials** (items of the Resource kind, which you build and forge with) and the two **currencies**, Credits and Thulium. This page says, for each one, what it is, how to get it, what it is for and how to farm it best.

- **Materials** are items in your Hangar inventory: they drop from aliens as [cargo crates](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md), come as mission rewards, or are made in the [Skylab](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md). They are spent in Assembly recipes, in the [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md) and on Skylab builds.
- **Currencies** live in your account. Every alien pays them, and so do missions and the Skylab's farms.
- **Not on this page**: ammunition ([Lasers](/wiki/06-Items/Lasers.md), [Rockets](/wiki/06-Items/Rockets.md)), equipment and boosters keep their own pages. Experience and Honor are scores, not money: they are in the alien table below.
- **Selling**: what you earned of them can be sold on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md#marketable-items), except the Ancient Control Unit, the ores (Velkonite and Orvium) and the Dark Matter Plate. A Reinforced Plate can be sold when a mission paid it, not when the Forgery made it.

Where a number says "sector", it is the sector of your company's map (1 is next to your base, 4 is the border). The figures are the game's own data, taken straight from it, so they follow when a drop, a recipe or a rate changes.

Every yield in this page is **per kill, on average**: the chance of each drop times the middle of its amount, added up. It counts kills, not time: a Crystalys takes far longer to kill than a Seeker. It assumes the crate is yours (no Resource Magnet Booster) and no luck (no Loot Luck Booster, no Luck Boost from the Season Store: both raise the chance of every drop, the Luck Boost by up to 10 points), and every drop is rolled on its own. The Credits and Thulium figures are those of the Alpha world; the other worlds pay more (see [Currencies](/wiki/06-Items/Resources.md#currencies)).

Experience and Honor are scores, not money: nothing is bought with them. Experience raises your level, which counts towards your rank; Honor plays no part in your rank. Only changing company takes any of it, and only half of your Honor; your Experience is never spent.

## Good to know

- **Crates**: an alien's drops fall in one crate where it blows up, reserved for the pilot who killed it and their clan for 30 seconds. The [Resource Magnet Booster](/wiki/06-Items/Boosters.md) adds 25% to what a crate holds. See [Cargo](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md).
- **Wrecks**: a destroyed [company pilot](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Company-Pilots.md) leaves no crate and no parts, whoever or whatever destroys it, so the pilots' ships are no source of materials. You can shoot down the pilots of another company once the Peace Protocol is over, where your [world](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#worlds-alpha-beta-and-gamma) allows PvP; destroying one of your own company's pilots costs 100 Honor.
- **Module upgrades**: Assembly makes the Starfire-3, Helios Beam, Damage Amp IV, Crit Amp IV, Absorption Shield Cell IV, Impulse Thruster IV, Heavy Shield Core, Engine III, Impulse Thruster III, Absorption Shield Cell II, Absorption Shield Cell III, Capacity Shield Cell II, Capacity Shield Cell III, Capacity Shield Cell IV, Impulse Thruster II, Momentum Thruster II, Momentum Thruster III, Momentum Thruster IV, Damage Amp II, Damage Amp III, Crit Amp II, Crit Amp III, Penetration Amp II, Penetration Amp III and Penetration Amp IV by upgrading the piece one step below, which it uses up. An upgrade asks for Velkonite Reinforced Plates, Dark Matter Plates and Orvium Reinforced Plates, as the Quantum Laser 3, Dark Matter Plate, Extra Slots CPU I, Extra Slots CPU II, Extra Slots CPU III, Jump CPU, Base CPU I, Base CPU II, Auto-Repair CPU, Testudo Formation, Bodkin Formation, Asterism Formation, Gemini Formation, Adamant Formation, Ballista Formation, Stiletto Formation, Rampart Formation, Sanctum Formation, Shrike Formation, Culler Formation, Redoubt Formation, Auger Formation, Cordon Formation, Centurion Formation and Gyre Formation do. The new piece keeps the Forge tier of the piece you put in, and its buffs are rolled again, so they can come out better or worse than the old ones. See [Module upgrades in the Assembly](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md#module-upgrades-in-the-assembly).
- **Where they are kept**: loose stacks in the inventory are what Assembly, the Forge and Skylab builds use. Stacks on a ship or in the Transport Cache are not.
- **The wipe**: materials in your inventory follow the [wipe rules](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md); ore banked in the Skylab's Resource Storage stays.
