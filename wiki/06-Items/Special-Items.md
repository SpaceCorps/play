# Special Items

<!-- wiki-search: special items; special item; consumable; consumables; magic hammer; hammer; schematics; philosopher's stone; philosophers stone; stone; tinkerer's badge; badge; forger's seal; seal; workforce contract; contract; foreman; engineer; hauler; worker; workers -->

**Special items** are six one-use items that save you time in the [Skylab](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md), the [Research Centre](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Research.md), the [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md) and the Production Queue of [Assembly](/wiki/06-Items/Overview.md#the-production-queue). You use one on something you already have, it does its job once, and it is gone. They are not items in the usual sense: your account holds a **count** of each kind, so they are not carried, not dropped and not deleted by a wipe, and none of them can be traded.

> [!NOTE]
> **Nothing gives them out yet.** For now an admin issues every special item. They can't be bought, sold, listed on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md) or given to another pilot, and a Magic Hammer is extremely rare.

## In one minute

- **Six kinds.** The Magic Hammer, Schematics, the Philosopher's Stone, the Tinkerer's Badge, the Forger's Seal and the Workforce Contract. Each is described below with its numbers.
- **Where.** The **Special items** section of the Hangar shows what you hold and has a **Use** button for each. The page the item works on shows a button too, when you hold one: the module of an upgrade in the Skylab, the running research, the Forge's Tier up panel and the Production Queue.
- **It asks first.** Every use opens a sheet that says exactly what will happen and what it uses. Using is final: it can't be undone. If a use is refused, nothing is spent.
- **They stay.** Your special items, and the Philosopher's Stone or the contract that is running, stay through the [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md).
- **Anywhere.** You can use them while you fly: none of them needs your ship landed.
- **What they don't do.** They never pay for you: what an upgrade, a research or a craft cost was paid when it started, and nothing is refunded or charged again.

## The six at a glance

| Item | Rarity | You can hold | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **Magic Hammer** | Legendary | 5 | Finishes one Skylab upgrade at once |
| **Schematics** | Rare | 20 | Finishes the research that is running, if it takes 24 hours or less |
| **Philosopher's Stone** | Epic | 10 | Your Credit Farm and Thulium Farm make twice as much for 10 hours |
| **Tinkerer's Badge** | Epic | 10 | Raises a piece of equipment one Forge tier, from Tainted up |
| **Forger's Seal** | Uncommon | 99 | Skips up to 30 minutes of the Production Queue |
| **Workforce Contract** | Common | 99 | Hires a Foreman, an Engineer or a Hauler for 1 hour |

The rarity is the game's usual scale, and **You can hold** is the most of one kind your account keeps: a gift that would pass it is refused whole, not cut.

## The six

### Magic Hammer

Finishes **one Skylab upgrade at once**: pick the module that is being upgraded and the Hammer ends the timer, whether it has 5 minutes or 6 days left. The module is at its new level the same moment, exactly as if the timer had run out at that second; a Core upgrade builds its bridge or its next Core as usual.

- **The price stays paid.** What the upgrade cost was taken when you started it. The Hammer refunds none of it and asks for nothing more.
- **Only a running upgrade.** A module that is not being upgraded, or whose upgrade has just ended by itself, is refused ("Nothing to finish: that module is not being upgraded.") and the Hammer stays with you.
- **Extremely rare.** The Skylab keeps its levels through the wipe, so a Hammer is worth the time of a step, up to 6 days on the Core. The game allows a pilot at most **one in 60 days**, and you can hold 5.
- **The Engineer** (a [Workforce Contract](#workforce-contract)) shortens upgrades by up to 1 hour; the Hammer takes all that is left.

### Schematics

Finishes **the research that is running now**, if the technology takes **24 hours or less** to research. 135 of the 151 technologies of the [Research Centre](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Research.md) qualify. The 16 that take 48 or 72 hours do not: the Wraith, three drone formations, Hull Plating III, levels 4 and 5 of the Engine and Defense Modules and seven Pilot technologies.

- **By research time, not time left.** A 48-hour technology with one hour to go is still refused: Schematics look at how long the technology takes in all.
- **The price stays paid.** The Dark Matter, Credits and Thulium were taken when the research started and are not given back. The science the research has not burned stays in your tank.
- **The research finishes the way a finished one does.** The technology is yours, the notices appear, and the **next technology of your queue starts at once** if it can.
- **Refused** without a Research Centre, with no research running, or when the technology takes longer than 24 hours.
- It never starts a research and never picks one for you.

### Philosopher's Stone {#philosophers-stone}

For **10 hours** of real time your **Credit Farm and Thulium Farm make twice as much**. The 10 hours run on the server's clock, so they pass while you are away or logged out.

- **Only while producing.** The farm must be producing: a farm that is switched off, being upgraded or without power makes nothing in that stretch, and the Stone does not make it up later.
- **The hopper still limits it.** The extra goes into the farm's hopper like any hour of production, and a hopper holds 72 hours. A full hopper stops the farm and wastes the bonus, so collect your farms before they fill; the Skylab page warns you while a Stone runs and a farm is nearly full.
- **One at a time.** A second Stone is refused while one is running. It is also refused if you have built neither farm.
- **The collectors, the Ammo Printer and the Rocket Factory** are not doubled by the Stone: that is the Foreman's job. A Stone and a Foreman on the same farm do not add up: the farm makes twice as much, not four times.

### Tinkerer's Badge {#tinkerers-badge}

Raises **one piece of equipment by one Forge tier**. It **always works** and costs **no Credits, no Thulium and no materials**. It is the [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md)'s Tier up with the dice and the price taken away.

- **From Tainted up.** The piece must already be **Tainted or better** (tier 2 and up of Standard, Tainted, Godly, Rupturing and Eternal). A Standard piece is refused, because the Forge's first step always works anyway.
- **One step.** A Badge on a Tainted piece makes it Godly, on a Godly piece Rupturing, on a Rupturing piece Eternal.
- **The buffs are rolled as in the Forge.** The same ranges and the same chances to fill a new slot, so a Badge piece is like a Forge piece.
- **The Forge's other rules hold.** One single piece that can carry buffs; not in the Transport Cache; not already Eternal. A piece on a ship works at its new tier at once, and it keeps its Marketable label.
- You find it as a second button beside **Tier up** in the Forge, shown when you hold a Badge and the picked piece qualifies.

### Forger's Seal {#forgers-seal}

Skips **up to 30 minutes** of your **Production Queue**: the queue moves up by that much work, the running job ends sooner, and the jobs behind it start earlier. With less than 30 minutes left in the whole queue it skips what there is.

- **The jobs it finishes can't be cancelled afterwards.** A finished job can't be cancelled, only collected. The sheet lists those jobs before you confirm.
- **Upgrades in place** (a Master Drone, a ship converted into a [design](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ship-Designs.md)) whose time the Seal finishes are completed at once.
- **Cancels still refund.** A job still waiting that you cancel gets its whole receipt back, as before; the Seal itself is not returned.
- **Only the Production Queue.** The Seal does not touch the P.E.T. Cradle's jobs, research, the Skylab's upgrades or an expedition.
- **Refused** when the queue has nothing running or waiting.

### Workforce Contract

Hires **one of three Skylab workers for 1 hour**. You pick the worker when you use the contract. **One contract at a time:** a second one is refused while one runs, and it is not spent. A worker with nothing to work on yet (no producer, no Ore Bay, no module built) is refused as well and the contract stays with you.

| Worker | For 1 hour |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Foreman** | Every producer makes twice as much: the Credit Farm, the Thulium Farm, the Velkonite and Orvium Collectors, the Ammo Printer and the Rocket Factory. It counts like the Stone: only while a module is producing, and a full hopper wastes it. The Forgery and the Ore Bay are not producers in this sense |
| **Engineer** | Skylab upgrades run at double speed: every upgrade that is running ends sooner, by 1 hour at most and never by more than half of what it had left; an upgrade you start inside the hour is shortened by at most what is left of the hour, and never by more than half |
| **Hauler** | The Ore Bay has no allowance limit: it moves as much as your cargo and the room in the Resource Storage allow. The allowance it had stored is left untouched afterwards |

The Hauler lifts only the allowance. Everything else about the [Ore Bay](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Skylab.md#ore-bay) stays: it must be built, switched on, not upgrading and powered, and your ship must be landed.

## Rules for all six

- **A count, not an item.** The Hangar's Special items section is the only place that lists them. They have no weight, no hotbar slot and no place in cargo or the Transport Cache; they never drop when your ship is destroyed.
- **Through the wipe.** Your special items and a running Philosopher's Stone or contract are kept, like the Skylab, the research and your looks.
- **Never traded.** Not on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md), not to another pilot, not for Credits.
- **Only the Hammer can move your pace**, and it is limited to one in 60 days. The other five are small by design: 10 hours of two farms, 1 hour of a worker, one tier, 30 minutes of a queue, one research of at most 24 hours.
- **One use, one confirmation, one sound.** Each use plays its own quiet cue.

## Common mistakes

- **The Hammer needs a running upgrade.** Start the upgrade first. A module whose timer has already ended has nothing left to finish.
- **Schematics look at the whole research time.** A technology of 48 or 72 hours can't be finished by them, however little is left.
- **The Stone does not stretch the hopper.** Collect before a farm fills, or the bonus is lost.
- **The Badge is for Tainted and up.** The first step of the Forge needs no Badge.
- **A contract needs work to do.** Build a producer, an Ore Bay or a module first, then hire the worker.
- **Check the sheet.** It names the item and what it uses; using is final.
