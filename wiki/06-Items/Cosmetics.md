# Cosmetics

<!-- wiki-search: look; looks; skin; skins; aura; exhaust; livery; wardrobe; paint; collection; nature white; company supporter; bloody red; bloody r.e.d; holiday skins -->

A **look** is what your ship wears. It has four parts: a **skin** (the paint of the hull), an **aura** (a soft glow round the ship), an **exhaust** (the colour and the shape of the trail behind its engines) and the **livery** of your drones. A look is only for show: it adds no damage, no hull and no speed, takes no slot and changes nothing a shot can hit.

> [!NOTE]
> **Nothing can be bought or earned yet.** For now an admin issues every look. The Hangar marks the ones you do not have as "Can't be obtained yet", and when you are given one a toast tells you and a soft chime plays.

## In one minute

- **Where.** The **Look** section of the Hangar's Spaceship view has three boxes, **Skin**, **Aura** and **Exhaust**, and the Drones view has one for the **Drone livery**. A click on a box opens the **Wardrobe**.
- **Try it on.** In the Wardrobe the ship turns in the look under your pointer. Nothing changes, and nothing is sent, until you click.
- **Whose.** A skin is for one ship model and your account holds it, so it can be worn on every ship you own of that model. An aura or an exhaust can be worn on any ship.
- **Who sees it.** Everybody, unless they have switched looks off in Settings.

## Skins

A skin is a paint scheme made from the ship's own hull: its panel lines, numbers and stripes stay and only the colours change. It is for one **ship model**, and the same skin is worn by every ship of that model you own and by every [design](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ship-Designs.md) made from it; a conversion keeps the look. The Protos has no skin yet, the Kitefin, Ostirion and Nomad have one collection, and the four ships you craft in Assembly have all ten.

Skins belong to your **account**, not to a ship, and they stay through the [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md); the ship you keep wears what it wore.

## Drone liveries

Every collection has a livery for your drones in the same colours, and a Master Drone wears a slightly lighter version of it than a Slave Drone. In the Hangar's Drones view the **Drone livery** box lets you pick **Match my ship**, which takes the livery of your ship's skin, or the livery of any collection you hold a skin of. **Show My Drones** and **Show Enemy Drones** in Settings › Interface still decide which drones you see.

## Aura and exhaust

An **aura** and an **exhaust** each have a **colour** and an **effect**. The colour is one of 24, the same list for both; the effect is the shape and the motion. The Wardrobe has a tab for each.

## The Wardrobe

- **Tabs.** Skin, Aura, Exhaust and Drones. A rarity chip filters a tab to one rarity, and **Owned only** lists what you can wear.
- **A tile says where you stand with the look.** **Worn** (the ship has it on), **Yours** (you hold it), **Comes with the slot** (every pilot has it) or **Can't be obtained yet**, with a dull tick when you click it.
- **Taking a look off.** The **Plain** tile of the Skin tab puts the ship's plain paint back, and **None** takes the aura off.
- **More than one ship.** The switch at the foot of the sheet, **Also apply to my other ships of this model** for a skin and **Also apply to my other ships** for an aura or an exhaust, makes one click wear the look on all of them.
- **Where you can change it.** Where you can change your gear: docked, or in a safe zone with the protection on, as in [The Hangar in Flight](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Hangar.md#when-it-is-open).

## Rarity

Every look has a rarity on the game's own scale, **Shoddy, Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Mythical, Legendary and Eternal** ([Items](/wiki/06-Items/Overview.md)). A tile shows it in the colour of its border, and another pilot's ship tells you the rarity of its look in the Target window and, on hover, what it wears.

## What you see of others

Two rows of Settings › Interface decide, and they are also the way to a calmer screen on a slow machine:

- **Show other pilots' looks**: **All**, **Mine only** or **Off**. All shows everybody's paint, drone colours, auras and exhaust colours. Mine only shows just your own ship and drones with their look, and every other ship plain. Off draws every ship plain, yours too.
- **Show pilot effects**: **All**, **Mine only** or **Off**, for the auras and the exhaust effects alone. An effect is drawn only when both rows allow it, and Off in either hides yours too.

Your **Detail** setting (Settings › Graphics: Low, Medium, High, Ultra) decides how many ships can be drawn with an effect at once, the nearest **4, 8, 12 or 16**, yours first, and how many different skins the game keeps ready. A look beyond that budget shows as the engine's plain glow, or in one flat tint of its skin, until a place frees up. Your own look is always drawn.

## Colour is not identity

Nothing a look changes tells you who a pilot is. The **name tag** (the company's letter in its colour, the clan's tag), the **minimap**, the **Target window**, the lock ring and the rings of the safe zones are never painted by a look, and a Company Supporter always shows its own wearer's company, never another's. Some skins are close to the colours of the swarms, which are a plain brick red for the pirates and a cold violet for the Dormant ships, so a red hull may be a Mars Supporter, a Bloody R.E.D or a pirate: read the name tag, as you always have.

## Sounds

Wearing a skin, an aura, an exhaust and a drone livery each has a quiet sound of its own, a look arriving as a gift has a soft chime, and a look you cannot wear yet answers a click with a dull tick. They follow the Sound Effects volume.
