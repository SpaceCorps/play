# Containers

<!-- wiki-search: container; containers; case; cases; crate; crates; lootbox; loot box; odds; drop rate; drop rates; chances; guarantee; pity; reel; opening; vault; crucible; archive; canister; rack -->

A **container** is a sealed find from Mission Control. When you open one, the server draws one prize from a table, and the chance of every prize in that table is printed on this page. The Containers page of the game shows the same table.

> [!NOTE]
> **Containers are random rewards earned in the game. They are never sold, and the odds are published here.** No container can be bought, traded or given away.

## What a container is

- **Earned.** You earn containers in the game. There is no price and no key: the Shop does not sell them and nothing else does.
- **Yours alone.** A container is bound to your account. You can not trade it, give it to a friend or list it on the [Auction](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Auction.md), and nothing a container gives can be listed there either.
- **The same for every pilot.** Premium changes nothing: it gives no extra openings and no better chances.
- **Kept through the wipe.** A container you have not opened stays on your account when the [wipe](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md) comes. What the wipe does to the prizes is under [The wipe](#the-wipe).
- **Opened at the station.** You open a container docked, on the Containers page. The server draws the prize when you press Open, and the press itself changes nothing. What does count is under [How a prize is chosen](#how-a-prize-is-chosen).

## Grades

Every prize has a **grade**, one of the game's rarity words. The grade is the first thing the draw decides. On the reel it is the frame of the prize's tile, in a colour and a shape, so that colour is never the only sign.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Uncommon | Green | Square |
| Rare | Blue | Diamond |
| Epic | Purple | Hexagon |
| Mythical | Pink | Star |
| Legendary | Gold | Crown |

Each container has its own chance for each grade, set out below. What you own never changes the chance of a grade.

## How a prize is chosen

The server chooses when you open a container, in three steps:

1. **The grade.** One random number is matched against the chances of the grades.
2. **The prize.** A second number is matched against the chances of the prizes of that grade. A ship you already own is not among them (see [The ships](#the-ships)).
3. **The choice among equals.** A prize that says *one of* several gives one of them, each equally likely.

The numbers come from the random generator of the server's operating system, and they are drawn after your container has been taken. Nothing you send can change them: not the button, not the time.

Two things from before do count. The guarantee counter carries over from one opening to the next; the guarantee of each container describes it above. And what you already own, including what you won in earlier openings, changes which prizes are left in the draw: a ship you own is not among them, and a look you own is not drawn while its grade still has one you do not (see [Duplicates and salvage](#duplicates-and-salvage) and [The ships](#the-ships)). What you own never changes the chances of the grades.

- Every chance is a whole number of parts out of 100,000. One part is 0.001%.

**Every opening is recorded** with its seed (the random number the server used), the numbers it drew, the hash of the table it drew from and the prizes your ownership took out of the draw. You can read them under **Details** in **History** on the Containers page.

**You can check one yourself.** The tool `scripts/containers-verify.py` in the game's source reads the table and the details of an opening and says whether the grade, the prize and the choice follow from the seed. The method is short:

```text
stream(seed, id): value i, for i = 0, 1, 2, ...
  = the first 8 bytes (big endian) of HMAC-SHA256(
      key     = seed,
      message = "sc-container-v1|" + id + "|" + i )
  where i is written as 4 bytes, big endian
below(n): take values until one is under 2^64 - (2^64 mod n),
  then use it modulo n (no modulo bias)
grade:    roll = below(total of the grades in play);
  the grade is the first whose running sum is above the roll
prize:    sub = below(total of the prizes left in that grade);
  the prize is the first whose running sum is above sub
choice:   a prize that is "one of N" gives the alternative
  number below(N)
seed = the 32 bytes shown under Details; id = the container's id.
When the guarantee forces an opening, only the guaranteed grade
and better are in play.
```

## Duplicates and salvage

- A **look** you own is not drawn while the grade still has a look you do not own. A collection counts as owned when you hold every skin of it.
- When you own every look of the grade, the prize pays **salvage** instead: Thulium, by grade. It counts as a prize of that grade for the guarantee.
- **Items** are never turned into Thulium: they stack, or arrive as they are.
- **Ships** are never given twice and never paid as salvage (see [The ships](#the-ships)).

## The ships

The Foundry Crucible can hold a ship. The table lists the ships with their **base chances**, the chances for a pilot who owns none of them. Those are the published chances.

A ship you already own can never drop. It is taken out of the draw, and its share goes to the other prizes of the same grade, in proportion to their own chances. So what you own changes your own chances: a little higher on the other prizes of that grade. The chances of the grades never change, and neither does the guarantee. A design made from a ship counts as owning it: a Paragon THUNDER counts as owning the Paragon.

## The wipe

- **Unopened containers are kept.** They stay on your account through the wipe.
- **Items from a container are ordinary items.** At the wipe they are lost unless you carry them in the Transport Cache ([Wipe Timeline](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md)).
- **Looks and ships stay with you**, like any look and any ship. Credits, Thulium and the time left on boosters follow their own rules at the wipe.
- **Nothing from a container can be listed on the Auction.**
