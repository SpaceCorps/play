# Items Overview

Items are the core components that upgrade your ship's capabilities. They can be purchased from the Shop, found as drops, crafted in Assembly (once their technology is researched), or raised and merged in the [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md).

## Rarity Levels

Items come in different rarities, which determine their quality and base stats:

- **Shoddy**: Basic starter equipment.
- **Common**: Standard military grade.
- **Rare**: High-performance gear.
- **Epic**: Advanced technology.
- **Mythical**: Legendary experimental prototypes.
- **Legendary**: Rare and powerful relic modules.
- **Eternal**: Cosmic tier modules with unrivaled power.

## Upgrading Modules

**Every craft in Assembly needs its technology first.** You research it in the Skylab's Research Centre ([Research](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Research.md) has the whole tree, the time of each technology, the fuel and the boost). Until you have it, the recipe's card in Assembly is locked and says which technology to research first. If you already held an item when research came into the game, you already have its technology.

A locked card has an **Open Research** button that opens the Research view of your Skylab with that technology picked. The card still shows the materials and the price, so you can plan, and locked recipes come last in their category. An Extra Slots CPU also waits for the one below it: the card of Extra Slots CPU II says to install Extra Slots CPU I first, and the card of III asks for II.

The top laser amps, tiers II to IV of the shield cells and thrusters, tiers II and III of the engines and the Adaptive Cores, and the Heavy Shield Core are not sold. You make each in Assembly from the piece below it (a Heavy Shield Core from a Basic Shield Core, an Engine III from an Engine II, an Adaptive Core II from an Adaptive Core I, an Impulse Thruster III from an Impulse Thruster II), some Thulium, what the aliens drop and plates (Velkonite plates from your Skylab's Forgery, and 3 Dark Matter Plates for the last tier). The piece must be loose in your inventory: take it off your ship and out of its laser, shield, engine or Adaptive Core first (a shield, an engine or an Adaptive Core must hold no cells or thrusters of its own), and out of the Transport Cache. **The new item keeps the [enchant](/wiki/06-Items/Overview.md#item-enchants) tier of the piece it uses up, and its buffs are rolled again** (a Godly Damage Amp III makes a Godly Damage Amp IV with new buffs, as many as it had). Which copy goes is your choice: the recipe card in Assembly shows your copies when they differ, and asks first before it uses one above Standard. If you choose none, the ones with the lowest enchant tier are used first, so your highest-tier copies stay (among copies of one tier the oldest goes first, whatever their buffs). The whole rule is on the [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md#module-upgrades-in-the-assembly) page.

On average a Goombah drops 4 Cataclysite, 3.25 Ship Fragments, 0.6 Reinforced Hull Plates and 0.25 Power Cores, so the drops of a Damage Amp IV or a Crit Amp IV take about 8 Goombahs; a cell's about 7 (tier II), 10 (III) or 14 (IV), a Heavy Shield Core's about 14; a thruster's about 4 (tier II), 10 (III) or 19 (IV), an Engine II's or an Adaptive Core II's about 4, an Engine III's or an Adaptive Core III's about 19. A Bulwark drops 2 Cataclysite, 2 Ship Fragments and 0.3 Reinforced Hull Plates, but no Power Core. Assembly presses each plate from 5 Dark Matter, a Velkonite and an Orvium Reinforced Plate and 250 Thulium, once you have researched the plate's recipe, and the Dark Matter comes from the black hole: a last-tier piece holds 15 Dark Matter, 7.5 N.I.K.E. rockets on average ([Dark Matter and Dark Matter Plates](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dark-Matter.md#what-the-last-tier-asks-for)). See [Resources](/wiki/06-Items/Resources.md) for where every material comes from.

The **Helios Beam** is an upgrade of the same kind, a laser out of a laser: it uses up a Starfire-III and takes 2,000 Thulium, 50 Cataclysite, 2 Power Cores, 4 Reinforced Hull Plates, 18 Orvium Reinforced Plates and 3 Dark Matter Plates, and keeps the enchant tier of the Starfire-III the same way. So is the **Starfire-III**: it uses up a Quantum Laser III and takes 1,500 Thulium, 100,000 Credits, 15 Ship Fragments, 1 Reinforced Hull Plate and 8 Velkonite Reinforced Plates, and keeps the Quantum Laser III's enchant tier. So is the **Quantum Laser III**: it uses up a Quantum Laser II (the Shop's laser) and takes 1,500 Thulium, 10 Ship Fragments and 2 Velkonite Reinforced Plates, and keeps the Quantum Laser II's enchant tier. All three are on the [Lasers](/wiki/06-Items/Lasers.md) page; the table above lists the amps, the cells and thrusters of tiers II to IV, the Heavy Shield Core and the Engine III.

## The Production Queue

Everything Assembly makes goes through **one Production Queue**, the card at the top of the Crafting tab. Its jobs run **one at a time**: a new job starts when the one before it finishes, also while you are away, and you pay when you queue it. A finished job waits for you: **Collect** hands it out, and **Collect all** (it shows from two finished jobs on) hands out every finished job at once. The queue holds **at most 100 jobs**: the one that runs, the ones that wait and the finished ones you have not collected yet.

**Several copies in one go.** A recipe card that allows it has the chips **×1**, **×5**, **×10** and **Max** above the Assemble button. A batch is **one job**, not several: it runs for the recipe's time times the number of copies, with one timer and one row in the queue (marked ×5, for example), and **one Collect hands out all the copies**. The cost of **all** the copies, the credits, the Thulium and the materials, is taken **up front, all or none**: if you cannot pay for every copy, nothing is taken and nothing is queued. A chip you cannot pay for is greyed out, and **Max** is as many copies as you can pay for right now, up to 25. With several copies the card shows the total cost, the total materials and the total time. A batch of rockets that would take you past the most rockets you can carry is refused whole.

**One at a time.** Ships, Extra Slots CPUs, the upgrade of a piece of equipment (a Damage Amp IV out of a Damage Amp III, a Heavy Shield Core out of a Basic Shield Core) and the Master Drone's upgrade have no chips: each uses up or installs the one thing you pick. You can still queue several of them, one after the other. A conversion of a ship into a [design](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Ship-Designs.md) is one of those jobs too, and it finishes by itself: there is nothing to collect, and **Collect all** leaves it out.

**Each piece rolls for itself.** Equipment rolls its [enchant tier](#item-enchants) when you collect it, and every piece of a batch rolls on its own: ten lasers from one batch are ten separate rolls, as if you had made them one by one. An upgrade keeps the tier of the piece it used up.

## Item Enchants

Equipment carries an **Enchant**: a tier that lets it hold up to a few **buffs**, each a percentage added to one of the item's stats (for example Shield Capacity +6.1%). There are 5 Enchant tiers:

| Tier | Buffs held at most | Size of each buff | Chance on a crafted item |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1. **Standard** | 0 | none | 88.89% |
| 2. **Tainted** | 1 | +2% to +5% | 10% |
| 3. **Godly** | 2 | +4% to +8% | 1% |
| 4. **Rupturing** | 3 | +6% to +11% | 0.1% |
| 5. **Eternal** | 4 | +9% to +15% | 0.01% |

- Shop items are always Standard. Equipment you build in the Assembly rolls a tier when you collect it (the chance column). Above that, the tier goes up in the [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md), one step at a time; the one other way is a module upgrade in the Assembly (a Damage Amp IV, a Crit Amp IV, a shield cell or a thruster of tier II to IV, a Heavy Shield Core, an Engine II or III, an Adaptive Core II or III, a Quantum Laser III, a Starfire-III, the Helios Beam), which keeps the tier of the piece it is made from.
- An item never holds more buffs than it has stats: a shield core has four, a laser three (the Quantum Laser I and II two, since they have no critical chance of their own), an engine, a Momentum Thruster or an Adaptive Core two, an Impulse Thruster, a Crit Amp I, a Repair Drone or a Hull Plating one (an Impulse Thruster has its flat speed to buff: the Forge rolls no buff on its multiplier of x1.017 to x1.03, which would be worth next to nothing), the higher crit amps two, the damage amps and shield cells three. The limit of the tier is the smaller of the two numbers. A Penetration Amp has one stat, so it holds one buff.
- The [Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md#buffs-by-tier) fills a slot after an item's first buff by chance: a tier-up always gives the first buff, and every other slot the new tier opens is filled with a 50% chance, so a tier holds that many buffs at most (a Godly item has two half the time).
- A **Range** buff never passes +5%, at any tier.
- Buffs stay small next to the next item up: a maxed Quantum Laser I still does less damage than a plain Quantum Laser II.

Enchanted stats are displayed on the item icon grid card (e.g. `[T]` for Tainted, `[G]` for Godly) and outlined in the item's statistics tooltip.

## The Forge

The **Forge** (the second tab of the Assembly page) raises a piece of equipment one tier at a time for credits and alien drops, and merges two copies of an item into one. A tier can't be skipped: Standard, Tainted, Godly, Rupturing, Eternal, in that order. Every step costs more than the one before, and can fail. The steps, the materials and the rules of a merge are in [The Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md).

## Currencies

Every material and both currencies, with where each comes from and what it is for, are on the [Resources](/wiki/06-Items/Resources.md) page.

- **Credits**: Standard currency used for basic items. Earned by killing aliens and completing [quests](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Quests.md); each level's Special mission also pays items (lasers, shield parts, Power Cores, Ship Fragments).
- **Thulium**: Rare currency used for high-end equipment. Every alien pays some when it dies (more from the tougher ones), and missions and the Skylab's Thulium Farm give more. See [Thulium](/wiki/06-Items/Resources.md#thulium).

## Categories

The Shop, the Hangar's inventory and the other item lists read in one order: the ship, its Hull Plating, then a laser with its amps and ammo, a shield with its cells, an engine with its thrusters, the Adaptive Cores, extras, drones, drone formations, boosters and resources. Inside a kind the cheapest comes first.

- **Hull Plating**: Armour for the hull plate slots of the four ships you craft, Hull Plating I to III, each adding more hull. See [Hull Plating](/wiki/06-Items/Hull-Plating.md).
- **Lasers**: Your primary weapon systems, and the [amps](/wiki/06-Items/Lasers.md) that go into them.
- **Shields**: Generators and [cells](/wiki/06-Items/Shields.md) for defense.
- **Propulsion**: Engines and [thrusters](/wiki/06-Items/Propulsion.md) for speed.
- **Repair Drones**: Extras that repair your hull, each faster than the last: Repair Drone I, II and III cost 5,000, 15,000 and 35,000 credits, Repair Drone IV 2,000 Thulium. The rates are in [Combat](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Combat.md).
- **Cloaking CPUs and the EMP Charge**: Extras for a fight or a flight: a Cloaking CPU hides your ship until you end it (S, M and L: 10, 25 and 50 uses, 5,000, 11,250 and 20,000 Thulium), an EMP Charge makes you untargetable for 3 seconds, breaks every lock on you and ends every cloak nearby (500 Thulium). See [Extras](/wiki/06-Items/Extras.md).
- **Research CPUs**: the Extra Slots CPUs add extra slots to every ship, the Jump CPU takes you to a company sector for Thulium, the Base CPUs take you home and the Auto-Repair CPU sends your Repair Drone out by itself. They are not sold: research them, then make them in Assembly. See [Extras](/wiki/06-Items/Extras.md#research-cpus).
- **Drone formations**: sixteen items for your drones that give bonuses and charge prices, such as a bigger shield for weaker guns. They are not sold: research them, then make them in Assembly with Thulium. See [Drone Formations](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Formations.md).
- **Resources**: what aliens drop and the Skylab makes for crafting: Ship Fragments, the four crystals, Power Cores, the Velkonite and Orvium plates, and two that come from elsewhere: **Dark Matter**, which the [black hole](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Black-Hole.md) gives back for a N.I.K.E. rocket, and the **Dark Matter Plate** that Assembly presses from it for the last tier of every upgrade chain and the top two steps of [The Forge](/wiki/06-Items/Forge.md).
