# Danger Sectors

<!-- wiki-search: ds; ds-1; ds-2; ds-3; ds-4; central pvp zone; pvp zone; pulsar; giant excavator; excavator; dormant swamp; swamp; event 2; tech surge -->

The **Danger Sectors** are the four sectors in the middle of the galaxy, `DS-1` to `DS-4`. The three companies meet there, and pilots may fight each other there in every world ([Spacemap Travel](/wiki/01-General/Spacemap%20Travel.md)). Each of `DS-1`, `DS-2` and `DS-3` has one company's gate; `DS-4` is the core, with the [black hole](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Black-Hole.md) in the middle. There is no station in any of them: the only safe places are the rings around the jump gates.

A civilisation of the old galaxy lived here, advanced and purple-black, and for a reason nobody knows it collapsed. Its remains were never entirely dead: the [Dormant Swarm](/wiki/05-Swarms/Dormant-Swarm.md) was the first sign. From **season day 11**, the start of event 2 (**Tech Surge**, see the [Wipe Timeline](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Wipe-Timeline.md#30-day-season-schedule)), more of it wakes, and the Danger Sectors change. Every pilot of the world is told on the day it begins, and what is new stays until the wipe.

## What is new from day 11

- **Pulsars and giant excavators.** `DS-1`, `DS-2` and `DS-3` each get a pulsar with a **giant excavator** beside it. Put Dark Matter in the excavator's tank, choose a resource, and it mines the pulsar: Thulium and rare ores land around it in boxes that anyone may take. It is the richest thing to fight over in the Danger Sectors, and the most dangerous. See [Giant Excavator](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Giant-Excavator.md).
- **Slumbering Voids.** While an excavator mines, **Slumbering Voids** fly in from the edge of the map in waves and hunt the pilots near it. Others patrol the swamp. See [Dormant Swamp](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dormant-Swamp.md#slumbering-void).
- **The Dormant Swamp.** In a corner of `DS-4` stands the base of the lost civilisation: guns that fire on every ship they see, Inert Masses that guard it, and in the middle the Unwakened. It is a place pilots are not meant to visit yet. See [Dormant Swamp](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dormant-Swamp.md).
- **A faster, richer Dormant Swarm.** The swarm now appears at the swamp, comes back sooner after it is destroyed and pays twice as much. See [Dormant Swarm](/wiki/05-Swarms/Dormant-Swarm.md).
- **Before day 11** none of this exists: the Danger Sectors are as [Spacemap Travel](/wiki/01-General/Spacemap%20Travel.md) describes them. A world on day 11 or later has it all at once.

## Where everything is

Every world has its own copy of all of it, and a pulsar and its excavator stand in the open third of their sector, far from every gate ring, on the side that faces the middle of the map. Distances are in map units; the sectors are 32,000 by 18,000 units.

## Keeping out of trouble

- **Radiation.** An excavator that has overheated or been destroyed, and its pulsar, burn every ship that stays inside their circles ([Giant Excavator](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Giant-Excavator.md#heat-and-radiation)). The game warns you before it happens, and the Star System map and the minimap draw the circles.
- **The swamp's guns.** A ship the swamp's turrets can see is shot at long before it can see anything worth the trip ([Dormant Swamp](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Dormant-Swamp.md#the-guns)). A course you click is bent round the guns and the radiation, as it is round the black hole, and a toast warns you when the place you click lies inside.
- **If you are destroyed there,** the choice to come back **on the spot** puts you at the nearest point outside the radiation and outside the swamp's zone, as it does for the black hole ([Getting Started](/wiki/01-General/Getting-Started.md#dying-and-coming-back)).
- **A group, and an escape.** The excavators draw Voids and rivals alike. Bring a [group](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Groups.md), know which gate is nearest, and remember that in the Danger Sectors you cannot jump out while you are being attacked ([Jumping Under Fire](/wiki/01-General/Spacemap%20Travel.md#jumping-under-fire)).
- **The rest is PvP as ever.** Nothing here is a safe place: the normal rules of your world apply, rivals included.
