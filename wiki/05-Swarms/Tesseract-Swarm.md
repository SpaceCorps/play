# Tesseract Swarm

The Tesseract Swarm is one huge mothership, the **Tesseract**, and the **Facets** it releases while it is being hit. The Tesseract lives at a fixed home in the [Danger Sector](/wiki/01-General/Danger-Sectors.md) `DS-4`, it has no weapon of its own, and it never leaves its sector. It is made for a very large group of the strongest pilots: its hull and shield are far beyond a Crystalys, and it mends itself as soon as it is left alone. The Danger Sectors are PvP sectors, so the fight can draw rivals as well as the Facets.

## The members

- **Tesseract**: a cube-shaped mothership many times the size of a Crystalys, of dark forged iron with glowing ember seams and with its name over it. A bay in its front wall opens while it is being hit, and a glowing cube turns in a cage on its roof. It is passive: it drifts slowly inside its home area while nobody hurts it, and once a pilot hits it, it stands still and holds its place. It fires nothing and it never chases anyone; its defence is its size, its mending and the Facets it releases.
- **Facet**: a small, fast ship of the same dark iron, with glowing edges, a lit lens in its nose and two floating wing plates. It exists only while the Tesseract is being hit. It chases nobody: it keeps to the Tesseract, flies short legs of its own to random places all round it, and fires on the nearest pilot who has hit the Tesseract whenever one is within its range. It leaves when the Tesseract is left alone. It is faster than any ship, so running away from the swarm does not work, but a pilot who keeps far from the Tesseract is never shot at.

## How the fight goes

- **Bring a very large group, and hit together.** The Tesseract's hull and shield are a pool of millions of points, so the fight takes a long time even for a large group of strong ships, and the pay is split by damage: every pilot who is there counts.
- **Never leave it alone.** Once nobody has hurt it for the time in the *At a glance* list, it mends its hull and then its shield at that speed, so a pause of a few seconds can undo a long minute of work. A Tesseract that has mended completely forgets who hurt it: the pay and the box go by the damage of the fight that kills it.
- **Every hit calls the Facets.** While the Tesseract is being hit, a batch of Facets arrives close to it every few seconds, up to the cap in the *At a glance* list. While nobody hits it, none come, and the ones that are already there wait and leave.
- **The Facets fly all round the Tesseract and fire on whoever is within range.** They do not go after the top dealer or any one pilot: each Facet flies its own short legs to random places of a wide ring round the Tesseract, in every direction, and whenever a pilot who has hit the Tesseract is within its range it fires on the nearest such pilot. A group that stays close together is within range of fewer Facets at a time, and shares their fire, unlike a group spread all round the Tesseract. A Facet has little hull and shield, and shooting one down pays nothing, but it takes a volley off the pilots near it. A pilot who has not hit the Tesseract is not their target.
- **A pilot who keeps far enough away is left alone.** The Facets never go farther from the Tesseract than the distance in the *At a glance* list, so a pilot who stays beyond that distance and the range of a Facet's laser is not shot at, however much damage he dealt; a Facet with nobody to fire at keeps flying round the Tesseract and leaves when the Tesseract is left alone.
- **Beta and Gamma are harder.** The swarms of those worlds are stronger ([Worlds](/wiki/05-Swarms/Swarms.md#the-worlds)), and so are the Tesseract's mending and every Facet's volley.
- **The Danger Sectors are PvP.** A rival can attack the group in the middle of the fight and wait for the box: it is the top dealer's (and its clan's) for 30 seconds, and then anyone's ([Cargo](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md)). Bring company.
- **The Tesseract comes back** after the time in the *At a glance* list, at full strength, at its home.

## Rewards and drops

The Tesseract pays for the fight it is: as much as many Crystalys together, and more for the damage it takes than a Crystalys, the best-paid alien. The pay is split by damage among the pilots who fought it ([how a boss kill pays](/wiki/05-Swarms/Swarms.md#how-a-boss-kill-pays)). Its box is for the pilot who dealt the most damage and holds Power Cores and Ancient Control Units, and now and then Dark Matter. The hull plates are not in that box: every pilot the kill pays gets a [private box](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Cargo.md#private-boxes) of plates of his own, that only he can see and take, for 10 minutes. It is rolled for his share of the damage, as a [Clan Warden](/wiki/03-Mechanics/Clans.md#warden-pay-and-loot)'s loot is: a pilot who dealt a fifth of the damage gets about a fifth of the amounts in the table, and in a group each pilot gets his part of the group's share, as the pay is shared. The Facets pay nothing, drop nothing and add no PvE points: killing them is no way to farm.
