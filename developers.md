## Integration Options
1. **MCP Server**: Connect your AI agent via Streamable HTTP at `https://spacecorps.github.io/server.json` or discovery card at `/.well-known/mcp/server-card.json`.
2. **REST API**: Call `https://spaceemit-api.sliplane.app/api/v1` for real-time telemetry, sessions, and game state.
3. **Agent Skills**: Load skills from `/.well-known/agent-skills/index.json`.

## Free Tier & Sandbox
SpaceCorps offers an unrestricted public community tier and local sandboxing via the open-source CLI suite on GitHub.
