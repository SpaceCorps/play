Premium for a SpaceCorps pilot account. Premium is the main way to support the developers of SpaceCorps. Premium features change from time to time, and Premium never offers any competitive advantage. Premium currently gives no gameplay advantage and no extra features. Features may be added over time; what Premium gives is always shown in the game, on the server you are connected to. You also get Premium for free by playing: Premium days are awarded at the end of each wipe (season) you play, depending on how far you got.

There are two ways to buy it:

  • Premium, 30 days: a one-time purchase for US$14.99. It gives 30 days. You can buy it again; each purchase adds 30 days on top of the days you have left.
  • Premium subscription: US$9.99 per month. Each monthly payment gives 30 days, until you cancel.

Premium belongs to the account you were signed in to when you paid. It cannot be moved to another account.

Premium starts as soon as Stripe confirms your payment, usually within a minute. If Premium does not appear, reload the Premium page; if it still does not, tell us.

5. The subscription: renewal and cancellation

A subscription renews every month until you cancel it under Manage subscription; cancelling stops future charges.

  • You are charged US$9.99 each month until you cancel, and each payment adds 30 days of Premium.
  • On the Premium page, Manage subscription opens your Stripe billing page, where you can cancel, change your card and see your invoices. You keep the Premium you have already paid for until it runs out.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe may try again for a while. While a payment has not gone through, no days are added.
  • If you delete your game account, a running subscription is cancelled.
  • A one-time purchase does not renew: it simply ends after its days.
