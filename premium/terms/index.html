2. What is sold

We sell Premium for a SpaceCorps pilot account. Premium doubles the experience the pilot earns from kills and quests while it runs. It is the only thing sold: it gives no ships, items, credits, Thulium or other advantage. It is digital content and a digital service; nothing is delivered in a box.

There are two ways to buy it:

  • Premium, 30 days: a one-time purchase for US$14.99. It gives 30 days. You can buy it again; each purchase adds 30 days on top of the days you have left.
  • Premium subscription: US$9.99 per month. Each monthly payment gives 30 days, until you cancel.

Premium belongs to the account you were signed in to when you paid. It cannot be moved to another account.

3. Price and payment

Prices are in US dollars. Payment is taken by Stripe, and Stripe shows you the total before you pay. We never see or keep your card number. See the privacy policy for what we and Stripe keep.

4. When Premium starts

Premium starts as soon as Stripe confirms your payment, usually within a minute. You ask for this when you tick the box before paying. If Premium does not appear, reload the Premium page; if it still does not, tell us and we will sort it out.

5. The subscription: renewal and cancellation

  • The subscription renews every month. You are charged US$9.99 on the same day each month until you cancel, and each payment adds 30 days of Premium.
  • You can cancel at any time on the Premium page under Manage subscription, which opens your Stripe billing page. Cancelling stops all further charges. You keep the Premium you have already paid for until it runs out.
  • If a payment fails, Stripe may try again for a while. While a payment has not gone through, no days are added.
  • We will not change the price of a running subscription without telling you first, and you can cancel before the new price applies.
  • A one-time purchase does not renew and cannot be cancelled afterwards: it simply ends after its days.

6. Right of withdrawal

If you live in the EU or EEA, you normally have 14 days to withdraw from a purchase made at a distance. This does not apply to digital content and digital services that start right away when you have clearly asked for that and have accepted that the right of withdrawal ends once they have started.

Premium starts as soon as your payment is confirmed. Before you pay, you ask us to start Premium now and you accept that your 14-day right of withdrawal ends once it has started. That is what the box on the Premium page says, and why the pay button stays off until you tick it. The same applies to each monthly payment of a subscription, which starts its 30 days when it is paid. This does not affect any rights you have by law that cannot be waived.

7. If something goes wrong

If you paid and did not get Premium, or you were charged twice, or you were charged after you cancelled, tell us. We will fix it or refund the payment.

8. Changes to these terms

We may change these terms. The terms on this page on the day you pay are the ones that apply to that payment, and to the monthly payments that follow it until you cancel or we tell you of a change.

9. Law
