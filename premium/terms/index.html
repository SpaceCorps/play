2. What is sold

We sell Premium for a SpaceCorps pilot account. Premium is the main way to support the developers of SpaceCorps. Premium features change from time to time, and Premium never offers any competitive advantage. Premium currently gives no gameplay advantage and no extra features. Features may be added over time; what Premium gives is always shown in the game, on the server you are connected to. Nothing else is sold. It is digital content and a digital service; nothing is delivered in a box.

You also get Premium for free by playing: Premium days are awarded at the end of each wipe (season) you play, depending on how far you got.

There are two ways to buy it:

  • Premium, 30 days: a one-time purchase for US$14.99. It gives 30 days. You can buy it again; each purchase adds 30 days on top of the days you have left.
  • Premium subscription: US$9.99 per month. Each monthly payment gives 30 days, until you cancel.

Premium belongs to the account you were signed in to when you paid. It cannot be moved to another account.

By buying you ask us to start Premium now and accept that your 14-day right of withdrawal ends once it has started.

Before you pay you tick a box that says you agree to these terms and ask for Premium to start now; the pay button stays off until you do. The law of the country where you live may give you rights that cannot be taken away by these words; where it does, those rights stand.
