What Premium is

  • Double experience. Everything your pilot earns from kills and quests counts twice, while Premium runs.
  • Nothing else. No ships, no items, no credits, no Thulium and no other advantage are sold. Premium is a thank-you for supporting the game.
  • It ends by itself. A one-time purchase adds 30 days. A subscription adds 30 days each month it is paid, and stops when you cancel.
